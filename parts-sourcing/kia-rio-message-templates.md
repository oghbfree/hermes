# Kia Rio Dealer Outreach Templates

## Dashboard Dealers — Message Template

### Initial Contact (WhatsApp)
> Good afternoon. Please do you have an LHD dashboard for a Kia Rio? 2017 model. I'm based in the UK and will be in Ghana in about a month. Please let me know if you have it in stock and the price. Thank you.

### Follow-up (if they respond positively)
> That's great. Can you send me a photo of the dashboard? I want to check the condition. Also, do you deliver within Accra or would I need to pick up?

### Price Negotiation
> I've spoken to a few dealers. What's your best price? I'm ready to buy when I arrive in Ghana next month.

---

## Steering Rack Dealers — Message Template

### Initial Contact (WhatsApp)
> Good afternoon. I need a steering rack for a Kia Rio 2017 — manual. Also, do you do RHD to LHD conversion for the same car? I'm coming to Ghana in about a month and looking for someone reliable. Please let me know.

### Follow-up (if they do conversions)
> Excellent. Can you give me an idea of the total cost for the rack plus the conversion? And how long does the conversion take?

### Quality Check
> Do you have photos of conversions you've done before? Just want to see the quality of work. Thank you.
