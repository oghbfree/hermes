Vertical AI Solutions for Elder Care in Ghana

MARKET CONTEXT
- Ghana's population over 60 is growing rapidly
- Caregiver shortage is acute (global trend: 2.1B seniors by 2050)
- Aging in place model fits Ghana's family structure
- Mobile money infrastructure enables remote payments

RECOMMENDED SOLUTIONS

1. FALL PREVENTION (Highest Priority)
- Low-cost wearables with SMS alerts
- Predictive analytics using movement patterns
- Partner with local telecoms for SMS alerts

2. VITAL MONITORING (High Priority)  
- Bluetooth blood pressure monitors with smartphone app
- AI symptom checker via chatbot
- Medication reminders in local languages

3. COMPANIONSHIP (Medium Priority)
- Voice companions in Twi/Ga languages
- Cognitive games via WhatsApp
- Family connection tools

TECH STACK FOR GHANA
- Hardware: Wearable sensors, BP monitors, motion sensors
- Software: WhatsApp Business API, cloud dashboard
- AI: Google Cloud Speech (Twi dialects), pre-trained health models

BUSINESS MODEL OPTIONS
A: Home Care Service + Technology (monthly subscription)
B: Tech-Only Platform (sell hardware + subscription)
C: Hybrid Model (start platform, build care team)

KEY CONSIDERATIONS
- Power: Solar/battery backup required
- Connectivity: SMS fallback for poor internet
- Language: Support Twi, Ga, Ewe
- Payment: Integrate MTN Mobile Money

RECOMMENDED APPROACH
Phase 1 (1-3 months): WhatsApp monitoring, 10-20 pilot clients
Phase 2 (4-6 months): Add AI triage, 50 clients  
Phase 3 (7-12 months): Full vertical AI, 100+ clients

INVESTMENT REQUIRED
- Phase 1: -15k
- Phase 2: -30k
- Phase 3: -75k
- Total to profitability: ~
