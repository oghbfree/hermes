# AKOMA ROBOTICS — SCHOOL-INTEGRATED PROGRAM PROPOSAL

## KISSi Education, Dome

---

## THE MODEL

Replace the standalone after-school robotics club with a **whole-class program integrated into school fees**. Every student in the enrolled class/year group participates — no opt-in, no separate payment from parents.

**How it works:**

1. KISSi Education adds **GH¢100/student/term** to existing school fees
2. Akoma provides: certified facilitators, mBot robotics kits, 12-week curriculum, AI Awareness module, final showcase + certificates
3. School provides: classroom space, computer access if available
4. Fee is collected through the school's existing billing — zero admin for parents, zero collection risk for Akoma

With a school of KISSi's size (~250 students), the programme is delivered **year-group by year-group**, so every child in the school can participate across the term.

---

## EQUIPMENT LOGISTICS — TWO OPTIONS FOR THE SCHOOL

Because school size and layout differ, KISSi may choose the delivery model that fits best:

**Option A — On-Site STEM Corner (recommended for KISSi's scale)**
- Akoma provides a lockable equipment cabinet stored on school premises
- Kits are set up in minutes — maximum hands-on time every session
- School names a custodian (e.g., a STEM teacher or deputy head); Akoma keeps an equipment register
- Written liability terms: school responsible for negligence/theft, Akoma for equipment defects
- **Best value at KISSi's size:** enables extra practice sessions and a permanent STEM presence on campus

**Option B — Fully Managed Mobile Delivery**
- Akoma transports all equipment to and from the school for each session
- Zero storage responsibility for the school
- Slightly less hands-on time per session due to setup/pack-down

Under either option, Akoma handles equipment, staffing, and curriculum — zero lift from school teachers.

## WHAT'S INCLUDED (12-WEEK TERM)

- ✅ Akoma-certified facilitator(s), on-site weekly (1-hour sessions per class)
- ✅ mBot robotics kits — one per pair of students (provided by Akoma)
- ✅ 12-week structured curriculum:
  - Weeks 1–4: Robot assembly, basic coding (mBlock), sensors, obstacle avoidance
  - Weeks 5–8: Line following, remote control, sensor fusion, team project kickoff
  - Weeks 9–10: AI Awareness module — "What is AI?", smart sensors, machine learning concepts
  - Weeks 11–12: Build & test final projects, showcase day with certificates
- ✅ Final Showcase Day for parents and staff
- ✅ Participation certificates for all students
