# AKOMA ROBOTICS — SCHOOL-INTEGRATED PROGRAM PROPOSAL
## Royal Zion International School

---

## THE MODEL

Replace the standalone after-school robotics club with a **whole-class program integrated into school fees**. Every student in the enrolled class/year group participates — no opt-in, no separate payment from parents.

**How it works:**

1. Royal Zion International School adds **GH¢100/student/term** to existing school fees
2. Akoma provides: certified facilitator, mBot robotics kits, 12-week curriculum, AI Awareness module, final showcase + certificates
3. School provides: classroom space, computer access if available
4. Fee is collected through the school's existing billing — zero admin for parents, zero collection risk for Akoma

---

## EQUIPMENT LOGISTICS

- Akoma does **not** store equipment on-site between sessions
- All equipment is transported to and from school for each weekly session
- As uptake is assessed, we will evaluate consolidating into a **single whole-school day** (all classes held in one day)

---

## WHAT'S INCLUDED (12-WEEK TERM)

- ✅ One Akoma-certified facilitator, on-site weekly (1-hour sessions)
- ✅ mBot robotics kit per pair of students (class set provided and transported by Akoma)
- ✅ 12-week structured curriculum:
  - Weeks 1–4: Robot assembly, basic coding (mBlock), sensors, obstacle avoidance
  - Weeks 5–8: Line following, remote control, sensor fusion, team project kickoff
  - Weeks 9–10: AI Awareness module — "What is AI?", smart sensors, machine learning concepts
  - Weeks 11–12: Build & test final projects, showcase day with certificates
- ✅ Final Showcase Day for parents and staff
- ✅ Participation certificates for all students

## WHY THIS WORKS FOR ROYAL ZION INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

- **Previous proposal was GH¢800/student** (after-school, parent-pays-directly) — too expensive for most families
- **This is GH¢100/student/term** rolled into fees — parents barely notice it
- **No separate enrollment** — every student in the class participates, no marketing needed each term
- Akoma handles equipment, staffing, and curriculum — zero lift from school teachers
- Showcase Day gives the school a visible parent-facing event every term
- Positions Royal Zion International School as a STEM-forward institution at minimal cost
