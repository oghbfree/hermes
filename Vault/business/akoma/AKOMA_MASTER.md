## 🏢 Business Overview

- **What:** Hands-on STEM education — mBot robotics + mBlock/Scratch coding for children
- **Core age range:** 8–14 (autism track: 6–16, grouped by ability)
- **Locations:** Accra, Ghana & London, UK
- **Website:** https://akomarobotics.com
- **Founder:** Mr. Herbert Blankson
- **Model:** Turnkey school partnerships (we bring equipment + certified facilitators; school hosts after-school/whole-class program)
- **Tagline:** "Build. Code. Innovate. Your Child's Journey into Robotics and Coding Starts Here."
- **Strategy pivot (Mar 2026):** 40 schools contacted, low momentum; moved from ₵800–1,000/student to **whole-class, fee-rolled-in pricing** (GH¢100/term) to guarantee headcount.
