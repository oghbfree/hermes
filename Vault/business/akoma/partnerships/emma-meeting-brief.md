| Term | Position |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Akoma Lead Facilitator (independent contractor), trains 1 named apprentice |
| **Split** | Emma 60% / Akoma 40% of collected fees — OR GH¢10–15/student/session kit+brand fee, **whichever is higher** |

| **Term** | Start with one term (12 weeks), one school or two. Review → renew / upgrade to formal partnership / end. |

- No deposit/equipment liability acceptance → no kits leave
- Wants kits "to run his own thing" unbranded → that's a rental customer, not a partner: GH¢50/kit/week + GH¢1,000 deposit
