## Core definition
> Marketing is NOT hustle, interruption, or hype. It is **telling a true story that helps people reach their desired outcome so effectively they tell others about it.**

## The non-negotiable principles (map to every 2Real/Akoma post)

1. **The "Cheap Pizza" trap — AVOID.** Competing on price alone = race to the bottom (lower quality/margins for customers who don't care). Be the *best pizza in town* — remarkable enough that customers pay a premium and tell others.
   → **2Real application:** don't lead every post with "cheapest price." Lead with the VALUE/outcome the item enables (the job it does right). Price is supporting evidence, not the headline. (Balance against the existing deal-card posts — a deal post can exist, but its HOOK is the outcome, not "low price.")
2. **Empathy over technical feat.** Solve a human problem; don't ask for admiration of how you sourced/built it. (2Real: the customer's win, not "UK container arrived.")
3. **Consistency over authenticity.** Customers want a professional who makes a promise and keeps it — every time. (Both brands: same CTA, same reliable delivery, never a bad-day post.)
4. **The 3 core desires — every offer serves one.** Everything humans want reduces to: **(1) Freedom from fear, (2) Status, (3) Affiliation (belonging).** Each post should name which desire it serves:
   - Freedom from fear: tools that don't fail on the job; tested-working guarantee; honest condition.
   - Status: pro-grade gear; the builder/student who looks capable; women-in-trades confidence.
   - Affiliation: "people like us do things like this" — the trades community; the maker/kid community; being part of a shop people trust.
5. **Smallest Viable Audience (SVA).** Stop appealing to everyone. Target ONE specific group per post (as we already wired into the human-story pillar). 10–1000 true fans beat mass reach. Speak only to that segment's words/pain/win.

## AI framing (this team already runs on it)
- **"Will you work for an AI, or will AI work for you?"** → This stack is set up so AI works FOR H (deepseek hands + GLM brain + bots). Keep it that way — never let the automation quotas dictate the human craft.
- AI handles the routine/volume; the HUMAN empathy + strategy is the differentiator. Do NOT let AI-generated copy sound like AI (that's the SlopMonster gate).

## Brand-voice applications
- **2Real (real tools, real work, real Ghana):** the story = honest tools that let a Ghanaian tradesperson do the job right and be proud of the result (Status + Affiliation + Freedom-from-fear). Consistency: reliable delivery + tested-working + CTA 0204252252 on every post.
- **Akoma (kid robotics):** the story = a child's curiosity, built by hand, that works (Affiliation + Status for the kid/parent). Empathy over technical feat — show the joy/outcome, not the spec sheet. Consistent: ages 7-14, enrolment CTA.

## The 5-step marketing process (check every campaign against it)
1. Invent a thing worth making, with a story worth telling.
2. Design it for a SMALL group who will particularly benefit.
3. Tell a story matching that group's built-in narrative: "People like us do things like this."
4. Spread the word with built-in viral mechanics (testimonial ask, shareable win, tag-a-builder).
5. Show up regularly, consistently, generously — for years.

## Standing output rules for content (merged into engine)
- Every post names its ONE target segment (SVA) + which of the 3 desires it serves.
- Hook leads with OUTCOME/VALUE/story, never pure price.
- Consistency rails enforced every run (CTA 0204252252, honest condition, reliable delivery).
- No hype/interruption voice; no fake-lifestyle claims; real photos only for people.
