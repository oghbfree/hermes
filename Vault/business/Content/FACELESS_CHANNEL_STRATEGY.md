## 1. The Core Decision

**Not "faceless OR Taiwah" — a hybrid.** A pure faceless channel exists to cut a weak on-camera face, make content cheap, and avoid face-lock-in. An AI persona (Taiwah) already removes all three pain points *while keeping a face brands and audiences attach to.*

**Winning structure:** Faceless content mechanics (b-roll + voiceover + repeatable formats) + Taiwah as recurring presenter + real 2Real inventory as the content fuel. Near-zero marginal cost — reuses the Taiwah persona, live stock, and the existing content pipeline.

**Tagline:** "We build ours" — indigenous engineering progress stories.

### Final pick
**Niche 2 — "The Business & Craft of Building," as a Taiwah-led hybrid faceless channel.** Highest score across all axes simultaneously, real whitespace, reuses existing assets, steep monetization ladder (AdSense → affiliate → course → brand deals → 2Real funnel).

## 3. Parameters — is a niche too narrow? (check BEFORE committing)

Fails ≥2 → widen the niche.

1. **Demand:** Google Trends + YouTube ≥ ~30–40k global searches/mo across the keyword cluster.
2. **Ceiling:** top 10 channels in the niche combined > ~500k subs? (If best channel <100k → too small.)
3. **Angle-count:** can you list 30 distinct episode/title angles in a month? (Wall at 10 → too narrow.)
4. **Resonance:** like:dislike ≥ 4:1 on first 20 uploads.
5. **RPM viability:** blended RPM × realistic views ≥ ~$1,000/mo by ~month 9.
6. **Diversification:** must spawn ≥2 off-YouTube monetizers naturally (we have 3+).
7. **Pivot-space:** a *cluster*, not a single keyword (trades survives algorithm shifts; a single keyword doesn't).

Niche 2 passes all 7.

## 4. Pathway Calendar (12 months)

### Month 0 — build
- Anchor: **YouTube (long-form/search) + TikTok/Shorts/Reels (discovery) + IG (audience)** — one funnel.
- Channel: "Taiwah Builds" — tagline *"The business + craft of building, told by a real Ghanaian builder."*
- Batch 6 scripts, each mixing (a) craft (b) business — never just the other.
- Confirm zero content cost beyond time (real inventory + Taiwa persona).

### M0–M2 — 90-day test
| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1–2 | Publish 2× long-form + 6× Shorts/wk. Format: "1 trade skill + 1 business lesson + 1 Taiwa moment." |
| 3–4 | Measure distribution indicator (see KPIs); lean into winners, cut losers. |
| 5–8 | Double down; reach 1,000 subs + 4k watch-hrs (via Shorts funnel). Monetize on YPP. |
| 8–12 | Affiliate links live + first 2Real inventory promo — channel pays *and* feeds the business. |

### M4–8 — build the ladder
- Course **"Pricing & Getting Paid as a Contractor"** (from real 2K ops), $49–99. Highest margin; teaches the whitespace.
- Sponsorship pitches to tool suppliers (already stock them — legitimate ad partner).
- Contractor community/membership (networking + templates).

### M9–12 — scale & systemize
- 2× long-form + 4 Shorts/wk reliable cadence (delegate to existing content engine).
- Spin off **Niche 4 (Akoma robotics)** as a 2nd channel once Niche 2 crosses ~50k subs — same face + funnel.

## 5. KPIs

- YPP by M4 → $250/mo by M6 → $1k+/mo by M9–10 → $2k+/mo by M12.
- Revenue mix target: ~50% course + sponsorships, NOT AdSense alone.
- Watch: **distribution indicator** — a single video getting >10× its channel-average views in week 1–2 signals a format to double; if nothing does by M3, the format needs rewriting, not patience.
