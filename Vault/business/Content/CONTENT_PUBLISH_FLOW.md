# CONTENT PUBLISH FLOW — Taiwah reels

_Written 16/11/26. Answers: batch-produce 4 reels then publish all at once on TikTok + Instagram +
Facebook, with WhatsApp Status daily — what should the flow actually be?_

---

## The short answer

**Keep batching the production. Stop batching the publish.**

Publishing 4 at once to all three platforms burns the whole batch in one day and teaches you nothing.
Drip them: **one reel per day per platform, Tue–Sat.** Same amount of content, five times the reach
window, and you get feedback before the last of the batch is spent.

---

## The flow

### 1. Produce in batch (keep doing this)
Reels are cheaper to make in a run — same session, same voice, same QC. No change here.

### 2. Export ONE clean master, no platform watermarks
Every reel exports once, clean, 1080×1920. **Never** upload a file carrying TikTok's watermark to
Instagram or Facebook — TikTok down-ranks content with a competitor watermark, and Instagram Reels
suppresses it too. One clean master, uploaded separately to each platform.

### 3. Publish one per day, in this order

| Slot | Platform | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1, morning** | **TikTok** | Fastest, cheapest read on whether the hook lands. Publishing here first tells you in ~2 hours whether the reel works. |
| **Day 1, +2–4 hours** | **Instagram Reels** | Same day, staggered. You have already seen TikTok's first-hour reaction and can adjust the caption. |
| **Day 2, morning** | **Facebook Reels** | FB is your *buyer* audience for 2Real, not your trend audience. It benefits from the reel already being proven, and it deserves its own day so you can reply to comments. |
| **Every day** | **WhatsApp Status** | 1 per day, continuing. Your warmest audience, 24h ephemeral, near-zero cost. |

**Never publish the same reel to all three in the same hour.** You lose the ability to answer comments
in the first 60 minutes — which is the single biggest lever on reach on all three — and you learn
nothing about which hook worked before you have spent the next one.

### 4. Weekly cadence
Working week is **Tue–Sat** (Sun/Mon off). That gives **5 reels per week per platform**.
You currently have 5 finished renders banked — that is one full week of publishing already in hand.
**Do not spend them all on one day.**

### 5. Repurpose the winner
After 7 days, whatever over-performed goes back out on **WhatsApp Status** a fortnight later with a
different caption/first line. Same asset, second life.

---

## What to measure — and what to ignore

**Ignore:** view count on its own. It flatters and it does not sell.

**Track per reel per platform at 24h and 7d:**
- **Average watch time** — the only real signal of whether the hook and pacing work
- **Saves + shares** — intent to act later
- **Comments, and whether you replied**
- **DMs / WhatsApp enquiries naming the reel** — this is the bridge to money
- **Sales attributed** — the only number that counts

Your own data already proves the format: **FB reels pulled 224 and 175 views with 10m17s and 6m23s
watch, while text Pro-Tip posts got 0–1 views.** Reels are the business. Text tips are dead weight —
stop making them.
