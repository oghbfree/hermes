## Policy
- Draft / test renders at **480p**; 720p only for the final approved pass.
- Plan-first (Kofi shotlist → render once). Re-rolls from weak prompts are the #1 credit burner — a re-roll must be justified in `reason`.
- Duration mismatch → re-cut the edit; **never** re-render VO, never time-stretch.
