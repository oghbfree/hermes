## Caption
The three lines every good build quote needs.

## Setting
Whiteboard behind the workbench. Old-school marker, drawing it live.

## Spoken Script (15-30s, 40-90 words)
Every good trade quote has three lines. First, the materials you are providing. Second, the labour, kept honest and separate. Third, the waste and the contingency — the bit everyone forgets. When you show all three, you look like a professional who has done this before. When you lump it all into one scary number, you look like a guess. Be the clearer one.
