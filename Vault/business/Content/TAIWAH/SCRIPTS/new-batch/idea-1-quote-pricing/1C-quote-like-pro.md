# SCRIPT 1C — Quote Like a Pro
**Idea:** "The quote prices you out of jobs you could have won"  
**Date:** 28/10/26  
**Status:** Draft  

---

## Caption
Quote like a pro, get paid like one.

## Setting
Workshop, sitting on a stool, drill resting on knee. Direct eye contact.

## Spoken Script (15-30s, 40-90 words)
You are never losing a job because you cost too much. Most of the time you are losing it because the customer cannot see what they are paying for. Break your price into what the job really takes. Show the work. Then ask for one clear yes. Here's the part nobody teaches: a confident builder who quotes clearly gets paid faster than the one who worries and half-thinks. Be that one.
