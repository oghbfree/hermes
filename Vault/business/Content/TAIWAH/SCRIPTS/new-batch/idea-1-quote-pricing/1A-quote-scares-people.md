# SCRIPT 1A — Quote Scares People
**Idea:** "The quote prices you out of jobs you could have won"  
**Date:** 28/10/26  
**Status:** Draft  

---

## Caption
You don't lose jobs on skill. You lose them on the quote.

## Setting
Workshop, workbench, tape measure and a quote pad on the timber. Mid-task, looks up to camera.

## Spoken Script (15-30s, 40-90 words)
You don't lose jobs because your price is high. You lose them because your quote scares people. A bare number makes a customer guess. And when they guess, they shop around. Try giving one price with a range built in. Materials, labour, waste. Tell them what you are charging for and what happens after they say yes. A clear quote closes. A scared one walks.
