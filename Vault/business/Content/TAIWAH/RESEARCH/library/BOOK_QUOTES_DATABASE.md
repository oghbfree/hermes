## ATOMIC HABITS — James Clear

### Quote 1: Systems Over Goals
> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

**Taiwah angle:**  
"My tool shop doesn't run on motivation. It runs on Monday inventory check, Tuesday sourcing, Saturday cleanup. When I skip the system, the shop suffers. When I follow it, everything flows. System beats hustle every time."

**Video concept:** Show Taiwah's weekly system (calendar/checklist), contrast with 'motivation Monday' that fades by Wednesday.

## USAGE RULES

1. **Saturday Week 3 only** — don't overuse book quotes (feels preachy if every week)
2. **Taiwah voice filter** — rewrite quote application in Taiwah's words (casual, direct, Ghana context)
3. **Real story required** — every quote needs a real 2Real/Akoma story (Francis customer, Dome Market moment, supplier delay)
4. **Action payoff** — end with one thing viewer can do this week (start a system, pay yourself first, control what you can)
5. **Stock photos allowed** — book cover, quote text overlay, workspace/tools, Ghana builders
6. **CTA always 0204252252** — spoken "CALL NOW" or "BOOK A VISIT", digit pill holds end screen
