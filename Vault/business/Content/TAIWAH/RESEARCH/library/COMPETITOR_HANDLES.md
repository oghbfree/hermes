## SCRAPING INSTRUCTIONS (For Outlier Scanner Cron)

**Per account:**
1. Pull last 7 days posts (or last 10 posts if inactive week)
2. Check views/likes/comments vs account's 30-day average
3. Flag posts ≥2× average engagement (outliers)
4. Extract:
   - Hook (first 5 seconds / opening line)
   - Format (side-by-side comparison, contrarian take, story, stat, tutorial, red flag)
   - Topic (what it's about)
   - Why it worked (tapped quality anxiety, addressed common objection, surprising stat, relatable story)
5. Output: `outliers-external.md` with Taiwah adaptation for each

**Example output format:**
```
@africabuildinghub — Post: "This is how much a mason charges per day in Ghana"
Views: 43.7k (8× avg 5.5k)
Hook: "This is how much..." (cost transparency, direct answer)
Format: Text overlay + voiceover, real numbers, comment engagement
Why it worked: Answered burning question (mason rates), transparency builds trust, Ghana-specific
Taiwah adaptation: "This is how much a REAL Makita drill costs (and why)" — cost transparency + value justification, Ghana pricing
```
