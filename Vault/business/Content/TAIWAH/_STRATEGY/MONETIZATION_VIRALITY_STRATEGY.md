## 0. THE ONE CORRECTION FIRST
**The "4,000 watch hours" target does NOT apply to a shorts-only channel.** It is YouTube *long-form* eligibility.
- Shorts-only YouTube = a **views route** (10M Shorts views / 90 days), not watch hours.
- TikTok, Facebook, Instagram have **no watch-hour requirement** at all.
- **And there is a deadline** — see YouTube section below (Feb 1, 2027 threshold change, verified).

### TikTok — REACH + LEAD GEN ONLY (no per-view pay in Ghana — verified)
- **Creator Rewards is open in only 8 countries** (US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil) — **Ghana is NOT on the list** (verified against TikTok Creator Academy, Oct 2026).
- TikTok Shop also not available in Ghana.
- **LIVE Gifts** is the only TikTok payment route documented for rest-of-world creators (Diamonds → PayPal). Not a video-payout path.
- **Format note (for when/if rewards open to Ghana, or for diaspora viewers):** Creator Rewards only pays on videos **≥1 minute** — the 60–90s podcast variant below is the format that would qualify.
- **Treat TikTok as reach + conversion:** WhatsApp enquiries, Akoma enrolments, brand deals. Monetization = funnel, not platform pay.

### Facebook — SECOND EARNER (Ghana eligible — verified)
- **Meta opened monetization to Ghana (July 2024).** Reels earnings run through **Facebook Content Monetization**.
- Note: older published thresholds (5K followers + 60K minutes) belong to the **old in-stream ads program that ended 31/08/25**. Content Monetization today is **invite-only** with no published rate — **check your own dashboard**: Meta Business Suite → Monetization → Eligibility.
- **You need a Page or Professional Mode ON** — do this now.
- **Stars** = the one guaranteed-paid feature once you hold **500 followers for 30 consecutive days**.

### Instagram — FUNNEL (no per-view pay)
- Instagram does **not pay per view**. Ghana is on the **Gifts** list (500 followers needed); **Subscriptions not open** in Ghana; Bonuses are invite-only and country-limited (US/SK focus).
- **Treat Instagram as funnel too** — same strategy as TikTok: brand deals + 2Real/Akoma conversion.

## 3. ORGANIC VIRALITY — WHAT MAKES TAIWAH SHORTS TRAVEL
Standard Shorts/Reels practice, built on the Craft + Business + Taiwah-moment formula. No guarantees — these are distribution levers.

1. **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds — visually AND spoken.** Open on the result, the mistake, or a bold line — never an intro. The viewer must know the payoff is worth 10 seconds before they decide to stay.
   - Examples: *"I quoted this job wrong and lost 800 cedis."* / *"Principals, what if your school's only job was finding a shelf for one box?"*
2. **Make people watch to the end or replay.** Completion rate + rewatches drive distribution more than anything. **20–35s, one lesson each**, end on a line that loops back to the opening.
3. **UK-vs-Ghana angle = the signature series.** It's what separates Taiwah from every other builder account. Repeatable formats ("Things nobody tells you about building in Ghana", "#BeatsTheUKPrice") give people a reason to follow and binge. The honest friction (price clash, standards compare, what each place does better) is inherently shareable — that's the share engine, lean into it deliberately.
4. **Real pidgin + local pain points.** Material, labour, "getting paid" stories travel in Ghana and the diaspora. End with a question people WANT to answer (*"What's the worst quote you've ever given?"*) — comments are rewarded on Facebook and YouTube.
5. **Series train the algorithm.** `#BeatsTheUKPrice`, `#QuoteLikeAPro`, `#AskTaiwah`, `#TaiwahBuilds` — recurring series give the platform a consistent audience signal, so it pushes to the *right* people. Right-audience views convert better than scattered reach.
6. **Search value (growing signal — especially TikTok + YouTube).** People search "how to quote a tiling job", "tools in Ghana", "UK vs Ghana building". Write the title/caption for the search query, not just the hook. TikTok's Creator Search Insights shows the actual queries — check it monthly.
7. **Reply to comments fast, pin a question, start threads.** Comment engagement is a distribution signal AND the growth loop — every reply boosts the score and the commenter returns. Answer the technical/opinion comments in Taiwah's voice (she already does this — keep it up).
8. **Same series name + look across all 4 platforms. Post natively** — re-cut/re-caption per platform, never raw re-upload with another app's watermark (reposts with watermarks get suppressed).
9. **Consistency beats perfection. One video a day for 90 days** teaches more than ten perfect ones. Study which hooks held viewers, then make more of those.
10. **Tools in motion.** Taiwah's own rule (hero tool in MOTION, never a static card) is also a retention win — keep it.
11. **Trending audio at publish (in-app, licensed).** Use the platform's trending-sound library at publish time — boosts discovery and matches the no-BGM-in-edit rule.
12. **Don't mix Akoma and 2Real audiences in one video.** Separate series, separate frames — mixing confuses the algorithm AND the viewer (2Real = tradesperson, price/quote/CTA 0204252252; Akoma = mentor, child-centred, enrolment CTA, no price talk).

**The 60–90s podcast variant (the one format unlock):** take the best-performing 15s reel and extend it to 60–90s as a podcast-style short (talking beat over her action). Reasons: (a) it's still a short, not long-form; (b) it unlocks TikTok Creator Rewards eligibility *if/when* the program reaches Ghana; (c) longer watch-time signals feed completion/watch-time ranking on every platform; (d) it gives the feed a deeper format without abandoning the shorts strategy. Ship it 1×/week as a flagship.

## 4. THE AI-DISCLOSURE RISK (one to manage — non-negotiable)
- **Taiwah is an AI-generated character.** Label content as AI-generated on **every platform** — YouTube and Meta now require disclosure for realistic synthetic content, and TikTok does too.
- **YouTube demonetizes channels it sees as mass-produced or repetitive.** Keep every video visibly different and always teaching something real.
- **An undisclosed AI persona is the easiest way to lose a monetization application.** Do not skip the disclosure.
