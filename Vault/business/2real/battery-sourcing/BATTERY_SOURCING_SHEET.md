## ORDERING NOTES

1. **Chemistry rule:** All pod tools get **Ni-MH** replacements (drop-in, works with existing chargers). Do NOT buy Li-ion conversions for pod tools unless seller confirms charger compatibility.
2. **2Ah spec:** Correct for all Ni-MH pods. For BL1830B buy 3.0Ah (standard, same money). PAP18 2Ah is fine.
3. **AliExpress vs Alibaba:** 5 pcs per SKU = AliExpress works (no MOQ). But better unit prices on Alibaba with one supplier covering multiple SKUs — ask suppliers to consolidate shipment.
4. **Shipping to Ghana:** Li-ion is hazmat. Confirm seller ships batteries to Ghana (special battery lines, 2–4 weeks air). For 80+ units, a consolidating freight agent is cheaper.
5. **Estimated total:** 80 batteries ≈ **$900–1,300** + shipping. At Jiji resale GHS 250–500/pack, potential retail ≈ GHS 20,000–35,000.
6. **Verification step before paying:** send the seller photos of your dead battery contacts + tool base, ask "does this fit?" — cheapest insurance there is.
