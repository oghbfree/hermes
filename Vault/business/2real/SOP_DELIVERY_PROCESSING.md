# SOP: DELIVERY & CUSTOMER CALLS — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 1.0 | **Purpose:** Automate + standardise the delivery and
customer-interaction procedures for WhatsApp / Jiji orders.

---

## 1. DELIVERIES & COLLECTION (within Accra & Tema)

**Customer messaging:**
- **Delivery** — default: customer pays for delivery first (with whichever rider
  we use). Pay-on-delivery possible same-day (depends on location) or next day —
  our rider sets up deliveries the day before / by 09:00 that morning.
- **Collection** — also available, but the location depends on where the item
  is (Dome retail walk-in, or Oyarifa warehouse). A human confirms the exact
  collection location/availability for the item.

Order flow:
1. Customer orders item/s.
2. Locate and confirm item/s with pictures to customer.
3. Package item ready for courier (if delivering).
4. Once confirmed, confirm the delivery quote with the rider; relay price to
   customer. (Deliveries default pay-before; pay-on-delivery possible same-day
   by location or next day per rider schedule.)
5. Once customer confirms, contact the rider to collect from us.
6. Once the rider hands the item to the customer to check and the customer is
   satisfied, request payment to the **merchant mobile money** (MoMo).
7. **Money must be received before the rider leaves.**

---

## 2. DELIVERIES OUTSIDE ACCRA AND TEMA (station handover)

1. Customer orders item/s.
2. Locate & confirm item/s with pictures to customer. Speak to courier to
   confirm delivery cost to the station.
3. If the customer agrees, they must be informed that:
   - the delivery driver will take the item to the station,
   - locate the **area station** and send contact details
     (receipt, driver's phone number or licence plate) so the customer can
     collect,
   - **customer must agree to this before the transaction can proceed,**
   - **customer must agree to pay the item price AND delivery before handing
     over to the area station driver.**
4. Package item ready for courier.
5. Once confirmed, speak to the delivery company to confirm price and relay to
   customer.
6. Once customer confirms, contact the delivery company to collect.
7. Once the courier gives the item to the customer to check and the customer is
   satisfied, request payment to the **merchant mobile money** (MoMo).
8. **Money must be received before the driver leaves.**

---

## 3. RECEIVING PHONE CALLS

1. **Greet:** *"Hello, good afternoon, 2 Real — how may I help you?"*
2. If you have an idea of the item, inform the customer; otherwise **search on
   Jiji** and inform the customer.
3. If the customer agrees, arrange collection; otherwise **offer delivery**.
4. After the call, record the customer on the spreadsheet:
   **(Date, Name, Phone number, Item).**
5. Save the contact on WhatsApp for communication.

---

## 4. MAKING A PHONE CALL (follow-up)

*"Hello, I am calling from 2 Real regarding an item you have shown interest
in. As we do not have many items left, I would like to know if you will
purchase it today. You can come and collect the item, or we can arrange a
delivery at your convenience."*
