### Positioning that sells these in Accra
- **Target buyers, not consumers:** printing shops, schools, churches, architects/surveyors (DesignJet = CAD/plan printing), cyber cafés, small offices. The DesignJet sells on **"A1 plans in 35 seconds"** — that's a surveyor/architect sale, not a home sale.
- **Demo beats description.** A nozzle/print test in front of the buyer kills the "will it work?" objection, which is the real reason wide-format sits.
- **Sell ink honestly** — if carts are low/expired, say so and price accordingly. A buyer who discovers dry carts returns the machine.
- **Bundle the two consumer inkjets** as a pair for an office starter.
