## Tier 1: Join First (4-6 groups)

### For 2Real Enterprises (retail + hand-tools + hardware, Dome walk-in & Oyarifa warehouse)

**1. Accra Buy and Sell**
   - facebook.com/groups/1166899307145476/
   - ~25k members, Accra-wide. Accepts product posts.
   - Best for: weekend tool deals, market-day promos, bulk/trade offers.
   - Post cadence: 1x/week (Saturday or Sunday morning).

**2. Buy & Sell New or Used in ACCRA (Ghana)**
   - facebook.com/groups/bssinaccra/
   - Active daily, explicit buy/sell intent.
   - Best for: shelf-item spotlights, clearance, new arrivals.
   - Post cadence: 1x/week.

**3. GHANA MARKET PLACE**
   - facebook.com/groups/444635012242059/
   - Large national group. Good for brand awareness.
   - Best for: hero product posts (not every-week items).
   - Post cadence: 1x every 2 weeks.

### For contractors/trades (farm build-out, real estate)

**4. Construction Workers in Ghana**
   - facebook.com/groups/325816082451777/
   - Group for building/road construction workers.
   - Best for: sourcing labour for Senya Beraku, materials deals.
   - Tone: professional, relationship-first. Not a sales channel for 2Real products.
   - Post cadence: 1x/month (project updates, labour needs).

### For entrepreneurs / H's personal network

**5. Entrepreneurs/Business Owners in Ghana**
   - facebook.com/groups/453923745029239/
   - Business community, Ghana-focused.
   - Best for: H's personal brand, sharing business lessons, finding partners.
   - Tone: value-first, mentor-mode. Never product pitches.
   - Post cadence: 1x/week (H posts personally, not 2Real page).

### For Akoma Robotics (parenting/education)

**6. Parents United GH**
   - facebook.com/groups/1076962897567045/
   - Parenting community, pre-tertiary school focus.
   - Best for: Akoma Robotics content (kids, STEM, learning tools).
   - Tone: helpful parent, not salesperson.
   - Post cadence: 1x/week.

## Reusable Sunday Group-Post Template

**Tone:** Community-first, warm, non-ad-speak.
**Format:** Value line → personal touch → CTA (soft).
**Photo:** sat-nav supplies ready-to-post stills.

### Template

[Photo from sat-nav]

Good morning, family. Happy Sunday.

This week at 2Real we've been thinking about [one thing: value — e.g. "the right tool for the job / getting the kids excited about learning / what we're learning about quality kit"]. 

We've been at it at Dome Market for [X years], and one thing we keep coming back to: [honest insight — e.g. "the right tool saves you a whole day / the best learning starts with a curious mind"].

[1 short sentence about the product/offer, framed as a solution]

If you're in Accra, come see us at Dome Market or Oyarifa — or DM to order. No pressure, just real stock, honest prices.

Have a blessed week.

— H & the 2Real team

### Variations by group

| Group | Angle | First line swap |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| Parents United GH | Akoma Robotics: kids + learning | "As a parent and someone working with young minds, I've been thinking about..." |
| Entrepreneur group | H's personal reflection on business | "Building 2Real from Dome Market to Senya Beraku — here's what I'm learning this week..." |
| Construction group | Farm build progress (not sales) | "Week [N] at the Senya Beraku site. [Photo]. Learning a lot about..." |

### What NOT to do

- No "BUY NOW!!!" or ALL-CAPS in group posts.
- No links in first comment (FB flags as spam). Put "DM to order" or "WhatsApp 0XXX."
- No more than 2 group posts per week until a group knows you.
- Never cross-post the same photo to 3 groups in one day.
