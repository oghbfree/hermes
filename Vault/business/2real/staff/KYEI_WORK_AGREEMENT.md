### The shape of it (worth noting)
The pattern is **not** "a bit every day, more at weekends." It's **1 hour on the four days H
works, 4 hours on the three days H doesn't** (Sun + Mon are H's standing days off; Sat is a
working day but gets the same 4h).

1. **Coverage** — Kyei is the presence on Sunday and Monday when H isn't there. That's the
   point of the arrangement, and it's sound.
2. **Supervision gap** — nobody is on site for 8 of Kyei's 16 weekly hours. If the work needs
   checking, it gets checked Tuesday, a day late. Worth a simple written task list so the
   work is self-evident rather than needing H to inspect it.

to misremember from both sides. This file is the single record: one row per worked day.

never project hours forward as if worked.
