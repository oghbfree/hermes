# 🇨🇳 China Sourcing Playbook — 2 Real Enterprises
> One page · Chief of Staff (sat-nav) · 22/10/26 · Grounded in live research of Accra's Chinese hubs

## Why this reframes sourcing
Current model = UK **used** goods, ~71% margin. China = **new** goods, thinner margin but **volume + new-product lines** Jiji buyers search for.
**Rule: DON'T replace the UK margin line.** Use China for cheap **consumable volume** (spray paint, adhesives, cables, small electronics) where velocity makes up thinner margin.

## Mindset
**Go as a wholesale buyer offering a repeat-volume relationship, not a shopper.**
These businesses live on volume and are used to Ghanaian micro-traders buying from them. **The pitch is steady repeat orders, not price-shopping.**

## What to bring (one page)
1. **Your proven-seller list** — inverters (970–1,000), drills (Bosch sold 700–1,580), spray paint (relentless repeaters), extension leads, lights, power tools. Ask: "what have you got in these, can you source more?"
2. **Your volume** — "I sell 20–40 of these a month across shop + Jiji." Volume unlocks wholesale pricing.
3. **Your terms** — on the table early.

## The pitch (adapt)
> *"My name's Oman — I run 2 Real Enterprises, a shop at Dome Market plus active Jiji/Facebook/WhatsApp selling. I sell [list] — these move steadily. I want to buy in bulk, regularly, if your pricing works. Can you show me what you stock in these, and can you source more from China for me if I order a consistent quantity? If the numbers work I'll be a standing customer — monthly orders, not one-offs."*

## Negotiate these (the money is here)
- **Wholesale/MOQ price** — reseller price, not shelf
- **Consistent-order discount** — commit monthly volume → better per-unit
- **Samples first** — quality "isn't always assured"; buy ONE of each before bulk
- **Invoice for VAT** — China Mall had a VAT-compliance shutdown; a proper-invoice wholesaler protects you (you're not VAT-registered — same 3% treatment as act ICT)
- **Delivery / stock-holding** — do they hold at Tema/Adenta so you restock fast?
