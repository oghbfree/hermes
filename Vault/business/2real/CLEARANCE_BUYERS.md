## 🛒 THE CLEARANCE FUNNEL (how it works)

1. **Sort clearance stock** into yellow/orange Amazon bags (staging area at shop)
2. **Wholesale buyer visits** (Ske Man) — evaluates bulk working goods
3. **Accepts?** → Wholesale deal (best margin recovery)
4. **Rejects?** → Goes to scrap dealers (Mashood or Tanko) for broken/damaged electronics + metal

**Principle:** Don't bulk-sell ALL stock — sort first. Wholesale gets working items; scrap gets broken/damaged only.

## 📦 CLEARANCE STAGING (yellow/orange Amazon bags)

**Purpose:** Pre-sort clearance stock BEFORE buyer visits  
**Location:** Shop (Dome walk-in or Oyarifa warehouse)  
**Process:**
1. **Yellow bags:** Working goods for wholesale eval (Ske Man first look)
2. **Orange bags:** Broken/damaged for scrap (Mashood/Tanko)
3. **Sort as you go** — don't wait for one big clearance day

**Rule:** Never sell ALL your stock wholesale — only the items that won't move retail (slow SKUs, minor damage, container tail-end).

---

## 💰 PRICING GUIDE (exit margin recovery)

| Channel | Condition | Target Price | Margin Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wholesale (Ske Man)** | Working | 40–60% of Jiji price | Best recovery (still selling as goods) |
| **Scrap (Mashood/Tanko)** | Broken/damaged | Scrap rate (metal kg + board value) | Lower, but clears dead stock |

**Example:**
- DeWalt drill (broken) listed 1,200 GHS on Jiji → won't sell retail
- Wholesale pass (doesn't take broken tools)
- Scrap: 50–80 GHS (motor + metal + board scrap value)
- **Recovered margin:** better than zero; frees shelf space

## 📋 WHEN TO ACTIVATE CLEARANCE

✅ **Call wholesale (Ske Man) when:**
- Yellow bags full (5+ working items ready for bulk eval)
- Container clearance needed (UK shipment tail-end, slow movers)
- Need cash flow boost (convert slow stock to liquidity)

✅ **Call scrap (Mashood/Tanko) when:**
- Orange bags full (broken/damaged batch ready)
- Wholesale rejects items
- Confirmed broken items accumulate (dead grinders, smashed laptops, etc.)

❌ **Don't call clearance for:**
- Fast-moving SKUs (Gorilla, Loctite, DeWalt bits) — these sell retail at full margin
- Items you haven't tested yet (test first, then route)
- Premium tools with minor damage (repair may beat scrap value)
