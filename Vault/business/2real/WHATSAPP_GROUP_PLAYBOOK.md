## 1. SELL — what to post, where, how often

### Expats / garage groups (Garage Sale, Ghana Mazad, near-expat)
- **What:** UK-brand electronics, tools, household — items they *recognise*. Bosch/Stanley/DeWalt, cameras, audio, kitchen, baby gear.
- **Angle:** "UK Imported, Tested Working" + delivery. Expats trust UK provenance — that IS the story (Godin: outcome = no-hassle quality).
- **Cadence:** 2-3 quality posts/week, spaced. NO daily spam.
- **Photo:** 1 real photo + price + WhatsApp 0204252252. No "BUY NOW!!!"

### Accra Buy & Sell / Winners Page / Ghana Recommends (local volume)
- **What:** everyday movers — spray paint, Gorilla glue (proven seller!), smalls, deals.
- **Angle:** "Pickup Dome / Oyarifa • Delivery available." Price-forward OK here (local buyers are price-driven — cheapest is what they want).
- **Cadence:** 1-2 posts/week each.

### Construction / woodwork / tools groups (HIGHEST conversion)
- **What:** everything tools — drills, saws, power tools, hand tools, consumables (Stanley, Bosch, Makita, Gorilla).
- **Angle:** Godin SVA — **"For the builder who's lost a morning to a cheap tool…"** (freedom-from-fear + status). One audience: tradespeople.
- **Cadence:** 2-3 posts/week in rotation across the groups (never same photo in 3 groups same day).
- **Bonus:** respond to "need a tool" posts FAST — first reply wins.

### The one-liner offer (works in ALL groups):
> "UK Imported tools & electronics — tested working, honest condition, delivered in Accra. WhatsApp 0204252252. Pickup Dome or Oyarifa."

## 2. SOURCE — the flip machine (monitor, don't obsess)

Your expat groups are a **free second Freegle**: people leaving Ghana sell quality UK goods at give-away prices.

- **What to watch:** UK-brand tools, electronics, appliances, building materials — your proven resell categories.
- **Buy signal:** price clearly below UK-equivalent value (e.g. a £100 drill listed at GH₵150 = flip to ~GH₵500+).
- **Routine (NOT all-day):** scan each source group **once daily (evening, ~10 min)**. Save promising listings to `Vault/business/networking/flip-candidates.md`. Buy within 24h or it's gone.
- **Rule:** only buy what you can sell at 2x+ after your own money + test time. No inventory hoarding.
- **Ghana Mazad is auction-style:** watch for slow-bid items ending cheap — snipe once, then flip.

## 3. NETWORK — reputation + leads (Spurs, Entrepreneurs, farmers)

- **Entrepreneurs Club:** H posts personally (mentor-mode, like FB §6): one business lesson/week. No product posts. Yields B2B + wholesale buyers (your Eric/Kuber pattern).
- **Farmers groups:** your farm angle — tools + garden equipment (Ben-type buyers, the condemned-goods buyer pattern). Mention the farm build-out; trust = shared ground.
- **Spurs Official:** community only — be a fan, be known. Referrals come to people people like.

## 4. The SYSTEM (rules so it's sustainable)

1. **Group onboarding:** for each group log → name · job (sell/source/network) · rules (ads allowed? posting windows?) · whether admin sells similar goods (if yes, expect friction — stay value-first).
2. **Cadence cap:** max 3 group posts/day TOTAL across all groups; never same photo twice in a day.
3. **Response speed:** replies within minutes = first reply wins. Keep the WhatsApp 0204252252 CTA on every post.
4. **Every order logged** in daily-sales-log + customer-interactions (the loop: Discover → Assign → Act → Verify → Persist).
5. **Never post where the group says no ads.** Never ALL-CAPS. Never link-farms (WhatsApp links get flagged — put the number, not a link).
