## Why this matters (30-sec version)
Right now every 2Real customer relationship lives on **rented land** — Jiji owns the chat, FB owns the group, WhatsApp owns the thread. If any platform changes overnight or an account gets flagged, you lose the relationship and the data. An **owned opt-in list** is the one thing you control: a customer's number/name on YOUR list, in YOUR registry, that you can ping on restock or first-pick without a platform's permission.

It also **directly feeds your #6 highest-ROI lever** (the repeat-buyer broadcast list). Every person who opts in is a proven buyer or warm lead — the exact people worth messaging.

## The offer (keep it dead simple)
**"2Real First-Pick List — get first access to new stock + restock alerts before anyone else. Free to join."**

- **What they get:** a WhatsApp message when fresh stock lands (e.g. "New Gorilla adhesive 250ml just in — first-pick for list members"), plus first dibs on high-demand items before they hit Jiji/FB.
- **What you get:** their number + name on YOUR list, permission to message them (they opted in), and a growing owned asset.
- **CTA everywhere:** WhatsApp **0204252252** — "Join the First-Pick List" on Jiji listings, FB posts, WA Status, and the content engine's quote-card footer.

## How it plugs into the buyer registry
- **Opt-in = auto-add to BUYER_REGISTRY.md** under a new `🟣 First-Pick List` section (name + number + "opted in").
- The **buyer-registry scanner** (being built) reads the WhatsApp chat list and flags candidate repeat buyers; the opt-in message is the cleanest signal — if someone replies "yes" to joining, they're in.
- **Broadcast rule:** only message people who opted in or are confirmed repeat/B2B buyers. Never cold-broadcast to everyone.

## 3-question test (from the video) — passing
1. **Can they buy from you today?** — Yes, via Jiji/FB/WA/Dome. (Not yet on an owned page — see gap below.)
2. **Can the not-ready take something free to stay in touch?** — ✅ **YES (this is the new piece):** join the First-Pick List, get restock alerts free.
3. **What would you sell next to a past buyer?** — ✅ Yes: first-pick offers on restocks + cross-sells (e.g. Gorilla buyer → other adhesives/tools).

## The honest gap (owned landing page)
The opt-in is the *first* owned collection point, but the cleanest version needs **one owned page** where people can join (a simple link you control). Options, cheapest first:
- **(a) WhatsApp-only opt-in (zero cost, now):** CTA "text 'FIRSTPICK' to 0204252252" → you (or the WA gate) add them to the list. No page needed.
- **(b) Link-in-bio / simple page:** a free Linktree-ish page or a single HTML page on a domain you own, with a "join the list" button → opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled.
- **(c) Full landing page:** an owned page with the offer + opt-in form → feeds a list. Most work, most owned.

**Recommendation:** start with **(a)** — zero cost, works today, and the scanner can auto-capture anyone who texts "FIRSTPICK." Upgrade to (b) when you want a shareable link.
