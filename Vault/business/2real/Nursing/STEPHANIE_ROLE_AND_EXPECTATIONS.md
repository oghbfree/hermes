## 1. DAILY ROUTINE

### MORNING — day starts at **6:00am**

**1a. Open & secure the house (6:00am, first thing)**
- [ ] Unlock main door / check gates are opening properly
- [ ] Open windows/doors to air the house (morning air)
- [ ] **Sweep the compound** (front + around the house) while it's still cool
- [ ] **Security walk-through** of the compound — gates, doors, perimeter
- [ ] Confirm all windows can close & lock properly; note any that stick/move

**1b. Mum's room & Mum**
- [ ] Gentle morning check — awake, comfortable, no overnight issues
- [ ] Morning vital signs + log them
- [ ] Tidy Mum's room: make the bed, hang clothes, clear surfaces
- [ ] Open her window for fresh air (close before heat/evening, secure at night)

**1c. Living area — set up the house**
- [ ] Arrange the sofa & chairs neatly
- [ ] Plump / puff the cushions so they're presentable
- [ ] Wipe down all tables & surfaces (dust, clean)
- [ ] Sweep / mop the floor (whole main area)
- [ ] Make sure everything is in place and tidy for the day

**1d. Kitchen & cleaning**
- [ ] Clean the kitchen daily — surfaces, sink, stove top
- [ ] Empty all bins — no rubbish left overnight or through the day
- [ ] **No flies**: clear food scraps, keep bins covered, dispose immediately
- [ ] Wash/dry dishes; hang kitchen cloths to dry
- [ ] Wipe fridge spills; check fridge daily (rotate excess items before they spoil)

### AFTERNOON — ongoing (attentive & proactive)
- [ ] Re-check throughout the day — tidy as needed, not just once
- [ ] Keep surfaces clean as they're used
- [ ] Re-sweep main/floored areas if needed
- [ ] Check windows as they're used — close & secure as the day cools
- [ ] Keep up: no flies, no dishes left, bins never overflowing

### Mosquito check & spray — late afternoon (~4–5pm)
- [ ] **Mosquito check around 4–5pm** — spray the rooms/areas then
- [ ] **Mum sits outside** until the spray fumes have dissipated (until safe/odour-free)
- [ ] Close windows/doors while spraying so it works; ventilate afterwards
- [ ] Confirm Mum is comfortable outside while she waits (shade, water, company)

### EVENING — security check & secure the house (end of day)

**Inside**
- [ ] Windows — ALL shut & latched (every room, including Mum's)
- [ ] Doors — ALL locked, inside doors too
- [ ] Final mosquito check — spray/protection as needed for the night
- [ ] Bins emptied or covered for the night (no flies, no smells)
- [ ] Final tidy — cushions/flat surfaces presentable for the morning

**Outside / compound**
- [ ] Gates & external doors locked
- [ ] Walk the perimeter — gates, fences, any access points
- [ ] Bring in anything left outside (clothes line, chairs, tools)
- [ ] Outside / security lights on if needed

**Mum settled**
- [ ] Confirm Mum in bed, comfortable, night light on
- [ ] Her window closed & locked; any care/log needs done
- [ ] Confirm the house is secure overnight before you rest
