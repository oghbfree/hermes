## ⏱ MORNING ROUTINE (the day starts at 6:00am)

### 1. OPEN & SECURE THE HOUSE — 6:00am (first thing)
- [ ] **Unlock** main door / check gates are opening properly
- [ ] **Open windows/doors** to air the house (morning air)
- [ ] **Sweep the compound** (front + around the house) while it's still cool in the morning
- [ ] **Do a security walk-through** of the compound — gates, doors, perimeter
- [ ] Confirm **all windows can close & lock properly**; note any that stick/move for fixing

### 2. MUM'S ROOM & MUM
- [ ] Gentle morning check on Mum — awake, comfortable, no overnight issues
- [ ] Morning vital signs + log (see `MUM_MEDICAL_MASTER.md` / daily routine)
- [ ] **Tidy Mum's room**: make/mooch the bed, hang clothes, clear surfaces
- [ ] Open her window for fresh air (close before heat/evening, secure at night)

### 3. LIVING AREA — SET UP THE HOUSE
- [ ] **Arrange the sofa & chairs** neatly
- [ ] **Plump / puff the cushions** so they're presentable
- [ ] Wipe down all **tables & surfaces** (dust, clean)
- [ ] Sweep/mop the **floor** (whole main area)
- [ ] Make sure **everything in place** and tidy for the day

### 4. KITCHEN & CLEANING
- [ ] **Clean the kitchen** daily — surfaces, sink, stove top
- [ ] **Take out / empty all bins** — no rubbish left overnight or through the day
- [ ] **No flies**: clear food scraps, keep bins covered, dispose of rubbish immediately
- [ ] Wash/dry dishes; hang kitchen cloths to dry
- [ ] Wipe fridge spills; check fridge daily (use/rotate excess items before they spoil)

### 5. AFTERNOON / ONGOING (attentive & proactive)
- [ ] **Re-check** throughout the day — tidy as needed, not once only
- [ ] Keep surfaces clean as they're used
- [ ] Sweep high-traffic/floored areas again if needed
- [ ] Check windows as they move / are used — close & secure as the day cools
- [ ] Keep up: no flies, no dishes left, bins never overflowing

### 5b. MOSQUITO CHECK & SPRAY (late afternoon)
- [ ] **Mosquito check around 4–5pm** — spray the rooms/areas then
- [ ] **Mum sits outside** until the spray fumes have dissipated (keep her out until it's safe/odour-free to re-enter)
- [ ] Close windows/doors while spraying so the spray works; ventilate afterwards
- [ ] Confirm Mum is comfortable outside during the wait (shade, water, company)

### 6. EVENING — SECURITY CHECK & SECURE THE HOUSE (end of day)

Do a **full evening security check**, top to bottom, before settling Mum for the night.

**6a. INSIDE the house**
- [ ] **Windows — ALL shut & latched** (every room, including Mum's)
- [ ] **Doors — ALL locked**, inside doors too
- [ ] Final mosquito check — spray/protection as needed for the night
- [ ] Bins **emptied or covered** for the night (no flies, no smells)
- [ ] Final tidy — cushions/flat surfaces presentable for the morning

**6b. OUTSIDE / compound**
- [ ] **Gates & external doors locked**
- [ ] Walk the **perimeter** — check gates, fences, any access points
- [ ] Bring in anything left outside (clothes line, chairs, tools) that could fly away or get taken
- [ ] Confirm outside lights / security lights on if needed

**6c. MUM settled**
- [ ] Confirm Mum in bed, comfortable, night light on
- [ ] Her window **closed & locked**; any care/log needs done
- [ ] Confirm the house is **secure overnight** before you rest
