## Breeding vs Fattening Decision

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|-------|------|------|----------|
| **Breeding (2 does + 1 buck)** | Self-replacing, manure steady, kids = income/expansion | 5-month gestation, need buck management, more feed | Long-term farm integration |
| **Fattening (buy 4–6 weaners, sell 4–6 months)** | Faster cash turn (4–6 months), simpler, no buck | Recurring purchase cost, no manure continuity | Quick cash flow |

**Recommendation**: **Start with breeding** (2 does + 1 buck). Kids = future stock + sales. Manure = soil. Fits permaculture loop.
