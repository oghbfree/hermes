| Harvest | Character | Forage Signature |

## 6. Key Insight — The Two-Harvest Rhythm

Your farm's nectar year has **two peaks**:

```
Jan  Feb  Mar  Apr  May  Jun  Jul  Aug  Sep  Oct  Nov  Dec
     ▓▓   ███  ███  ▓▓▓  ░░░  ░░░  ░░░  ▓▓   ███  ▓▓   ░░░
          ▲ mango/citrus              ▲ citrus2/coconut
          HARVEST 1 ─────────►        HARVEST 2 ──────►
          (May-Jun pull)              (Oct-Nov pull)
```

**Coconut is the never-ending base** — it's why your honey is genuinely "Coastal Bloom" multifloral year-round. Mango and citrus are the **seasonal superchargers** that make the two big harvests worth labelling separately.
