I, Peter [surname], make the following statement of my own free will:

1. I am the son of the late **Uncle Sam** of Bonsokuo, who served as **caretaker of the Senya Beraku Stool land at Akwaass/Onsuku** under the authority of the palace and the stool's Lands Authority (the Akondoh Ewinde authority of the late **Nenyi Akondoh XI**, a former Chief of Senya Beraku).

2. My late father, Uncle Sam, guided **Mr. Oman Herbert Blankson** to the said land in or around 2014. Mr. Blankson is a **grandson of the late Nenyi Akondoh XI** — his mother is the second-born daughter of the late Chief of Senya Beraku.

3. I personally witnessed that Mr. Blankson viewed the land, agreed terms with the palace (through the late Nenyi Nicholas and Nenyi Aroger), and made payments for the land in instalments through the stool's Lands Authority.

4. My late father and I saw Mr. Blankson develop the land continuously — including planting coconuts, food crops, and constructing structures — over the years since 2014.

5. To my knowledge, Mr. Blankson **completed the full payment** of GHS 30,000 for the 7.628 acres. The stool's own receipt, No. OSL/001/15 dated 22 November 2015, acknowledged part payment of GHS 8,000, and was issued by the stool's Lands Authority under the Akondoh Ewinde authority.

6. To my knowledge, no other person was granted the said land by the Senya Beraku Stool. The purported lease by the Kwei Krom chief (Ebusuapanyin Kwesi Begyina and Nana Kwei Ababio XIII) to Flycotec Company Limited in December 2020 was, to my knowledge, made **without any authority over the said land**.

7. I make this statement willingly to assist Mr. Blankson, the stool, and any court or tribunal in establishing the true history of this land.
