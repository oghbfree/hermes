## RE: COMPLETION OF INDENTURE, ISSUANCE OF FINAL RECEIPT AND STOOL PROTECTION OF MY LAND — 7.628 ACRES AT SENYA ONSUKU (PILLARS SGC/E612/15/1–6)

Dear Nana and Elders,

### 1. Who I Am

I write to you as a **grandson of the late Nenyi Akondoh XI, a former Chief of Senya Beraku**, my grandfather on my mother's side. My mother is the second-born daughter of the late Chief of Senya Beraku. I am therefore not a stranger to this stool, but a member of its royal family (adamfo of the Akondoh lineage), writing to you in a spirit of respect, family unity, and good faith.

### 2. Background of the Acquisition

Sometime in 2014, I expressed to my uncles my desire to acquire family stool land for farming. I was directed to the palace, where I met the late Nenyi Nicholas and Nenyi Aroger, who identified land available for viewing. I was guided to the land by the stool's caretakers, the late **Uncle Sam** and his son **Peter** of Bonsokuo, both appointed by the stool.

Following customary introductions at the palace, I agreed to purchase a parcel of stool land measuring **7.628 acres (3.087 hectares)** at Senya Onsuku, Awutu Senya District, for **GHS 30,000**, payable in instalments while I developed the land. The transaction was conducted through the stool's own Lands Authority — the **Senya Beraku Stool Lands Authority under the Akondoh Ewinde lineage of my grandfather, Nenyi Akondoh XI** — which issued me **Receipt No. OSL/001/15 dated 22 November 2015**, acknowledging part payment of **GHS 8,000** towards the seven-acre land at Akwaass, Senya Beraku. I have since completed **full payment** of the said GHS 30,000.

This was both a **bona fide purchase** and an **intra-family grant made with the knowledge and blessing of the palace**. I formalised the grant with payment precisely so that my title would be definitive and beyond future family redistribution — an act of prudence and respect for the stool, not a renunciation of my family rights.

### 3. Delay in Documentation

At all material times I requested my final receipt and indenture. I was repeatedly asked to return — the secretary was unavailable, chieftaincy matters were pending — and I complied each time, continuing to develop the land in good faith on the stool's assurance. The deaths of my grandfather Nenyi Akondoh XI, the late Nenyi Nicholas, and our caretaker Uncle Sam during this period contributed to the delays. Because the final receipt was not issued, I could not register the land earlier. This delay — caused by circumstances on the stool's side, not mine — is the sole reason a third party was able to interfere with my land.

### 4. The Land Was Clean at Purchase

An Official Search from the Lands Commission dated **16 June 2015 (O/S No. CCT/2014/15)**, requested by me, confirms the site is **not State Land** and that the portions were **not affected by any recorded transaction** at the time of my purchase. I annex a copy. I purchased from the stool in good faith and in priority to any later transaction. I note with respect that my grandfather, Nenyi Akondoh XI, was himself actively dealing in and registering stool land in this very area during that period (e.g., his lease to Lexington Property Development Company Ltd dated 17 February 2014, referenced in the same search report), confirming his capacity and the stool's authority over the land.

### 5. The Encroachment

In or around **December 2020** — five years **after** my documented part payment and after I had completed payment — Ebusuapanyin Kwesi Begyina and Nana Kwei Ababio XIII of Kwei Krom executed leases over my parcel in favour of **Flycotec Company Limited** (23 December 2020) and **Kas Estate Ghana Limited** (28 December 2020), as confirmed by the Lands Commission Official Search report dated **5 July 2023 (W1/183/2023)**, a copy of which I annex.

Flycotec has since destroyed my crops (including my coconut plantation of some 230 trees), erected survey pillars and a building on my land, and continues development. I have measured and documented the encroached area (**approx. 0.44 acres**, GPS survey of 31 August 2026, coordinates and photographs annexed).

I respectfully record that when I raised this matter, **Nenyi Aroger stated on 24 February 2025** that the Kwei Krom chief *"sold the land and they shouldn't do this because they don't have authority/jurisdiction."* I am grateful for this confirmation, and for the stool's decision of **26 March 2025** to demarcate the land and prepare an indenture in my name — a process now underway with the draft indenture prepared between the stool (through Tufuhene Bentum IV, Acting President) and myself.

An affront against this land is an affront against the stool itself and against the house of Nenyi Akondoh XI. The Kwei Krom chief had no jurisdiction over Senya Beraku stool land, and certainly none over land granted within the ruling family itself.

### 6. My Demands

I therefore respectfully request that the stool, within **14 days** of the date of this letter:

1. **Complete and execute the indenture** in my name over the 7.628 acres bounded by pillars SGC/E612/15/1–6, and issue my **final receipt** — one that expressly acknowledges the part payment of GHS 8,000 (Receipt OSL/001/15) and the completion of the total consideration of GHS 30,000;
2. **Fix a date for the surveyor** to re-confirm the pillars and boundaries, as agreed on 26 March 2025;
3. **Warrant and defend my title** as grantor, in accordance with customary law and the stool's duty to its own family, by:
   - (a) formally summoning the Kwei Krom chief and elders to the palace to answer for the purported 2020 leases;
   - (b) issuing a **stool letter of title protection** addressed to Flycotec Company Limited and Kas Estate Ghana Limited, confirming that the land belongs to the Senya Beraku Stool and has been validly granted to me, a grandson of Nenyi Akondoh XI, and demanding they cease all development and remove their pillars;
   - (c) confirming in writing whether the stool will join or support me in any action against Flycotec, including the pending matter involving Kas Estate and Ebusuapanyin Begyina;
4. **State the exact fee** for the indenture processing (per your letter of 26 March 2025) and provide an official receipt for it;
5. **Confirm in writing** that no further sales or leases of any part of my parcel have been made or will be made by the stool;
6. **Acknowledge in writing** my family's continuous occupation and development of the land (my coconut plantation of 230 trees, food crops, beehives, and structures), including cultivation carried out on my behalf by Sammy of Bonsokuo, son of the late caretaker Uncle Sam.

### 7. Reservation of Rights

This letter is written in good faith and in a spirit of preserving our relationship with the stool and protecting the dignity of the house of Nenyi Akondoh XI. However, if the stool does not respond within 14 days, or if Flycotec's development continues, I will be compelled, without further notice to anyone, to:

- (a) register my interest with the Lands Commission upon execution of the indenture;
- (b) instruct solicitors to issue a cease-and-desist notice and seek an interlocutory injunction against Flycotec Company Limited;
- (c) report the malicious destruction of my crops and trespass to the Police and the Lands Commission;
- (d) pursue all remedies available under customary and statutory law, including claims for damages against all parties involved.

I trust the stool will act swiftly to protect a son of the royal family who has honoured his obligations in full over more than ten years, and whose grandfather served this stool as Chief.

Yours faithfully,
