**ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF LAND OWNERSHIP AND PERMISSION TO FARM**

**Date:** _______________

I, **Sammy [full surname]**, of Bonsokuo, near Senya Beraku, Awutu Senya District, son of the late **Uncle Sam** (caretaker of the Senya Beraku Stool land at Akwaass/Onsuku), hereby declare as follows:

1. The farmland lying at Akwaass/Onsuku, Senya Beraku, measuring approximately **7.628 acres** (bounded by survey pillars SGC/E612/15/1 to SGC/E612/15/6), belongs to **Mr. Oman Herbert Blankson** of Accra, who purchased it from the Senya Beraku Stool.

2. Mr. Blankson is a **grandson of the late Nenyi Akondoh XI**, a former Chief of Senya Beraku, and the transaction had the knowledge and blessing of the palace.

3. I have farmed portions of this land **with the permission of Mr. Oman Herbert Blankson**, the owner, and I continue to do so.

4. I recognise **no other owner** of this land. I know of no grant or permission given by me or my family to any other person (including Flycotec Company Limited or Kas Estate Ghana Limited) to enter, farm, or develop any part of this land.

5. I make this declaration willingly and truthfully.
