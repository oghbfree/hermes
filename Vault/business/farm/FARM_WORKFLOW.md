## 🎯 Core Operational Principles

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Water = Bank** | Every drain → pond. Every drop captured. Dry season irrigation from wet season storage. |
| **Daily Eyes** | 7 AM + 6 PM photo + voice note from farm lead. No exceptions. |
| **Skin in the Game** | Staff paid on *verified* output (harvest kg, sales GHS, beds weeded), not attendance. |
| **Redundancy** | 2 people know every system (pump, keys, drainage, bees, goats). |
| **Record Everything** | Harvest kg, sales GHS, expenses GHS, rainfall mm, pond level cm → Obsidian daily log. |
