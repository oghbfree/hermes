## ⚖️ Habib + Family Harmony Framework

### Division of Authority
| Role | Person |
|------|--------|
| **Overall caretaker & H's main contact** | **Habib** (seniority — he was there first, knows the farm) |
| **4-acre plot lead** | **The family man** (independent, reports to H directly) |
| Shared tools | Keeper: Habib (tool inventory under his control) |

### Work Split — What Each Covers

**Habib (3 acres + existing roles):**
- Hive visual checks (every 2 days) — NON-NEGOTIABLE, stays with him
- Sugar water / drinker top-ups
- Coconut circles (50cm) — his current ongoing work
- Beehive area security
- Tool custody

**Family man (4 acres + compound):**
- Weeding his 4 acres
- Compound cleaning (both rooms, yard, well area)
- Water fetching for household use
- Nursery help
- Crop observation & pest reporting
- Harvest labour (paid extra per harvest? decide)

### Points of Friction to Manage Upfront

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Who gives instructions? | Habib is NOT the family man's boss. Both report directly to H. Prevents rivalry. |
| Shared tools | One register, Habib signs out/in. Family man borrows, returns same day. |
| Water / borehole use | Share. Habib's use for hives = priority; family household use second. |
| Compound cleaning duty | Alternating weeks OR family does compound, Habib does hive area only. |
| Noise/kids near hives | 2-year-old must be kept AWAY from hive area (sting risk). Physical distance rule. |
| Family visitors | Family may host visitors but must inform Habib; no overnight strangers without H's OK. |
| Sammy farming on the land | Decide: does he continue under this new arrangement? Coordinate with Peter before family arrives. |

### Weekly Check-in (H via WhatsApp)
- Habib: hive report (existing checklist)
- Family man: 4-acre plot report + compound status (photo)
