## 1. 🥥 Coconut Water Pouches — RAW, FRESH

### Brand Position
**This is a health-conscious product.** Coconut water is prized for its natural electrolytes, enzymes, and nutrients — pasteurization destroys these. We sell it **raw, fresh, and cold-chained** — same as you'd drink it straight from the nut.

### Equipment Needed
- DZ260D chamber sealer (critical — handles liquid without sucking into pump)
- Refrigerator or cooler (for cold chain)
- Vacuum pouches (food-grade, 100–200ml size)
- Heat press (for outer label seal)

### Raw Materials
- Mature coconuts (free from farm — 230 trees)
- Fine mesh strainer
- Optional: lime, ginger, mint for flavoured versions

### SOP — Step by Step

| Step | Action | Detail |
|:----:|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Harvest** | Select mature brown coconuts. 1 nut = ~300ml water |
| 2 | **Husk & crack** | Remove outer husk, crack open, collect water in clean container |
| 3 | **Strain** | Pass through fine mesh strainer to remove solids |
| 4 | **Chill** | Refrigerate immediately to 4°C — preserves freshness |
| 5 | **Fill pouches** | Fill vacuum pouches with 100–200ml each. Leave 2cm headspace |
| 6 | **Seal** | Chamber sealer: set vacuum time ~5-8 sec for liquid. Seal |
| 7 | **Keep cold** | Store in fridge/cooler until sale — **shelf life: 5–7 days refrigerated** |
| 8 | **Label** | Date, brand, "Natural • No Preservatives • No Pasteurization", keep refrigerated |
| 9 | **Sell cold** | Dome Market — sell from a cooler. Customers see it's fresh, not shelf-stable |

### Why No Pasteurization
| Method | Effect | Our Choice |
|--------|--------|:----------:|
| Pasteurized (63°C/30min) | Kills pathogens, shelf-stable 6 months | ❌ Destroys enzymes, electrolytes degrade |
| **Raw, cold-chained** | **Full nutrients, natural enzymes, fresh taste** | **✅ 5–7 day fridge life — sell as "Fresh from Senya Farm"** |

> **Health-conscious positioning**: "Straight from the coconut to the pouch, never heated, never preserved. Fresh from Senya Farm."

## 2. 🌶️ Shito (Ghanaian Black Pepper Sauce) — HIGHEST POTENTIAL

### Equipment Needed
- DZ260D chamber sealer (for liquid/grainy sauce)
- Heat press (for stand-up pouches)
- Heavy-duty cooking pot (large batch)
- Blender or food processor (for grinding pepper)

### Raw Materials
- **Fresh chilli peppers** (Scotch Bonnet, Long Red — you have seeds planted)
- **Dried fish** (herrings, shrimps — from market)
- **Onions, garlic, ginger** (farm or market)
- **Vegetable oil** (palm oil + veg oil blend)
- **Tomato paste** (market)
- **Salt, stock cubes** (market)

### SOP — Cooking

| Step | Action | Detail |
|:----:|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Prep ingredients** | Grind peppers, onions, garlic, ginger separately |
| 2 | **Heat oil** | Large pot, medium heat. Mix palm + veg oil 50:50 |
| 3 | **Fry aromatics** | Onions first → garlic/ginger → pepper paste — fry until oil separates |
| 4 | **Add protein** | Dried fish, shrimps (rinsed, deboned). Stir well |
| 5 | **Season** | Salt, stock cubes, tomato paste. Simmer 20-30 min |
| 6 | **Cool** | Remove from heat. Let cool to room temp |
| 7 | **Fill pouches** | Chamber sealer: 200g or 500g stand-up pouches |
| 8 | **Seal** | Chamber seal. For extra security, heat press the top edge |
| 9 | **Label** | Brand name, date, ingredients, net weight, spice level (Mild/Hot/Very Hot) |
| 10 | **Shelf life** | 6 months unopened (oil preserves it). Refrigerate after opening |

## 3. 🍯 Honey Portion Packs

### Equipment Needed
- Home vacuum sealer (dry goods — simpler than chamber)
- Small pouches (50g, 100g)

### Raw Materials
- Honey from your hives (Kanzoni shop + farm hives)
- Optional: ginger, lemon, cinnamon for infused varieties

### SOP

| Step | Action |
|:----:|--------|
| 1 | **Harvest honey** | Kanzoni or Kwasi extraction — **cold extraction only, no heating** |
| 2 | **Strain** | Through fine mesh to remove wax bits — **raw, unheated** |
| 3 | **Weigh** | 50g or 100g portions |
| 4 | **Fill pouches** | Leave 2cm headspace |
| 5 | **Seal** | Home vacuum sealer on dry setting |
| 6 | **Label** | "Senya Farms **Raw** Honey — 100g", date, "Do not heat — keeps natural enzymes" |
| 7 | **Sell** | GHS 10/50g, GHS 15–20/100g |

> **Raw honey positioning**: Most supermarket honey is pasteurized (heated), which kills natural enzymes, antibacterial properties, and pollen. Your honey is **raw, unheated, unfiltered** — the health-conscious buyer pays a premium for this.

### Convenience Angle
- Bulk honey sells GHS 80–120/kg
- 100g packs sell at GHS 15 (= GHS 150/kg equivalent)
- **50% more revenue** just by portioning
