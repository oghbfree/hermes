# Daily Exercise Schedule — Precision Ankle Pump Routine

**Estimated Total:** ~12 minutes/day (two 6-minute sessions)

---

## ☀️ Morning Session — After Waking / Before Getting Up
*Goal: Activate circulation and clear fluid that pooled overnight.*

| # | Exercise | Reps | Time |
|---|----------|------|------|
| 1 | **Precision Ankle Pump** | 20 reps | ~90s |
| 2 | **Seated Calf Raise** | 15 reps | ~60s |
| 3 | **Slow Ankle Circle** | 10 ea. dir. per foot | ~90s |
| 4 | **Seated Knee Extension** | 10 per leg | ~60s |
| 5 | **Standing Heel Raise** | 15 reps | ~60s |

**Best done:** Before getting out of bed (exercises 1–4 can be done seated on the edge of the bed). Exercise 5 (standing) done after getting up, using a chair or wall for balance.

---

## 🌙 Evening Session — After Dinner or Before Bed
*Goal: Flush out fluid accumulated during the day's sitting/activity.*

| # | Exercise | Reps | Time |
|---|----------|------|------|
| 1 | **Precision Ankle Pump** | 20 reps | ~90s |
| 2 | **Seated Calf Raise** | 15 reps | ~60s |
| 3 | **Slow Ankle Circle** | 10 ea. dir. per foot | ~90s |
| 4 | **Seated Knee Extension** | 10 per leg | ~60s |
| 5 | **Standing Heel Raise** | 15 reps | ~60s |

**Best done:** After the evening meal, in a comfortable chair with back support. TV-watching time works well.

---

## Notes

- **Safety:** Always hold onto a stable chair/wall for the Standing Heel Raise.
- **Pacing:** Rest 15-30 seconds between exercises if needed.
- **Consistency > Intensity:** 6 minutes twice daily is more effective than one long session.
- **Swelling check:** If ankles look puffier than usual in the evening, consider adding an extra set of Ankle Pumps before bed.
