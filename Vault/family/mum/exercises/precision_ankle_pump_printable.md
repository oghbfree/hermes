# Precision Ankle Pump Routine — Printable Daily Sheet
**For Mom** | Total Time: ~6 minutes | Do **2× daily** (Morning & Evening)

---

## 📋 Quick Reference Card

| # | Exercise | Reps | Hold | Timing | Key Cue |
|---|----------|------|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Precision Ankle Pump** | 20 | 2 sec | ~90 sec | Heel down, toes up → hold 2s |
| 2 | **Seated Calf Raise** | 15 | 2 sec up / 2 sec down | ~1 min | Slow lower = eccentric pump |
| 3 | **Slow Ankle Circles** | 10 each way × 2 feet | — | ~90 sec | Full circles, both directions |
| 4 | **Seated Knee Extension** | 10 each leg | 2 sec | ~1 min | Straighten leg parallel, hold |
| 5 | **Standing Heel Raise** | 15 | 2 sec up / 2–3 sec down | ~1 min | Body weight = max pump |

**Total per session: ~6 minutes** | **Daily total: ~12 minutes**

## 💡 Tips for Mom
- Sit tall, back supported for seated moves
- Use chair back for balance on standing heel raises
- Breathe normally — don't hold breath
- Stop if any sharp pain (mild stretch is fine)
- Consistency > intensity — daily matters most
