# Precision Ankle Pump Routine — Reference Guide
**For Mom's Ankle Swelling / Circulation** | Source: Physiotherapy protocol | Updated: 2026-07-18

---

## 📋 Complete Exercise Reference

### 1. Precision Ankle Pump (Primary Pump)
**Purpose:** Maximize calf muscle pump → push fluid up from ankles
- **Position:** Sitting, heel on floor, foot relaxed
- **Movement:** Pull toes UP toward shin (dorsiflexion) → HOLD 2 seconds → relax down
- **Reps:** 20
- **Key:** Heel stays planted. Don't rush the hold — the 2-second hold IS the pump.
- **Feel:** Calf should feel light contraction.

---

### 2. Seated Calf Raise (Eccentric Focus)
**Purpose:** Slow eccentric lowering = stronger pump, builds calf endurance
- **Position:** Sitting, feet flat, knees ~90°
- **Movement:** Rise onto toes (2 sec up) → SLOW lower (2–3 sec down)
- **Reps:** 15
- **Key:** The SLOW DOWN is the money rep. Don't drop the heel.
- **Progress:** Add weight on thighs (water bottles, book) when easy.

---

### 3. Slow Ankle Circles (Mobility + Lymphatic)
**Purpose:** Move ankle through full ROM → lymphatic drainage + joint health
- **Position:** Sitting, one foot slightly off floor
- **Movement:** Full slow circles — 10 clockwise, 10 counter-clockwise → switch feet
- **Reps:** 10 each direction × 2 feet = 40 circles total
- **Key:** Go SLOW. Full range. Pain-free only.
- **Feel:** Gentle stretch all around ankle.

---

### 4. Seated Knee Extension (Quad Activation + Pump Assist)
**Purpose:** Quads assist venous return; knee extension = calf stretch at top
- **Position:** Sitting tall, back supported
- **Movement:** Straighten one leg until parallel to floor → HOLD 2 sec → lower slow
- **Reps:** 10 each leg
- **Key:** Don't lock knee hard. Squeeze quad at top.
- **Feel:** Front of thigh works; gentle calf stretch at top.

---

### 5. Standing Heel Raise (Functional Weight-Bearing Pump)
**Purpose:** Full body weight = maximum calf pump; functional carryover to walking
- **Position:** Standing, hands on chair back / counter for balance
- **Movement:** Rise onto toes (2 sec) → SLOW lower (2–3 sec)
- **Reps:** 15
- **Key:** Full range. Don't bounce. Control down = pump.
- **Progress:** Single-leg when strong enough (hold support).

## ⏰ Protocol Summary

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Frequency** | 2× daily (Morning + Evening) |
| **Session Time** | ~6 minutes |
| **Daily Total** | ~12 minutes |
| **Progression** | Add reps (+5) or weight when current reps feel easy for 3 days straight |
| **Consistency Target** | 14/14 sessions per week (2× daily × 7 days) |
| **Review** | Reassess swelling / comfort at 2 weeks, 4 weeks |

---

## ⚠️ Safety & Modifications

| Issue | Modification |
|-------|--------------|
| Knee pain on knee extension | Reduce range — only extend to comfortable range |
| Balance issues on standing heel raise | Use two hands on sturdy surface; do seated calf raise instead |
| Sharp ankle pain (not stretch) | Stop that move. Ankle circles only if pain-free. |
| Fatigue / cramping | Reduce reps by half, build back up |
| Swelling worsens | Stop exercises, elevate legs, contact physio/GP |

## 📞 When to Contact Physio / GP
- Swelling increases despite consistent exercises
- New pain, redness, heat in calf/ankle
- Shortness of breath / chest pain (seek urgent care)
- No improvement after 2 weeks of daily consistency
