# Precision Ankle Pump Exercises for Mom

## Exercise 1: The Precision Ankle Pump
- **Reps:** 20 (approx. 90 seconds)
- **How to do it:** Sit flat with your back supported. Press your heel firmly down into the floor while pulling your toes and forefoot up toward your shin as far as comfortably possible.
- **Key:** Hold the contraction for 2 full seconds at the top before completely releasing back to a flat foot. This specific 2-second hold activates the entire calf belly to forcefully eject blood back up toward the heart [08:15].

## Exercise 2: The Seated Calf Raise
- **Reps:** 15 (approx. 1 minute)
- **How to do it:** While seated with feet flat, lift both heels off the floor as high as they will comfortably go, keeping the balls of your feet on the ground. Hold for 2 seconds at the top.
- **Key:** Lower your heels back down very slowly (taking 2 full seconds). This slow descent creates an "eccentric contraction" that adds extra pump activation [10:32].

## Exercise 3: The Slow Ankle Circle
- **Reps:** 10 reps each direction, per foot (approx. 90 seconds)
- **How to do it:** Lift one foot slightly and rotate your ankle in a slow, full circle. Do 10 times clockwise, 10 times counter-clockwise, then switch feet.
- **Key:** This moves the joint through all planes, gently compressing the surrounding network of lymphatic vessels to stimulate drainage of fluid that has leaked into the ankle tissue [12:14].

## Exercise 4: The Seated Knee Extension
- **Reps:** 10 reps per leg, alternating sides
- **How to do it:** From your chair, slowly straighten one leg out in front of you until it is parallel to the floor. Hold for 2 seconds at full extension (feeling a light stretch behind the knee), then slowly lower it.
- **Key:** This clears the popliteal fossa (the junction behind the knee) where blood often bottlenecks when sitting at a 90-degree angle for too long [13:53].

## Exercise 5: The Standing Heel Raise
- **Reps:** 15 (loaded pump)
- **How to do it:** Stand behind a chair with both hands on the back for balance. Rise all the way up onto the balls of your feet, hold for 2 seconds, and then take 2 to 3 seconds to lower your heels back down.
- **Key:** Because you are lifting your entire body weight, this generates the most forceful calf contraction of the whole routine and yields the greatest improvements in clearing pooling blood [18:31].

## Total Estimated Time: ~6 minutes
