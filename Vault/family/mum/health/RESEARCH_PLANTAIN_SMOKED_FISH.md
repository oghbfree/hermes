# RESEARCH: Plantain & Smoked Fish — Pros/Cons for Comfort (92, CKD 3b, T2D, HTN)
> Completed 16 Sep 2026 · Sources: KidneyFoods (USDA/KDOQI-verified), Kidneyline, WebMD, Acibadem, peer-reviewed PAH studies (Nigeria/Cambodia/Poland)

## 🍌 PLANTAIN — Verdict: ✅ KEEP, but green + boiled + small portions
### Pros (evidence-backed)
- **Low glycemic index (GI ~40s — under the 55 "low" threshold)** — WebMD lists plantain as a genuinely good diabetes choice; resistant starch in GREEN plantain digests slowly, doesn't spike blood sugar, feeds gut bacteria.
- Fibre, potassium, magnesium, B6, vitamin C, vitamin A; supports satiety and bowel regularity (relevant to her 8 Sep loose stools? Note: sudden increases cause gas/bloating — introduce gradually).
- Boiled/baked/roasted = healthy everyday preparation.

### Cons (the CKD 3b catch)
- **Potassium ~471mg/100g = 24% of the CKD Stage 3–4 daily potassium limit — officially rated "LIMIT" for CKD.** More potassium than banana. Ripe (yellow/black) plantain has MORE potassium and magnesium than green, plus more sugar.
- Fried plantain (kelewele, chips) = calorie-dense, oil-absorbing, often salted — not heart-friendly; her 30–31 Aug fried-plantain days coincided with her BP 138/92–138-92 diastolic-high readings (correlation, not proof — but the pattern supports the caution).

### Rule for the house (matches the 22 Aug plan)
**Green/semi-ripe only, boiled or roasted, small portion (~½ cup), not daily, never on days she also eats other high-potassium foods (banana, orange, cocoyam, beans in quantity).** Steve's "no plantain" is stricter than needed IF green+boiled; Ferguson's "rare/small" is the right balance. Dr Morris/17 Sep labs should include **potassium** — that number decides whether even the rare portion stays.

## 🐟 SMOKED FISH — Verdict: ❌ PHASE OUT — strongest ban of the two
### Pros (the honest side)
- Cheap, palatable protein; omega-3 PUFA retained (Cambodian study: good EPA+DHA source); smoking preserves without refrigeration.

### Cons (serious, multi-source)
- **PAH contamination: West African market smoked fish repeatedly measured ABOVE EU safety limits** — Southern Nigeria study (PMC5615098): PAH4 up to 15× the EU limit (0.12 vs 0.03 mg/kg region); benzo(a)pyrene above the 0.05 mg/kg guideline in catfish & bonga; lifetime cancer-risk estimates exceeding USEPA thresholds. Ghana trends noted in the literature too.
- **Sodium/salt curing** → direct blood-pressure impact — her BP is the active battle.
- **Biogenic amines** (tyramine, histamine) from microbial action in smoked fish: tyramine causes **hypertension, headache** — an avoidable BP risk with her profile; histamine risk with poor storage.
- Her own record: the **31 Aug regurgitation episode followed a kenkey+shito+smoked-fried-fish lunch** — consistent with fatty/smoked/processed foods disagreeing with her.

### Rule for the house
**Phase out fully.** Replacements that satisfy the same taste slot: **fresh fish stewed/baked in homemade (low-salt) tomato-pepper gravy**, grilled tilapia (fresh, not smoked), koobi-free light soups. If she strongly craves it, allow a rare small piece of *fresh*-grilled fish with the same pepper sauce — the flavour anchor (pepper/ginger/garlic) can be preserved without the smoked vehicle.

## One-line summary for Stephanie
> Plantain: green + boiled + small + rare = fine. Smoked fish: out — fresh fish cooked the same way is the swap.
