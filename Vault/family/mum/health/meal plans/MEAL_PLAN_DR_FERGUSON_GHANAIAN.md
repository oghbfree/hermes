## 🔬 Dr Ferguson's Core Principles (Mum's Protocol)

1. **Alkaline-focused** — green vegetables, fruits, nuts; minimize acid-forming foods
2. **Plant-based first** — vegetables, legumes, whole grains as the foundation
3. **Limited animal protein** — small portions of fish preferred; red meat sparingly
4. **No processed food / no refined sugar** — whole foods only
5. **Living enzyme-rich** — raw or lightly cooked where possible; steaming > frying
6. **Green smoothie daily** — leafy greens + fruit + water as staple breakfast/drink
7. **Hydration** — water, herbal teas throughout the day

**Adaptations for Mum (CKD 3b, Diabetes, HTN, BMI 39.2):**
- Soft textures (stewed, mashed, flaked — easy to chew and digest)
- Low-salt preparation (HTN management)
- Controlled portion sizes (obesity management)
- No added sugar (diabetes — HbA1c 41, controlled)
- Adequate protein but not excessive (CKD consideration)
- Small, frequent meals rather than 3 large ones

## 📅 7-DAY MEAL PLAN

### MONDAY

**Mum — Breakfast (08:00)**
- Key: Soft, warm, easy to eat
- Soft-boiled egg (if available) or milky porridge (koko — millet porridge, no sugar, touch of honey)
- Sliced papaya or banana

**Mum — Lunch (13:00)**
🐟 **Saltfish Stew with Rice** *(Primary: Saltfish)*

- *Dr Ferguson note: dried fish is salt-heavy — soak well to reduce sodium (HTN); portion small*

**Mum — Dinner (18:30)**
🐟 **Smoked Mackerel Pâté with Yam** *(Primary: Smoked mackerel ×1)*
- Flake smoked mackerel, mash with small knob of butter and squeeze of lemon
- Serve with boiled yam (soft, sliced)
- Steamed alefu greens on the side
- *Dr Ferguson note: mackerel = omega-3 (anti-inflammatory); butter kept minimal*

**Ebony — Lunch**
🐟 **Saltfish Stew with Rice** *(same pot, larger portion, can handle more pepper/spice)*

**Ebony — Dinner**

- Roasted plantain
- Sautéed okro
- *Dr Ferguson note: red meat is acid-forming — keep to 1-2x/week max; lamb is leaner than beef*

## ⚠️ Clinical Notes for Mum

**Diabetes (HbA1c 41 — controlled):**
- No added sugar in any preparation
- Honey only in tiny amounts (½ tsp max)
- Fruit portions controlled (½ banana, small pawpaw)
- Rice portions moderate (not heaped)

**CKD 3b (eGFR 41):**
- Protein portions kept moderate (not excessive)
- Saltfish soaked thoroughly to reduce sodium
- Adequate hydration between meals
- Avoid high-potassium excess (banana limited to ½ per serving)

**Hypertension:**
- Minimal added salt in all cooking
- Dawadawa used sparingly (sodium content)
- Pepper for flavour instead of salt

**Obesity (BMI 39.2):**
- Portion control — small plates
- No fried foods (all steamed, baked, stewed)
- Butter used sparingly

**Housebound / Mobility:**
- All meals soft-textured (stewed, flaked, mashed)
- Easy to eat with minimal chewing
- Smaller, more frequent meals if appetite is low

## 🔄 Carer Cooking Instructions

1. **Prep the night before:** Soak saltfish overnight; marinate meats
2. **Batch cook:** Rice and banku can be made in bulk for 2-3 days
3. **Separate seasoning:** Cook the base stew/soup mild for Mum; add pepper/salt to Ebony's portion after
4. **Texture check:** All fish should flake easily; all vegetables soft (not crunchy)
5. **Green smoothie daily:** Blend fresh greens + banana + ginger + water every morning for Mum
6. **No frying:** Steam, bake, poach, or stew only
7. **Butter:** Use sparingly — small knob per dish, not generous
