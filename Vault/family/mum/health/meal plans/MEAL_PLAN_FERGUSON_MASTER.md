## 🔬 CORE PROTOCOL RULES (Dr Ferguson)
1. **Alkaline-focused** — vegetables, fruits, nuts; minimise acid-forming foods
2. **Plant-based first** — vegetables, legumes, whole grains as foundation
3. **Limited animal protein** — small fresh-fish portions preferred; red meat sparingly
4. **No processed food / no refined sugar** — whole foods only
5. **Living enzyme-rich** — raw/lightly cooked; steaming > frying
6. **Green smoothie daily** — leafy greens + fruit + water
7. **Hydration** — ≥1.5–2 L/day of water/unsweetened liquid

### Adaptation for Mum
- Soft textures only (stewed, mashed, flaked)
- Minimal added salt (use pepper, lemon, ginger, garlic)
- Controlled portions; small frequent meals
- Fruit only before 12 PM (before noon)
- **NO smoked fish ever** · **NO plantain ever** (see Research note below)
