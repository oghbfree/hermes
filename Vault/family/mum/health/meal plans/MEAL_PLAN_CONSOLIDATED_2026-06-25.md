## 🔬 Dr Ferguson Protocol (Core Rules for Mum)

1. **Alkaline-focused** — vegetables, fruits, nuts; minimize acid-forming foods
2. **Plant-based first** — vegetables, legumes, whole grains as foundation
3. **Limited animal protein** — small portions of fresh fish preferred; red meat sparingly
4. **No processed food / no refined sugar** — whole foods only
5. **Living enzyme-rich** — raw or lightly cooked; steaming > frying
6. **Green smoothie daily** — leafy greens + fruit + water
7. **Hydration** — water, herbal teas throughout

### Clinical Adaptations for Mum
- CKD 3b, Diabetes (HbA1c 41), HTN, BMI 39.2
- Soft textures only (stewed, mashed, flaked)
- Minimal added salt (use pepper, lemon, ginger, garlic for flavour)
- Controlled portions
- Fruits only before 12 PM
- Small, frequent meals
- **NO smoked fish ever**
- **NO plantain ever**

## 👩‍🍳 Cooking Instructions (Carer / Stephanie)

1. **Prep the night before:** Marinate chicken/fish; chop vegetables.
2. **Batch cook starches:** Banku, fufu, rice can be made in bulk (2–3 days).
3. **Mum’s seasoning:** Cook base mild for Mum; add pepper/salt to household portions after.
4. **Texture check:** All fish flakes easily; vegetables soft (not crunchy).
5. **Green smoothie daily:** Blend fresh greens + banana + ginger + water.
6. **No frying:** Steam, bake, poach, or stew only.
7. **Salt discipline:** NO added salt in Mum’s cooking; use lemon, pepper, ginger, garlic.
8. **Fruit timing:** All fruit for Mum before 12 PM only.
9. **Portion sizes:** Small plates; Mum does not need large servings.
10. **Smoked fish rule:** Any smoked mackerel on premises = household/others only. Never Mum’s pot.
11. **Plantain rule:** Any plantain on premises = household/others only. Never Mum’s pot.
