# 🦠👁️ MICROBIOME + EYE-HEALTH MENU
## For Mum (Comfort, 92) — organic, nutrient-rich, non-inflammatory
> **Purpose:** Feed the gut microbiome (prebiotic fibre, fermented/probiotic foods, polyphenols) AND protect eye health (lutein, zeaxanthin, vitamin A/C/E, omega-3, zinc) — while staying safe for **CKD 3b, diabetes (HbA1c 41) and hypertension**.
> **Created:** 26 Aug 2026 · **Saved in:** `Vault/family/mum/health/meal plans/`
> ⚠️ **Key caveat:** Many classic "gut-health" and "eye-health" foods are **high in potassium, phosphorus or sodium** — for CKD these must be limited. This menu is the **kidney-safe** version.

---

## 🧠 WHY THIS MATTERS FOR MUM SPECIFICALLY
- **Microbiome ↔ kidneys:** a healthy gut flora produces less urea-toxin load and helps regulate inflammation — relevant to her CKD 3b (eGFR 41).
- **Microbiome ↔ blood sugar:** gut bacteria help manage blood glucose — relevant to her diabetes.
- **Eyes:** her last eye check (Sep 2025) was normal (normal IOP, stable Rx). Lutein/zeaxanthin (not made by the body) + omega-3 + zinc help protect against AMD and cataracts as she ages.

## ✅ ALLOWED (kidney-safe microbiome + eye foods)

### PREBIOTIC FIBRE (feed good gut bacteria) — kidney-safe picks
- **Garlic, onions, leeks** (inulin/FOS) — but go easy (moderate potassium)
- **Chicory** (if found) — high inulin
- **Asparagus** — inulin
- **Leeks** — good lutein too
- **Green plantain / unripe plantain** — resistant starch (Ferguson restricts — use small/occasional)
- **Cooked & cooled potato/yam** — resistant starch (reheating increases it)
- **Flaxseed, chia** (small) — fibre + omega-3
- **Tiger nut** — prebiotic resistant starch (already in her diet ✓)

### PROBIOTIC / FERMENTED (live beneficial bacteria) — kidney-safe
- **Plain, unsweetened yoghurt** (small; low-fat) — calcium-rich, gut-friendly, also eye zinc. Check potassium low-serve.
- **Unsweetened kefir** (small)
- **Fermented vegetables** (homemade, LOW-SALT sauerkraut/cabbage kimchi) — the salt is the catch for CKD/HTN; use mild, low-sodium homemade only
- **Fermented brown rice / ogi / kenkey-type** (traditional Ghanaian fermented staples, watch sodium)
- **Bone broth** — gut-lining support; homemade, low-salt ✓

### POLYPHENOL-RICH (feed good bacteria, anti-inflammatory, eye antioxidant)
- **Berries: blueberries, raspberries, strawberries** (allowed — lower potassium than banana/orange) ✓ eye + gut
- **Pomegranate juice** (small, unsweetened) — high antioxidant
- **Red/black grapes, red wine (small)** — she already has red wine as a longstanding part of dinner ✓
- **Green/herbal tea** (ginger, mint, camomile, fennel) ✓
- **Dark chocolate (≥70%, small)** — if tolerated; watch sugar

### EYE-HEALTH SUPERSTARS (also gut fibre) — kidney-safe
- **Leafy greens: kale, spinach, kontomire (cocoyam leaves), lettuce, swiss chard** — lutein+zeaxanthin, folate. NOTE: cook to reduce potassium; moderate portions (spinach/kale high K — serve cooked & moderate)
- **Broccoli, sweetcorn, peas** — lutein/zeaxanthin
- **Eggs** — lutein/zeaxanthin (+ fat improves absorption), vit A/E, zinc ✓ (she has 2 boiled eggs daily — ideal!)
- **Carrots, sweet potato, pumpkin, red/orange peppers** — beta-carotene (vit A). Sweet potato high-ish K — moderate.
- **Oily fish: fresh mackerel, wild salmon, sardines (fresh)** — omega-3 (DHA), kidney-safe if **fresh, not smoked** ✓
- **Avocado** — lutein, vit E, healthy fat (moderate K — small portion)
- **Legumes: black/red/cannellini beans, lentils, peas** — zinc for melanin/retina + fibre. CKD: moderate portions (phosphorus) — serve small, soaked/boiled.

## ⚠️ LIMIT / AVOID (high-potassium/phosphorus/sodium, despite "healthy" rep)
| Food | Why limited for Mum |
|---|---|
| Banana | High potassium (CKD) |
| Orange / orange juice | Citrus acid + high K |
| Dried fruit (dates, raisins) | Concentrated potassium + sugar |
| Potato (large servings) | High potassium |
| Whole grains (oats, barley, quinoa) in bulk | Phosphorus load (CKD) — small/occasional |
| Nuts in large amounts | Phosphorus + potassium — small handfuls only |
| Smoked/cured fish, processed meats | Sodium + phosphorus (CKD, HTN) |
| Commercial fermented sauces (soy, Maggi) | High sodium |
| Store-bought kombucha (if sweetened) | Sugar + sodium |
| Excessive red meat / fried food | Inflammation + saturated fat (also bad for eyes — deposits under retina per AAO) |

## 🧑‍🍳 KITCHEN PRINCIPLES
1. **No frying** — steam, poach, stew, grill, roast, or bake (frying creates AGEs/free radicals — bad for gut & eyes)
2. **Low salt** — homemade fermented veg, herbs, garlic, ginger, lemon, pepper for flavour
3. **Eat the rainbow daily** — colour = polyphenols + carotenoids (gut + eye)
4. **Leafy greens cooked** (reduces potassium; better tolerance in CKD)
5. **Small frequent meals** — easier on gut, better glucose
6. **Fermented foods small & homemade** — live cultures without excess sodium
7. **Organic where affordable** — reduce pesticide load; wash thoroughly
8. **Hydration** — ≥1.5–2 L water/herbal tea daily (kidney & gut)
