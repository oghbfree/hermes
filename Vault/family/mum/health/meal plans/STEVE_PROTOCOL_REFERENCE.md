## 🍲 MAKING HOMEMADE STUFF (recipes)
- **Mayonnaise:** 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 egg yolk + ½ cup coconut/avocado oil + pinch turmeric; refrigerate
- **Vegetable muffin (frittata):** 2 tbsp coconut oil, spring onions, 1 clove garlic, coconut milk, black pepper, bell peppers, onions, zucchini, ghee butter, salt
- **Bone broth:** organic free-range chicken + rosemary, oregano, thyme, black pepper, turmeric, carrots, onions, garlic, ginger, salt — boil ~7 hours
- **Gluten-free biscuits:** ghee butter, eggs, coconut flour, baking powder, honey, salt — bake
- **Almond biscuits:** 250g blended almonds, 2 eggs, salt, ghee butter, 1 tbsp honey — chill 30 min, bake
- **Coconut dumplings:** coconut milk + coconut flour / cassava flour + salt — boil; serve with vegetable sauce
- **Dumplings:** cassava or coconut flour + salt + water — mould, boil, eat with vegetable sauce
- **Floor/cleaning detergent:** 1 tbsp bicarbonate of soda + 1 lemon in water for mopping

## ⚕️ AILMENT REMEDIES (healing common ailments)
- **Cold & cough:** 2 cloves garlic + cayenne pepper + honey in warm water — 1 tbsp ×3 daily (not too hot); crush garlic 15 min before use
- **Runny/stuffy nose:** plenty of fluids (esp. water); saline nasal spray
- **Yeast die-off reactions:** place warm towel on abdomen
- **Fever:** soak socks in water, wring + wear; sponge with cold water bath (toe→head, not head→toe)
- **Fever blisters:** cotton swab in oil of cloves → apply to base; destroys germ & helps remove
- **Mucus dissolver:** pineapple (with skin+leaves) + ginger + lemon — boil 5–10 min to drink
- **Mouth sores:** flavonoids in citrus fruits/juices help; eat the white membranous segments (concentrated)
- **Headaches:** fresh fruit & vegetable juices
