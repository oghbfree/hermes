## 🧑‍🍳 CARER'S KITCHEN RULES (this plan)
1. **No frying** — steam, bake, roast, poach, stew, or soup
2. **No grains** — replace corn-dough porridge with **tiger-nut porridge / coconut milk porridge**; replace rice with **cauliflower "rice" / coconut flour** in small amounts
3. **No butter** — use **ghee or coconut/avocado oil**
4. **No banana / orange / pineapple / groundnut**
5. **Low salt** — use unprocessed rock/sea salt sparingly + herbs, ginger, garlic, pepper, lemon
6. **Fruit only before noon** (allowed = mango, pawpaw, watermelon, pear, grapes)
7. **Protein-heavy breakfast** — eggs/fish/chicken in the morning (Steve: protein helps focus)
8. **Steamed veg preferred** — raw veg may be harsh on the gut
9. **Do less carbs** — yam/cocoyam/plantain only in small portions
10. **Improvised starches** — use cassava flour / coconut flour dumplings with vegetable sauce (recipes in Steve's reference)
11. **Home-made favoured** — bone broth, frittata, dumplings (recipes in `STEVE_PROTOCOL_REFERENCE.md`)
12. **Hydration** — ≥1.5–2 L water / herbal teas daily
