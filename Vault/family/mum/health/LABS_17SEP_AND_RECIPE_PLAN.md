# 🥤 DR MORRIS RECIPES — SCREENED for Comfort (K 5.48 ⚠️ · Na 161 🚩 · HbA1c 4.0 ✅ · eGFR 68)
> Traffic-light system. At potassium 5.48 (top of range), high-K fruit goes to AMBER/RARE; sugary juices limited (she's diabetic-controlled but juice = fast sugar). Water is the real medicine this week (sodium 🚩).

## ✅ GREEN — serve freely / regularly
- **Green juice: Celery + Parsley + Apple + Ginger** — the standout: celery is a natural sodium-potassium balancer (mild diuretic — pairs well with Furosemide), apple is lower-K, ginger anti-inflammatory. ⭐ Best recipe on the list.
- **Cucumber juice** — hydrating, very low K — perfect for the sodium problem.
- **Watermelon juice** (small glass) — hydrating, lower K, lower sugar per serving.
- **Fruit salad: Apples + Grapes (small), Plums, Watermelon, Pawpaw (small), Tangerine (occasional)** — lower-K picks.
- **Warm water + lemon** — keep.

## 🟡 AMBER — small portions, 2–3×/week max, never two on one day
- **Carrot + Beetroot + Ginger + Lemon juice** — nutritious but beetroot is K-moderate-high → small glass, not daily.
- **Pawpaw smoothie + natural milk** — pawpaw moderate K; milk adds K too → small glass.
- **Mango + natural milk** — mango moderate-K, sugary → small, occasional.
- **Starfruit + strawberry** — 🚨 EXCEPT: **starfruit is a known kidney toxin in CKD — OMIT starfruit entirely** (neurotoxin risk even at Stage 2 with her numbers/age). Strawberry fine.
- **Pineapple + lemon + ginger** — pineapple is on the phase-out list + K-moderate → rare, tiny.
- **Blueberry + strawberry juice** — fine fruits but juice concentrates sugar → small.

## 🔴 RED — off the menu (potassium 5.48 + diabetes)
- **Orange juice** — high K + fast sugar. Fresh orange slices occasionally > juice.
- **Banana in any smoothie** — highest-K item on the list. Out (consistent with the existing phase-out).
- **Coconut (hard) + Dates + Tiger nut "natural milk"** — all three are potassium-dense. ⚠️ This "natural milk" is the biggest trap on the list: delicious but exactly what a K of 5.48 can't support regularly. If Dr Morris wants a milk base, dilute small amounts or ask him about it explicitly.
- **Pomegranate + pineapple juice** — pomegranate very high K.
- **Grapes juice** (drinking juice) — grapes as whole fruit fine (green), juice concentrates K+sugar.
- **Jackfruit + milk** — jackfruit is very high K. Out.

## Suggested weekly rhythm
- **Daily:** 1 green juice (celery-parsley-apple-ginger) or cucumber juice · water goal set by Dr Morris · fruit salad from green list
- **3×/week:** one amber smoothie, small glass, before 4pm
- **Never:** red list items; no two amber items same day
- **Rule of thumb for Stephanie:** if it's a thick sweet juice, it's probably high-K — the greener and more hydrating, the better for the sodium 🚩
