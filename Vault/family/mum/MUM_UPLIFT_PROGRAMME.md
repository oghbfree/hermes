## 💪 2. EXERCISES WITH STEPHANIE (twice daily, 10–15 min)
**Safety first:** all seated or holding steady support · stop if pain · no massage unless she asks · after the 11 Sep fall, keep everything stable & supervised.

### 🌅 Morning set (with Stephanie, ~10 min) — in bed or on chair
1. **Ankle pumps** — point & flex feet ×10 each (helps circulation + oedema)
2. **Ankle circles** — 5 each direction
3. **Knee marches** (seated) — lift knees alternately ×10
4. **Shoulder rolls** — up, back, down ×8
5. **Neck turns** — gentle left/right ×5 (go slow; she had neck pain 9 Sep)
6. **Hand squeezes** — squeeze soft ball/washcloth ×10 each hand
7. **Arm circles** — small to large ×8

### 🌤️ Afternoon set (or evening) — chair-based
1. **Sit-to-stand** — from sturdy chair, arms optional, ×5 (builds leg strength; helps prevent falls)
2. **Heel raises** (holding chair) ×10
3. **Seated boxing** — "punch" forward alternately ×10 each arm (fun + shoulders)
4. **March in place** (holding support) ×20 steps
5. **Breathing** — 5 slow deep belly breaths (relaxation; helps BP)

### 🌳 Walking (the fun one)
- **Compound lap** with Stephanie or a grandchild — 5–10 min, her pace, morning + afternoon
- Make it an **activity, not exercise**: "let's check the flowers", "walk to see the neighbours", "supervise the kids playing"

**Track it:** Stephanie logs what was done + how she felt in the daily report (a simple "✔ exercises done, enjoyed the punching one").
