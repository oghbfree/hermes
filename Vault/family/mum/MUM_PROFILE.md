## 👶 Early Life & Family
- Born at **Senya** (now Senya Beraku) to **Nenyi Akondoh** (tenth Traditional Chief of Senya) and **Madam Efua Mansina**.
- Grandfather opened a post office; used funds to take Mum to Winneba.
- Left Senya at age 7, walked the seaside to Winneba with Uncle Gyachie and Okine Anderson.
- Stayed with a lady named **Rose** (post office telephone operator) for 7 years; left Winneba at 14.

## 🎓 Education
- Methodist Infant School / Junior, Winneba
- Nkyamkron Methodist Girls Boarding School — finished Std 7 (1953)
- Ashley Secretarial College, Accra (1954, ~1 year)
- **London:** Paris Academy School of Fashion (1965), 299 Oxford Street (dressmaking; full gowns)
- **Catering:** Lewisham College (City & Guilds 706/1 catering) + Leiths School of Food & Wine — Diploma in Food & Wine

## ✈️ UK Journey
- Left Ghana **10 Nov 1964** on BOAC (student flight); arrived London 11 Nov 1964.
- First worked at the **Grosvenor Hotel** (linen room assistant), then Fashion House Rembrandt, Tottenham Court Road.
- Shorthand typist; worked at **National Lotteries** 4 years as clerical assistant before coming to UK.
- Career: worked from home for years after first child; later became a **caterer** via agency until retirement.

## 💍 Married Life & Family Homes (UK)
- Met husband (Prof Robert Herbert-Blankson) through family; traditional engagement; worked together to buy a house.
- London moves over the years: Stockwell → Putney → Brixton Rd → Catford → Sydenham → Devonshire Rd → **35 Whatley Rd** → **Melford Court** → **Philip Walk** (1 Feb 2010). *(Tracked in real-estate holdings.)*

## 🏡 Personality & Passions
- Skilled maker/seamstress ("making nice things for myself and for people if wanted").
- Loved travel: Venice, Paris, Florida.
- Surgery history: **gall bladder out 1992**.
