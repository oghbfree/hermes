- **Movement**: Passive range-of-motion exercises for the residual limb and intact leg 2–3× daily. Chair-based upper-body exercises (arm raises, seated marches) to maintain circulation and upper-body strength.
- **Mental wellbeing**: Regular social contact (family calls, day centre if attending). Consider Age UK telephone befriending or Royal British Legion support if applicable. Low mood is common post-amputation — low threshold to raise with GP.
- **Falls & wheelchair safety**: Wheelchair brakes on for all transfers. Anti-tip devices checked. Night light for nocturia trips (if transferring).
