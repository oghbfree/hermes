## 2. Practical Feeding Guidance
- **Steady fluids** through the day, not one big drink — glucose-clot-UTI protection. Dark urine = early warning.
- **Small, frequent meals** — easier for hiatus hernia/gastric ulcer than large ones; space away from lansoprazole timing (30–60 min before food).
- **Protein + vitamin C** at each meal (fish, eggs, beans, lean meat; citrus/peppers) to support skin integrity/healing over the stump & sacrum.
- **Fibre + hydration** for bowel regularity (reduces reliance on Laxido/senna).
- Quick-absorbing carbohydrates in moderation (diabetes) — prefer low-GI.
- **Textural safety** given frailty — ensure food is soft/chewable if swallowing/mastication is an issue.
- Mealtime assistance via 4x daily Southwark carer visits (AM/Lunch/Tea/PM).

---

## 3. Foods to Favour / Limit
- **Favour:** oily fish, eggs, beans/lentils, poultry, leafy greens, peppers, oats, wholegrains, water/weak squash, fortified drinks if appetite poor.
- **Limit:** high-sugar drinks, concentrated sweets, very spicy/acidic foods (gastric), excessive caffeine late in day, alcohol (unscreened — caution with meds).

## 5. Watch / Red Flags (diet-related)
- Full-day refusal of eating/drinking → escalation (see FAMILY_INSIGHTS_DAD red flags).

- Any swallowing difficulty, choking, or food-related aspiration risk → flag to carer/GP.
