## Making it work with Kobena specifically (the "sensory" bits)

1. **Every transition needs warning + "now/next".** e.g. "Lego for 10 more minutes, THEN [meaningful]." Use a **finished box / now-and-next board** (from his SLT record — he responded well to these markers).
2. **Low-plot, low-pressure demands.** When you need something (see the "came and check the time" moment), give a **choice + low voice**: "Time or TV? — no stressed voice." Unexpected demands → echolalia/self-slap. Reduce the *elective* demand-count.
3. **Timed screens = the single biggest lever.** Set the timer tofire *with him present*; when it rings, "finished — TV done — [next]".
4. **Brother pairing:** The two fight over toys/attention. Schedule a **couple of shared gross-motor pair activities** (bikes, ball) so the rivalry has an outlet; then separate quiet-times (Kobena builds quietly, Nenyi draws).
5. **Watch for Kobena's refusal cue** (shake hands + "no") — it's a request, not defiance. Answer muilded: "Ok, no Lego — [alternative]" not a struggle.

### Age-gap note (Nenyi: Kobe watches below Kobe's own age level)
**Nenyi's real point:** Kobena (11) is not watching what his *peers* (11yo typical) watch — he's on very young content. That's worth listening to, not dismissing.

**The honest tension:** Kobena is 11 chronologically, but his language/social development tracks younger (clinical record: minimal speech, receptive/expressive delay). So Sesame St / Sago Mini / Dr Panda are *developmentally useful* to him right now. BUT Nenyi is right that kids that age shouldn't be stuck there either — school friends being at a different level is part of why Kobe read as "not ready for mainstream," and those shows emphasise his difference.

**The right move = level up, don't stay.*
- Keep a * smaller* amount of the familiar comfort shows (Sago Mini/Dr Panda) as regulated downtime, BUT
- **Push into age-appropriate content** that still matches his aided attention and interests:
  - **STEM / building** (his Lego love): shows about engineering/space/cars/construction — *e.g. building/LEGO creators, "how it's made" style, space/nature docs.* Visual, low-plot, but *ranked older.*
  - **Simple science / nature documentaries** (Blue Planet-style), animal facts — strong visuals, calm narration, age-suitable.
  - **Construction/vehicle content** and *real* car/train/highway clips — he loves cars/trains/wheels; "real" footage reads older than cartoons while feeding that hyperfoveography.
- **Framing for both:** "We're levelling Kobe up." Set the goal that Kobe's watches match his number of candles more each week. Nenyi can feel *help* by sometimes curating "one big-boy pick" for Kobe that's still gentle.
- **Politic:** *each* screen block stays age-and-level-matched, not identical. Nenyi picks his own; Kobe picks from a *curated "growing up" shelf* (STEM/space/nature/Lego-build) plus a smaller comfort-show slot.

**Checklist to acknowledge Nenyi's point:**
1. Treat it as a sign of care/fairness (Kobe "being kept young") — not a dig.
2. Adjust Kobe's shelf: add age-up content, keep limited comfort shows.
3. Let Nenyi occasionally be the one to find Kobe a big-boy pick — turns sibling, not critic.
