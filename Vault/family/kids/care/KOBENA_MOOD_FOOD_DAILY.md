### Mood & behaviour legend (keep simple)
- **Mood:** calm · happy · anxious · irritable · flat · distressed
- **Behaviour/regulation:** calm / stimming (which type) / sensory-seeking / sensory-overload / great transition / struggled with transition / good interaction / fought over toy
- **Sleep:** rough bedtime → wake time; if restless/awake overnight, note it
