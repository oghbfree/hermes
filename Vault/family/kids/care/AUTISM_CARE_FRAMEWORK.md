## The principle
An "all-rounded view" comes from **seven lenses**, each with a named professional (or department) producing evidence, then a single **case coordinator** holding the whole picture so therapies reinforce each other instead of conflict. The natural/holistic approach is the family's foundation; the team safeguards it (esp. the detox component) as the child grows.

## Communication pathway (sign language / variation)
- **Evidence supports sign+visuals:** his early SLT records show he responded well to **objects of reference, "finished box", now-and-next board, and actions/signs alongside spoken language** — this is strong support for pursuing a signing system now.
- **Primary manual system = Makaton** (already started — "STARTED MAKATON WITH VIDEO"). It combines **speech + sign + symbols**, which fits autism better than pure BSL. Continue it.
- **Layer with low-tech AAC:** core vocabulary board (top ~50 words), PECS / photo cards, schedule cards; an iPAD AAC app (Proloquo2Go-style) as backup.
- **Who needs training:** the facilitator (Makaton Level 1 + daily signs), family + sibling (carry-over), the SALT directs the whole thing.
- **Milestones:**
  1. **Confirm hearing** (ABR) first — the single gate that decides sign-only vs sign+speech.
  2. Agree a fixed **core vocabulary set**; train facilitator to Level 1.
  3. Build the **visual schedule / choice board** (matches his need for visuals + calendar).
- **Aligns with:** his visual learning style and the existing calendar/consistency approach in `kids/school/SCHOOL_MASTER.md` + `food/KIDS_FOOD_MASTER.md`.
