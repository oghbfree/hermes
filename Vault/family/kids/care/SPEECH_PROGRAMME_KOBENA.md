# SPEECH & LANGUAGE PROGRAMME — KOBENA (bespoke)

> **Designed:** 8 Sept 2026 · For delivery by (facilitator) + family · aligned to Mission Clinic directives (sign+speech together, weekly cadence)
> **Basis:** Kobena's full record — UK SLT (Ainscow 2017: strategies that worked), OT (Young 2018), paediatric reviews, 2026 profile (echolalia, hand-shake+"no" cue, name-writing, reading, Makaton started), observation log (2017–18), mood/food diary.
> **Model:** Total Communication — **speech + sign + visuals + real objects simultaneously**. Every activity targets EXPRESSIVE language through his visual/gestural strengths.
> **Cadence:** Daily 20–30 min structured block + all-day embedded practice. Review data weekly.

---

## LEVEL: where he is now (baseline)
- **Receptive:** understands routines, familiar phrases, context + gesture cues. Limited comprehension of novel spoken instructions without visual support.
- **Expressive:** no conversational speech. Echolalia (repeats heard phrases, incl. "don't do that"). Humming/singing (tuneful, high-pitch). Says "no" + hand-shake refusal cue. Writes name. Recent guided/phrase speech with prompting (per Mission).
- **Communication functions present:** refusal ("no"), leading adult by hand to request, pulling to desired items.
- **Missing functions:** requesting with symbol/sign/word, choice-making verbally, commenting, protesting appropriately, greeting, answering.

## PROGRAMME GOALS (12-week arc)
| Wk | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Requesting:** 5 core signs/words used independently to request (eat, drink, more, open, help) |
| 3–6 | **Choice-making:** verbal approximation or sign for preferred item from 2 choices ("book or bubbles?") |
| 7–10 | **Protesting + greeting:** appropriate "no/stop" without distress behaviour; waves + says/signs "hi/bye" |
| 9–12 | **Commenting:** points + signs/names what he sees ("car!", "ball!") — the bridge to spontaneous speech |

**Success definition per goal:** 8/10 opportunities across 3 consecutive days = mastered, add next.

## THE CORE VOCABULARY (first 12 signs+words)
eat · drink · more · open · help · finished · no/stop · car · ball · bubbles · book · mummy/daddy
- Pair EVERY word: **say it + sign it + show the object/picture**. One spoken word per utterance (single words only for now — "more" not "do you want more?").
- Signs: Makaton core signs; keep consistent across home, school.

## DAILY STRUCTURE (20–30 min + embedded)
**1. Warm-up (3 min):** sing a familiar song with signs (wheels on the bus / nursery rhyme he sings). He leads — join, don't direct.
**2. Request drills (10 min) — the core:**
- **Sabotage/contrived situations:** favourite snack in CLEAR closed container he can't open; bubbles with lid on; toy on high shelf. Wait for **any** communication attempt (reach → sign → sound → word). Model the target: "open?" (pause 5 sec). Reinforce ANY upgrade: reach→point→sign→word.
- **Hand-over-hand → hand-under-hand → independent:** if he grabs your hand to operate, you complete WITH him, then shape toward him doing the sign.
- 10–15 trials/day, interspersed with play (not table-bound).
**3. Choice-making (5 min):** 2 real items at eye level: "car OR ball?" — hold up both, model each word+sign, wait 5 sec. Reinforce choice instantly.
**4. Story/comprehension (5 min)** — see comprehension loop below.
**5. Play closure (2 min):** finished box, tidy-up song — his known routine.

## THE COMPREHENSION LOOP (doctor's directive #3)
Daily with a short, visual story (photo books, 3–4 picture cards, or his YouTube clip content):
1. **Read/Watch** (short!)
2. **Show-me question** (receptive): "Where's the dog?" — he points. 80% accuracy target.
3. **What-happened question** (expressive): "What did the boy do?" — accept sign, single word, or phrase. Model the answer if he can't: "ran!" then pause.
4. **Praise the ANSWER, not the reading** — "Yes! The boy ran!" — the confidence comes from proof of understanding.
5. Data: ✓/✗ per question type in the log.

## SIGN + SPEECH PROTOCOL (how every session runs)
- Every target word: **SAY it (exaggerated mouth movements, slow) + SIGN it (hand-over-hand assist fading to independent) + SHOW it.**
- **Expectant pause:** 5 seconds after modelling. Count 5 in your head. Wait for any attempt.
- **Reinforce upgrades:** if he normally reaches and today he points, treat the point as the win, then model the sign+word.
- Never withhold food/water as therapy leverage. Sabotage = playfully incomplete, not distressing.

## WHAT TO AVOID
- Flashcard drills (2017 log: screaming/sirens — they failed before)
- Long table sessions; demands stacked; removing sensory tools (hands) as punishment (25/9/17: he just switched to body stims)
- Echo-correction ("don't say it like that") — echo back the correct model instead
- Talking over his self-talk. His high-pitch self-talk is processing — wait, then join.
