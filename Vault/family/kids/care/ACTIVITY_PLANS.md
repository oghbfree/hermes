## ✅ SATURDAY (today) — "Build & Cook Day"

- Make **jacket potatoes** for tonight + prep stew base for tomorrow

**🌙 Evening wind-down**
- Bath → Kobe sings nursery rhymes → **Nenyi reads the bedtime story** (low-pressure speech rep) → lights out on time (school-week sleep rhythm matters now)

**Saturday theme:** contribution + skills. Both boys "work" (cook/build) and earn their screens — Dr Umar's privilege model in action.
