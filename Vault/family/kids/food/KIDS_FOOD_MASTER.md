## Diet approach
- Historically **gluten/dairy/sugar-free (GFCF-style)** direction.
- Note: one legacy source argues gluten/dairy are "not the issue" and that **grains/cereals & soy** are the driver; get him off all soy & all grains, no exceptions (Rob Mensah protocol). ⚠️ Treat as one practitioner's historical view — cross-check with current providers.
- Avoid sugar; move away from corn/cereal/snacks; make own spices.
- Bulk cooking ≥2×/week.

### Foods to include
- **Iodine:** kelp, cranberries · **Selenium:** brazil nuts, wild yellowfin tuna, sardines, spinach · **Potassium:** potatoes, avocado, plantain, yoghurt, butternut, kidney beans, cowpea · **Magnesium:** spinach, sesame · **Zinc:** lamb or ribs · **Boron** (supplement)
- Coconut oil added to prepared food; butter; ghee; cocoa butter; animal fat/tallow (saturated fats per legacy protocol)
- Raw/camel milk (Kobena historically had camel milk), yoghurt, cheese, cottage cheese
- 2 peeled apples, red onion, root vegetables, asparagus, peas, bananas, blend vegetables into food
- Watch **vitamin A** intake
