## 4. SOURCED / PROMOTED-ITEM PAYMENT (v2.1 — three clean tiers)

**Worked example (non-H item):** H base price = GH¢100 → John adds **10% = GH¢110 sale price (fixed)** → **GH¢7 to H, GH¢3 to John** on that sale. His 3% comes only when the fixed-price sale closes.

| Item | Who sets price | Sale mechanics |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| **H's own stock** | H | **NO markup for John** — his job is to list/sell it at H's price (piece-rate for listing applies) |
| **Wife's items / any item NOT H's stock, promoted on H's pages** | **H sets base** | **John adds a 10% markup on top of base → split 7% to H / 3% to John** at that fixed price (e.g. H base GH¢100 → selling price GH¢110; GH¢7 to H, GH¢3 to John) |
| **John's OWN items** | John | **He may list up to 5 of his own items free** (no H commission on those); beyond 5 = negotiate |

**Key rules:**
- **H sets ALL base prices** — especially Wife's goods (priced high). John does **not** discount H's stock.
- **Non-H promoted items sell at base + 10%, fixed.** That 10% markup is **split 7% to H / 3% to John** — John keeps the smaller, H keeps the larger share. **Price is fixed** (one number), not negotiable at the counter.
- **H's own stock = no markup for John** (it's H's margin; John is paid via listing piece-rates + base).
- **John's 5 free own-item listings** — his personal items get free exposure on the listings; beyond 5 is negotiated.
- **Logging:** every item logged with H base price + the fixed 10% markup → auto-split 7/3 in the tracker.
- **If Wife's goods are sold via HER OWN pages** (not H's), that's Wife's business, her terms, her payout — OUT of H's framework (clean boundary).
