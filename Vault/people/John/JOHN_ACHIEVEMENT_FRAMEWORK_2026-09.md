**Key rules:**
- **H sets ALL base prices** — especially Wife's goods (priced high). John does **not** discount H's stock.

- **H's own stock = no markup for John** (it's H's margin; John is paid via listing piece-rates + base).
- **John's 5 free own-item listings** — his personal items get free exposure on the listings; beyond 5 is negotiated.
- **Logging:** every item logged with H base price + the fixed 10% markup → auto-split 7/3 in the tracker.
