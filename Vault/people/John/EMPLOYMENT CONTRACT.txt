6. EQUIPMENT AND ASSET ACCOUNTABILITY
6.1 The "John Protocol" - Equipment Management Standard
The Employee is personally responsible for implementation and execution of the Equipment Management Protocol ("The John Protocol") as the primary enforcer of equipment accountability across all Akoma Robotics operations.
6.2 Pre-Course Equipment Check (24 Hours Before)
Mandatory Procedures:
Access all equipment for upcoming course from secure storage
Verify equipment list matches master inventory (serial numbers)
Test all equipment for functionality
mBot units: Power on, movement test, sensor check
Laptops: Power on, software load test, connectivity verification
TVs/projectors: Display test, input verification
Photograph all equipment from multiple angles showing condition
Document any missing items or functional issues
Prepare equipment checklist for facilitator sign-off
Complete pre-course documentation: date, time, facilitator name, Employee signature
Equipment checked includes signed verification both parties acknowledge condition and accountability.
6.3 Post-Course Equipment Check (Within 2 Hours of Course End)
Mandatory Procedures:
Collect all equipment from facilitator with signed return agreement
Count and physically inspect every item
Compare against pre-course checklist
Document and Photograph any damage, loss, or functional issues
Note any discrepancies between expected and returned condition
Prepare incident report if damage/loss identified
Complete post-course documentation: date, time, facilitator name, Employee signature
If Damage/Loss Identified:
Photograph damage clearly with date/time stamp
Document facilitator's explanation
Notify Strategic Lead immediately
Hold facilitator financially liable per facilitator contract terms
Deduct from facilitator commission if agreed
6.4 Monthly Equipment Audit
Monthly Procedure (First Friday of each month):
Full inventory verification of all equipment
Serial number cross-check against master list
Condition assessment of each item
Functionality testing of all operational equipment
Maintenance needs identification and scheduling
Photographic documentation of any items with concerns
Submission of monthly equipment audit report to Strategic Lead
Audit Report Contents:
Date and time of audit
Equipment count and serial numbers
Condition notes for each item
Any missing or damaged items
Recommended maintenance or repairs
Employee signature and date
6.5 Liability and Financial Accountability
Employee Liability:
Employee is financially responsible for equipment loss or damage due to:
Negligence in pre/post-course checks
Inadequate storage or security
Unauthorized use or relocation
Failure to document condition changes
Employee is NOT liable for:
Normal wear and tear from appropriate use
Manufacturer defects
Damage due to facilitator misconduct (facilitator bears liability)
Damage from circumstances beyond reasonable control
Damage/Loss Process:
Report to Strategic Lead within 24 hours
Provide photographic evidence
Provide written explanation
Strategic Lead determines liability
If Employee liable: Amount deducted from bonuses or salary per agreement
If Facilitator liable: Facilitator held accountable per their contract
Financial Protection:
For equipment loss/damage exceeding ₵500, Employer and Employee meet to determine payment plan
Maximum deduction from single paycheck: Not to exceed 25% of that month's earnings
Deductions spread across multiple months if necessary for sustainability
6.6 Master Equipment Inventory
Employee is responsible for maintaining the master equipment inventory documentation including:
Complete list of all equipment with descriptions
Serial numbers for each item
Current condition status
Location of each item
Maintenance history log
Any equipment issues or concerns
Master inventory updated monthly and provided to Strategic Lead.
