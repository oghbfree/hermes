Signature short textured black afro (3-4 inches) held back with a simple black fabric headband. Always wears medium-sized gold hoop earrings. ## Consistency Modifiers

### Lighting
- **Golden hour**: Warm directional sunlight 1 hour before sunset, long soft shadows, 3200K
- **Studio clean**: Even diffused lighting, soft shadowless, neutral 5500K
- **Dramatic**: Single hard light 60-degree angle, deep shadows, 8:1 contrast

### Expressions
- **Confident**: Slight smile, direct eye contact, relaxed eyebrows
- **Focused**: Neutral expression, eyes engaged with task
- **Joyful**: Genuine Duchenne smile, crow's feet visible, bright eyes
- **Professional**: Calm composed, soft smile, attentive eyes

### Wardrobe
- **Casual**: Fitted ribbed terracotta-orange midi dress, black ankle boots
- **Professional**: Tailored neutral blazer, simple gold jewelry
- **Athletic**: Form-fitting activewear in solid colors

### Negative Prompt (always include)
"smooth plastic skin, airbrushed, beauty filter, floating, severed limbs, extra fingers, blurry face, synthetic skin, doll-like, waxy skin"
