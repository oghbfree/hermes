## 1. Role & Scope

1.1 The Facilitator is engaged as an **independent contractor** to provide one-on-one educational and developmental support ("Facilitation Services") for **two children**: Kobena Herbert-Blankson (12, autism spectrum disorder) and Nenyi Herbert-Blankson (10, speech articulation support).

1.2 Services include:
- In-class facilitation and support for Kobena at Kansview Montessori School, Oyarifa (including the pull-back-and-observe protocol directed by his clinician)
- Delivery of the family's written Speech & Language Programmes for both children (attached as Annex A and B)
- Daily written progress logs (submitted via WhatsApp) covering food, mood, behaviour, and speech-programme data
- Story comprehension, storytelling, reading aloud, and structured daily routines
- Collaboration with the Family's occupational therapist, teachers, and clinicians as directed

1.3 The Facilitator is **not** engaged to provide medical or nursing services. All clinical decisions remain with the Family's clinicians (Mission Clinic and/or other providers).

## 2. Working Pattern

2.1 **Term time:** Monday to Friday, during school hours and agreed after-school support times.

2.2 **School holidays:** the Facilitator works **three (3) days per week** (days to be agreed with the Family before each holiday). Holiday facilitation covers both children per the programmes and daily routines.

2.3 Occasional additional hours (weekend activities, appointments accompaniment) by mutual agreement; compensated if outside school/extra classes hours.


## 3. Remuneration

3.1 **Term-time monthly fee: GH¢2,500** (two thousand five hundred Ghana cedis), payable by mobile money or bank transfer on the **14th day of each month**.

3.2 **Holiday-period pay (pro rata):** For any school holiday, payment is **pro-rated** to the actual days worked:
- **Holiday rate = GH¢2,500 ÷ 30 days × days worked.** At 3 days/week, a **full month-long holiday** works out to approximately **GH¢1,250** for that month.
- Shorter holidays: (holiday days worked ÷ 30) × GH¢2,500, paid on the normal 14th of the month following the holiday period.
- Holiday days are scheduled and agreed with the Family before each holiday begins.

3.3 **Annual pay review:** The fee is reviewed **once every 12 months** from the effective date, with any adjustment agreed in writing by both parties. Monthly child-progress reviews (clause 6) inform the annual review but do not themselves change the fee.

3.4 The first month is payable on the 14th of October 2026.

3.5 The Family will provide, at no cost to the Facilitator: training materials and programme documents.

## 4. Duties of the Facilitator

4.1 Follow the Family's written programmes (Speech & Language — Kobena; Speech & Language — Nenyi) and the family's behavioural framework, including the "no corporal punishment, natural/logical consequences" approach.

4.2 Maintain **daily written logs** (WhatsApp) and keep simple data sheets as specified in the programmes.

4.3 Maintain **confidentiality** at all times regarding the children's medical, educational, and family information — during and after this Agreement. No photos, videos, or information about the children may be shared or posted on any platform without written consent from the Family.

4.4 Arrive punctually; notify the Family as early as possible of any absence.

4.5 Never administer medication or supplements, — these remain the Family's responsibility.

4.6 Never use physical discipline, shouting as a strategy, or any intervention that forces compliance.

4.7 Participate in review meetings (weekly 5-min data review, monthly clinician reviews) as reasonably required.

## 5. Probation

5.1 The first **3 months** from the effective date constitute a **probationary period**. The first week is observation-only (no programme changes).

5.2 Either party may end this Agreement during probation with **7 days' written notice** (or immediately for cause under clause 8).

5.3 Successful completion of probation = the Family confirms the role in writing.

## 6. Monthly Child-Progress Reviews

6.1 **Every month**, the Family and the Facilitator will hold a progress review covering **each child separately** (Kobena and Nenyi): daily logs, speech-programme data, progress against the written programmes, and the Facilitator's reliability and collaboration.

6.2 These monthly reviews inform the **annual pay review** (clause 3.2) but do not themselves change the fee.

## 7. Leave & Absence

7.1 The Facilitator is entitled to Ghanaian public holidays that fall on working days.

7.2 **Sick leave:** notify as early as possible. Up to **2 paid sick days per month**; beyond that, unpaid unless the Family agrees otherwise.

7.3 Planned leave (family events, etc.): agree in advance with the Family; unpaid beyond the sick allowance unless otherwise agreed. During any absence, no replacement may be brought by the Facilitator without Family approval.

## 8. Termination

8.1 **Notice:** Either party may terminate with **2 weeks' written notice** (or payment in lieu) after the probationary period. During probation, 7 days' notice applies.

8.2 **Immediate termination for cause** by the Family: breach of confidentiality (clause 4.3); any physical punishment or endangerment of a child; dishonesty; repeated unexplained absence; failure to follow the agreed programmes after warning.

8.3 On termination, the Facilitator returns all programme documents, data sheets, and any Family property, and all confidentiality obligations continue.

## 9. Safety & Conduct

9.1 The Facilitator will follow the children's established routines and safety requirements (including supervision during outdoor play and trampoline use).

9.2 Any injury, incident, or concerning behaviour involving the children must be reported to the Family the same day.

9.3 The Family's home CCTV operates in working areas; the Facilitator acknowledges this.

## 10. Independence

10.1 The Facilitator is an independent contractor, responsible for her own taxes (income tax, SSNIT contributions as applicable to self-employed persons).

10.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship with Kansview Montessori School or any clinic.
