## Job Advert (as published)
**Role:** Facilitator for a Child With Autism — Full-Time
**Overview:** The facilitator provides one-on-one support to a **10-year-old child with autism during school hours**. Primary responsibility: assist the child academically, socially, emotionally and behaviourally to ensure successful participation in classroom and school activities while encouraging independence and confidence.

### Responsibilities
**Classroom / academic support**
- Assist the child in understanding classroom instructions and assignments
- Help the child stay focused and engaged during lessons
- Simplify or repeat instructions when necessary using appropriate communication methods

**Behavioural & emotional support**
- Monitor and manage emotional regulation and sensory needs
- Help the child remain calm and focused during stressful or overstimulating situations
- Encourage healthy interaction with classmates and teachers
- Support the child in developing communication and social skills

**Communication & reporting**
- Maintain regular communication with parents regarding the child's daily progress, challenges, and achievements
- Work collaboratively with teachers, school administrators, therapists, and parents

### Requirements
**Essential skills:** strong patience & emotional intelligence · work calmly under pressure · good communication & interpersonal skills · build trust & rapport with children · strong observation & problem-solving · follow structured routines & behaviour plans · basic classroom support & organisation.

**Minimum qualifications:** SHS certificate or equivalent · experience with children (especially autism/special needs) · clear spoken English · **minimum 1 year experience**.
