### 1. ENQUIRY → LOG → QUOTE (≤5 min)
```
Phone/WhatsApp/Jiji rings
    ↓
OPEN TAB 1 (Enquiry Log) → Fill row A–F
    ↓
CHECK ZOBAZE (Stock + Price)
    ↓
IN STOCK? → Quote Zobaze price → "Ready today"
OUT OF STOCK? → Log Tab 6 → Source 3 quotes in 24h → WhatsApp group
    ↓

```

    ↓

```

    ↓
Tab 5: Delivery Log → Customer Called ✓ / Satisfied ✓
    ↓

```
