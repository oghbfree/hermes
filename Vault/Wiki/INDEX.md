## How this works
- **Ingest:** drop a link/note → WikiBot files it under `raw/`, distils a concept, links it, updates this index.
- **Query:** ask → WikiBot searches `raw/` + `concepts/` and answers with cross-links into `queries/`.
- **Prune:** weekly cron archives `raw/` entries older than ~90 days with no concept links (see `scripts/prune_wiki.py`).
