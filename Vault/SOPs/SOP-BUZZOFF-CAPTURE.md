# SOP — Buzz Off: Bee Capture & Relocation (2 Real Venture)
> Trigger: client reports bees on their property. **We PRESERVE and RELOCATE — never kill.** This is the core brand promise and the first thing the client hears.

## Phase 1 — Remote intake (call/WhatsApp)
Collect before visiting:
1. Location + access (estate? gated? landlord permission needed?)
2. Where the bees are (room, ceiling, wall, tree, roof void) + **how long** they've been there (duration ⇒ colony size + comb volume)
3. Are they **entering the house**? Any stings so far? Children/elderly/pets on site?
4. **Allergies in the household** (anyone stung before / known bee-allergic) — safety-critical
5. Any previous attempts to kill/wash them (chemicals change the job)
Photos + a short video from the client (doorway, entry hole, activity level) — you already have this instinct; make it mandatory.

## Phase 2 — Site survey (the visit)
Document everything:
- **Location of colony** — exact spot, entry/exit hole, height, cavity depth (knock/tap test), comb visible?
- **Photos + video** — wide shot (context), close-up (entry hole), video of activity (30s is enough). These serve survey records AND marketing (with client consent).
- **Access** — ladder needed? ceiling hatch? scaffolding? wall opening? power socket for lights? water source nearby?
- **Colony assessment** — size estimate (bees/hour at entrance), aggression (calm or defensive from 2m), comb age (fresh white vs dark old comb = established colony, bigger job), honey volume, signs of disease/pesticides.
- **Capture plan** — vacuum? trap-out? cut-out? swarm-catch box? queen accessibility (catch the queen = success).
- **Safety plan** — bee suits, smoker, first aid, who vacates the house and for how long.

## Phase 3 — The client conversation (say this explicitly)
- **"We don't kill bees — we remove and relocate them alive."** This is your differentiator; say it first.
- Explain the method chosen (swarm catch / cut-out / trap-out), the timeline, and **what they must do during capture** (stay inside/clear the area, keep windows shut, pets away).
- **The part clients never know: comb cleanup matters more than bee removal.** If comb/honey stays in the wall or ceiling, it melts, attracts ants, cockroaches, and wax moths, and the smell draws a NEW colony back within months. Include removal of comb + sealing of the entry hole in the job — this is your quality mark AND your upsell.
- **Callback reality:** if the queen isn't captured or comb scent remains, scout bees return. Explain your guarantee terms (see pricing).
- **Honesty:** relocation may fail on some jobs (queen lost, colony too deep) — set expectations, don't overpromise.

## Phase 4 — Quote & booking
- Price by job type: swarm catch (cheapest) < accessible comb cut-out < high/structural cut-out < trap-out (multi-visit, most expensive). Height, wall opening/rebuilding, and comb volume move the price — **H approves every quote.**
- Deposit terms, what's included (comb removal + sealing + one follow-up visit?), what's extra (rebuilding, second colony on site, emergency night jobs).

## Phase 5 — Capture day
- Bee suits ALWAYS (even "calm" colonies); smoker lit before first touch; work in shade or early morning/evening when foragers are home.
- Capture queen if at all possible → colony follows.
- Remove ALL comb; bag and seal it; sweep/honey-wipe the cavity to kill scent; **seal the entry hole** (mesh + filler, or advise client's mason).
- Relocate colony to the Senya farm apiary (or approved site) same day; log hive box ID.

## Phase 6 — Follow-up (the part everyone forgets)
- **3-day callback:** client checks for returning bees (scouts). Any return = free re-visit per guarantee.
- **7-day photo:** relocated hive status for the client — proves the preservation story → referrals.
- **Review request + before/after photos** → marketing engine ([[SOP-CONTENT-ENGINE]]).

## The survey form (use every visit)
Colony location / entry hole size & height / access complexity / estimated comb age & volume / aggression / queen seen y-n / allergies on site / rooms to vacate / equipment needed / capture method / comb removal + sealing y-n / quote band / photos-video taken / client consent for marketing y-n.

## Questions we should ask clients (master list)
1. How long have the bees been there? 2. Anyone allergic/stung? 3. Landlord permission? 4. Prior kill attempts (chemicals)? 5. Water source / sweet smells nearby (attracts return)? 6. Do they want the comb removed + hole sealed (recommend YES always)? 7. Do they want the follow-up visit? 8. Consent for photos/video for marketing?

## Escalation
Aggressive colony in an occupied home, suspected chemical-contaminated comb, structural opening needed, or client refuses comb removal → H decides before the job is booked. **Money terms are always H-approved.**
