# SOP — Brand Identity & Collateral Rules
> Applies to ALL content generation, image prompts, and marketing copy.

## Senya Coastal Bloom (honey)
- **Products:** multi-floral honey — 100g (GHS 15–20) · 250g (GHS 35–45) · 500g (GHS 60–75)
- **Premium line voice:** "Fresh from Senya Farm" — raw, unprocessed, no pasteurization, health-conscious. Never make health claims that aren't true.
- Value-add machinery available: chamber sealer, sous-vide, heat press (honey packs on SilverCrest = primary value-add).
- Phase 2 (planned): shito, water.

## Hard collateral rules (ALL brands)
1. **Logos come from `workspace/content-assets/[brand]/` ONLY** — never invent, regenerate, or "improve" a logo.
2. **Taiwah's face is LOCKED** to `MASTER_LOCK.png` — no new generations of her face, ever.
3. **Never describe brand text/wordmarks in image prompts** — text comes from real assets, not AI painting.
4. Use **real Zobaze SKUs** in any product content.

## Voice
- H style: direct, no fluff, precise numbers. Marketing: warm, health-conscious, Ghanaian-practical.
- Multi-brand posts follow [[SOP-CONTENT-ENGINE]] channel/format rules. WhatsApp: no markdown tables, bullets only. Telegram: ≤4000 chars.

## Approvals
Anything published (Jiji, WhatsApp broadcast, social) uses pre-approved templates where they exist ([[SOP-WEEKLY-FLASH-SALE]], [[SOP-CONTENT-ENGINE]]). New campaign types → H approves sample first.
