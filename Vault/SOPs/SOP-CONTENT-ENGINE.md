# SOP — Multi-Brand Content Engine
> Trigger: content requests, scheduled posts, or campaign weeks.

## GOAL OF OUR SOCIAL MEDIA CAMPAIGNS (primary — everything serves this)
1. **2Real: sell tools.** Social exists to drive WhatsApp orders to 0204252252.
   Every week's success = WhatsApp order inquiries generated (tracked via source tally).
   Saves/likes are only proxies; sales is the metric.
2. **Akoma: fill courses.** Drive trial-class bookings and term enrolments (WhatsApp message us).
3. **Taiwah Builds: build the audience engine** (Niche 2) — the long-game monetization
   layer (course, sponsors, affiliates) and 2Real's organic reach multiplier.
4. **Brand authority** — "we build ours": UK-sourced quality + Ghanaian engineering pride.
   Authority content supports 1–3; it never replaces them.
RULE: any post that does not serve goal 1, 2 or 3 needs a reason to exist. Content is
a sales and enrolment machine first, an audience builder second.

## Generation rules
1. Source from `workspace/content-assets/[brand]/` only — logos, product shots, locked faces ([[SOP-BRAND-IDENTITY]]).
2. Real Zobaze SKUs + real prices in every product post. No invented stock.
3. Platform formats: WhatsApp bullets (no tables), Telegram ≤4000 chars, Jiji short titles with real specs.
4. Brand voice per [[SOP-BRAND-IDENTITY]]; per-brand tone variants live in each brand's asset folder README.

## Pipeline
Brief → draft (copy + assets) → H sample approval for new campaign types → schedule → publish → log performance into the content-performance review (weekly).

## Cadence
- Scheduled publications: `Vault/business/Content/` + social-media-operations registry.
- Weekly flash sales: [[SOP-WEEKLY-FLASH-SALE]].
- Weekly performance review: compare engagement vs baseline, feed winners back into templates.
