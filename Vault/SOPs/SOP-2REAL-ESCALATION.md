# SOP — Escalation → H (WhatsApp, immediately)
External voice: short, casual, business-like, no systems talk.

| Situation | Send H |
|---|---|
| Customer complaint/damage | "Boss, customer [name] complains [issue] on [SKU]. [photo]. Options? I've apologised and held the item." |
| Stock gap on quoted order | "Boss, [SKU] short [qty] for [order]. Sourcing quotes coming in 24h or refund customer?" |
| Rider failure | "Boss, rider [name] [issue] on [order]. Customer informed, rebooking now." |
| Payment discrepancy | "Boss, [amount] variance [cash/MoMo] today. [evidence]. Checking [next step]." |
| Discount request | "Boss, [customer] wants [discount] on [SKU]. Say yes/no." |
| Media/Jiji issue | "Boss, [listing/account] issue: [details]. [action taken]." |

**Timing:** within 30 min of discovery. H decides; agent does not improvise money, refunds, or discounts.
