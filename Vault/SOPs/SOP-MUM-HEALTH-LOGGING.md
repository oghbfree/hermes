# SOP — Mum's Daily Health Logging
> Trigger: a MORNING/AFTERNOON/EVENING report from Stephanie (topic 4) arrives.

## Flow
1. **Verify day** — reports carry wrong dates sometimes. Log by content; note mislabels ("labelled 16/9, logged 18/9").
2. **Deduplicate** — verbatim copy of prior day ⇒ mark UNVERIFIED, confirm with H. Never log fiction.
3. **Append** day entry to `family/mum/health/MUM_MEDICAL_MASTER.md` (Status / Vitals / Meds / Meals / Log keys / flags) and meals row to `MUM_FOOD_MASTER.md`.
4. **Update BP trend line** (top of medical master). **Right arm standard** (left reads ~10–17 higher — note arm if different).
5. **Medication rule:** Furosemide 20mg BD after BP check. **HOLD if sys <115–120 or dia <60** (she runs low-normal) or **>140** pending Dr Morris protocol. Log every hold + reason. 20mg after low reading resumes only per next reading.
6. **Gaps are gaps** — "no report" logged honestly, never invented vitals.
7. **Post** one-line kanban comment to card `t_e7b88210`: `BACKFILL (date): ...` — key readings, meds, flags.
8. **Escalate to H** if: BP >150 or <100 repeated, fall, confusion, chest/reflux severe, no bowel >2 days, any injury.
