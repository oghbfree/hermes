# SOP — Jiji Dominance (daily 16:00 block)
> Calendar-fixed, non-negotiable. Jiji is a sales channel, not a noticeboard.

## Daily 16:00 block (Tue–Sat)
1. Refresh/repost top listings (rotation keeps them at the top of category).
2. Reply to ALL chats — enquiries logged per [[SOP-2REAL-ENQUIRY-HANDLING]].
3. Update Jiji Stats tab in tracker (views, chats, replies, conversion).
4. Check competitor pricing on top 10 SKUs → price drift flagged to H.
5. New stock → listed same day with real photos, real Zobaze SKUs, brand assets only ([[SOP-BRAND-IDENTITY]]).

## Rules
- Never fabricate reviews/ratings. Never undercut agreed margin without H approval.
- Replies use the voice rules in [[AGENTS_READ_FIRST]] (as H, direct, no AI-speak).
- Weekly: review which listings convert; dead listings get new photos/title before price cuts.
