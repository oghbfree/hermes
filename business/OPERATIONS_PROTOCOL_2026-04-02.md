## 3. FINANCIAL COMPLIANCE PROCEDURES
### VAT Preparation Checklist
**Deadline:** 25th of each month  
**Tools:** Google Sheets VAT Template

#### WEEK 1: Data Collection
- [ ] Gather all sales invoices (MTN Merchant + cash)
- [ ] Collect all purchase receipts
- [ ] Verify MTN Merchant statement reconciliation

#### WEEK 2: Data Entry
- [ ] Input sales into "VAT_Sales" tab
- [ ] Input purchases into "VAT_Purchases" tab
- [ ] Cross-check totals with bank deposits

#### WEEK 3: Review & Submission
- [ ] Schedule review meeting with boss (22nd-24th)
- [ ] Make corrections as noted
- [ ] Boss submits final return (25th)

#### WEEK 4: Filing & Documentation
- [ ] Print and file submitted return
- [ ] Archive supporting documents
- [ ] Update tax calendar for next month

### Bookkeeping Standards
- **Daily:** Record all sales/cash movements by 6:00 PM
- **Weekly:** Reconcile MTN Merchant every Friday
- **Monthly:** Archive financial records in Google Drive

## 4. EQUIPMENT MANAGEMENT ("THE JOHN PROTOCOL")
### Triple-Check System

#### PRE-SESSION CHECK (At Base)
- [ ] Present all equipment to Operations Manager
- [ ] Verify against Equipment Inventory Checklist
- [ ] Test all electronic components
- [ ] Both parties sign checklist
- [ ] Take photos of equipment condition

#### ON-SITE CHECK (Session Venue)
- [ ] Unpack in presence of students
- [ ] Visual inspection with students
- [ ] Test key components functionality
- [ ] Note any pre-existing damage with students as witnesses

#### POST-SESSION CHECK (Return to Base)
- [ ] Return immediately after session
- [ ] Operations Manager conducts full inspection
- [ ] Clean and charge all equipment
- [ ] Update inventory records
- [ ] Both parties sign completion

### Equipment Fines Schedule
- Missing signature: GHS 50 per check
- Late return: GHS 25 per hour
- Unauthorized use: GHS 200
- Damage due to negligence: Repair cost + GHS 100
