🌞 MUM'S UPLIFT PROGRAMME (for Stephanie)

THE GOAL: one belly-laugh a day, movement twice a day, and things she ENJOYS. She decides what she wants — never force.

😄 DAILY LAUGH (10-20 min)
• Play funny Ghanaian comedy clips on her phone together
• "Joke of the day" — family sends voice notes, play at tea time
• Laugh-together game: fake laugh (ho-ho-ha-ha), whoever breaks into real laughter first wins
• Dance in the chair to highlife/oldies — she taps along, you dance silly
• Research: daily laughter LOWERS blood pressure in 90+ — good for her recent 145s!

💪 EXERCISES WITH YOU (morning + afternoon, ~10 min each)
MORNING (in bed/chair): ankle pumps x10 · ankle circles · knee marches x10 · shoulder rolls x8 · gentle neck turns x5 · hand squeezes x10
AFTERNOON: sit-to-stand x5 (sturdy chair) · heel raises x10 · seated "boxing" punches x10 · march in place x20 · 5 deep breaths
WALK: compound lap 5-10 min morning + afternoon — make it fun ("let's check the flowers")
✔ Log in report: "Exercises done — enjoyed the punching one"

🎨 ACTIVITY MENU (she picks 1-2/day, rotate)
• Granny's assignments — grandkids get tasks, SHE inspects & grades (1x/week)
• Garden time — water flowers, tend her rose
• Cooking direction — she "directs" you while you cook (she trained at Leiths!)
• Autobiography hour — record her voice telling Winneba/1964/London stories (family treasure)
• Cards ("Spare", snap) · name-that-tune with Ghanaian oldies · photo time · hand care IF she wants
• One family call daily · fortnightly outing (she thrives on these)

📅 RHYTHM: morning exercises + chat · late morning garden/call · afternoon activity + walk · laughter moment · evening TV+milk as normal

⚠️ SAFETY (especially after the 11 Sep fall):
• Hold her hand on toilet trips until doctor assesses; night light ON; non-slip mat
• Never force a declined activity — skip, try another day
• Pain = stop the exercise, note it
• Log what made her LAUGH so we do more of it

💛 The best medicine: her grandsons' visits, her flowers, getting out of the house, and being the boss. Keep those flowing.
