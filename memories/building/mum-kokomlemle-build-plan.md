## 5. Build sequence (step by step)
1. Dig foundation **1 metre deep** (1–2 days)
2. Pour concrete + **DPC** (damp barrier) on top (1 day)
3. Lay blocks to window level
4. **Lintel** (beam) + 3 blocks on top
5. Roof (8 sheets if single-room scale; more for full apartment)
6. Plaster inside & out
7. Carpenter: doors, wardrobes, ceiling
8. Electrician: wiring, sockets (kitchen ×3, hall ×6, bed ×4, cooker ×1), switches, exhaust fan, outside socket, antenna ×3, AC point
9. Plumber: toilet, walk-in shower, borehole pump, kitchen sink
10. Solar (Mike): panels + inverter for lights/fans

## Glossary
- **DPC** = damp-proof layer stopping ground water in walls
- **Lintel** = concrete beam over doors/windows
- **Wawa board** = softwood plank for formwork/roof
- **12mm iron rod** = steel bar reinforcing concrete
