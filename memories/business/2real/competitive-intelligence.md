## 2. Competitive Intelligence

### Key Competitors to Monitor
| Competitor | Website/Platform | What to Track |
|-----------|------------------|---------------|
| Supply Master | supplymaster.store | Pricing, product range, promotions |
| Power Tools Mall | powertools-mall.com | Pricing, new arrivals, deals |
| Total Tools Ghana | Various | Range, pricing, positioning |
| Jiji sellers | jiji.gh | Pricing benchmarks, new listings |
| Facebook Marketplace | FB | Local competitor activity |

### What to Track
- **Pricing:** How do competitor prices compare on common items?
- **Range:** What do they stock that we don't? (opportunity gaps)
- **Promotions:** Flash sales, bundle deals, seasonal campaigns
- **Content:** What type of posts get them engagement?
- **Positioning:** Are they competing on price, quality, range, or service?

### Tools
- Web search for competitor names + "Ghana tools"
- Jiji Ghana search for common product categories
- Facebook search for competitor pages
- Price comparison on 5-10 benchmark products

## 3. Market Sentiment & Trends

### Sources to Check
- **Construction/Real Estate:** Ghana construction news, housing starts, building permits
- **Seasonal Factors:** Rainy season (waterproofing), dry season (outdoor), holidays (DIY)
- **Social Trends:** What DIY projects are Ghanaians searching for?
- **Emerging Categories:** Smart home, solar tools, garden equipment, safety gear

### Key Questions
1. Is building activity up or down in Ghana?
2. What season is coming and what products does that demand?
3. Are there new tool technologies or brands gaining traction?
4. What DIY content is trending on Ghanaian social media?

## 4. Content Performance Review

### Metrics to Track
- Facebook post engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- WhatsApp Status views
- Jiji listing views and enquiries
- Direct WhatsApp enquiries mentioning posts
- Which products actually sold from content

### Analysis
- **Top 3 posts:** What made them work? (Product? Image? Caption?)
- **Bottom 3 posts:** What fell flat? Why?
- **Content type winners:** How-to vs showcase vs deal vs Taiwah
- **Time/day analysis:** When does engagement peak?

### Feedback Loop
- John's observations (ask him what customers mention)
- H's insights (what sold well, what got enquiries)
- Numbers (if available from Zobase reports)

## 5. Strategic Output

### Monthly Report Deliverables
1. **Content Calendar:** 4-5 weeks of scheduled content with specific products
2. **Pricing Intel:** Where we're competitive, where we're not
3. **Stock Alerts:** Items to clear, items to restock
4. **Opportunity Spots:** New products to consider, gaps in market
5. **Quick Wins:** Low-effort, high-impact actions for the month

### Iteration Principles
- **Learn from the past:** What worked last month gets repeated/refined
- **Test and measure:** Try new things, track results
- **Stock-driven content:** Real inventory drives real promotions
- **Competitor-aware:** Don't be cheaper â€” be smarter
- **Seasonal alignment:** Right product, right time
