GHANA IMPORT CONSIDERATIONS
===========================

CUSTOMS & DUTIES
- Import Duty: 0-20% (medical devices often 0% if classified correctly)
- VAT: 15% on CIF value
- Processing Fee: 1% of FOB value

SHIPPING ROUTES
1. Direct from China: 30-45 days, cheapest
2. Via UK warehouse: Faster but higher cost
3. Air freight: 7-14 days, expensive

KEY CONTACTS
- Ghana Standards Authority (GSA) - product certification
- FDA Ghana - medical device registration
- GRA Customs - duty calculations
