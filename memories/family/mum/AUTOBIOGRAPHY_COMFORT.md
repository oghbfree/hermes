## Early Life

### Place of Birth & Hometown

I was born at Senya to Nenyi Akondoh the tenth then Traditional Chief of Senya and Madam Efua Mansina.

### Parents & Siblings

- **Father:** Nenyi Akondoh, the tenth Traditional Chief of Senya
- **Mother:** Madam Efua Mansina
- **Grandfather:** Opened a post office; used the funds to take Mum to Winneba

### Childhood Memories

Left Senya at the age of 7 and walked on the seaside to Winneba with Uncle Gyachie and Okine Anderson. Went to Dadson who worked at Ghana Post Office in Winneba. Lived next door to the King's house (Ahenfie).

Stayed with a lady named Rose who worked at the post office as a telephone operator for 7 years. Left Winneba at age 14.

---

## Education

**Methodist Infant School, Winneba** — started at age 7 (Junior school)

**Left Winneba in 1949**

**Methodist Girls' Boarding School, Nyakrom** — Standard 4 to Standard 7, finished 1953

**Ashley Secretarial College, Accra** — 1954, 1 year, didn't complete the course

**Vocational School, Osu** — shorthand typist course

**National Lotteries** — worked as a clerical assistant for 4 years before coming to the UK

**Paris Academy School of Fashion, 299 Oxford Street** — started 1965, studied dress designing and cutting

**Lewisham College** — City & Guilds 706/1 Catering

**Leiths School of Food and Wine** — Diploma in Food and Wine

---

## Coming to the UK

I left Ghana on November 10th, 1964 with British Airways (then called British Overseas Airways — BOAC) with a student flight. Arrived in London on the 11th of November on a cold winter morning.

I immediately started work at the Grosvenor Hotel as a linen room assistant.

Started school in 1965 at Paris Academy School of Fashion, 299 Oxford Street.

Then moved from the Grosvenor Hotel to work at Fashion House Rembrandt, Tottenham Court Road, making parts of dresses, and was there until I got used to making full gowns.

---

## Young Adulthood

### How She Met Robert

A friend of mine — Quaion Sackey (sister) — introduced my ex-husband to me. He came from Liverpool.

We got engaged traditionally and we worked together until we were able to buy a house.

*[Note: Aunt Hannah in Stockwell was also part of the early London network.]*

---

## Marriage & Family

Our parents did the traditional engagement at home in Ghana. Worked and bought a house.

As soon as we bought the house, Almighty God blessed us with a baby boy, now Mr. Blankson. A lot of people call him Herby.

### Children

- H (Oman) Herbert-Blankson ("Herby") — born after Mum and Dad bought their first house
