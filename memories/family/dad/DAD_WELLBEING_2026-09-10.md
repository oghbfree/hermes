**Health Intelligence:**
• Prosthesis / stump: current NHS guidance (multiple UK trusts) stresses daily stump washing with mild soap, careful drying, and checking for redness or skin changes — which can look different on darker skin (a small mirror helps). Stump board keeping the knee straight.
• DVT (NHS Inform, current): hydration and hourly movement remain the key guards — with a dilated vessel plus wheelchair use, dehydration and long sitting raise clot risk. One-leg swelling, pain, redness or warmth = urgent GP/NHS 111.
• Pressure areas (NICE CG179, 2026): a high-specification foam/redistributing cushion is recommended for wheelchair users; reposition regularly.

**Wellbeing Tips:**
• Hydration — regular small sips through the day; good for both circulation and bowels.
• Circulation — seated ankle pumps / foot circles hourly; small pressure-relief lifts in the chair.
• Skincare — daily check of residual limb, foot and pressure points; unscented moisturiser at night.
• Nutrition — protein (~1g/kg/day), calcium + vitamin D for bones, fibre + fluids.
• Mood — keep familiar routines and as much contact with loved ones as possible; peer support eases adjustment.
