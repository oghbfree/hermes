# Dad's 3-Day Wellbeing Check — 2026-06-28

## Health Intelligence

**Prostate Cancer Survivorship (Post-Prostatectomy):**
- NHS confirms regular PSA monitoring remains standard after radical prostatectomy
- New National Cancer Plan (Feb 2026) aims to save 320,000 more lives by 2035 — may expand survivorship support
- Watchful waiting / active surveillance protocols unchanged; post-surgery follow-up focuses on PSA trends, urinary/sexual function

**Below-Knee Amputation (BKA) & Wheelchair Mobility:**
- NHS amputation guidance: compression garment on stump worn daily (off at bedtime), minimum 2 garments rotated
- Phantom pain management: compression, mirror therapy, medication review if needed
- Key: protect remaining limb — extra strain on intact leg; regular podiatry/skin checks essential
- Wheelchair seating: proper cushion, pressure relief every 15–30 minutes to prevent pressure sores

**Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) Prevention:**
- High risk: age 90+, wheelchair-bound, previous vascular issues
- NHS: move/change position hourly; hydration critical (dehydration = risk factor)
- Compression stockings (knee-high) recommended if tolerated
- Warning signs: unilateral calf/thigh pain, swelling, skin colour change, shortness of breath = call 999

**Dilated Blood Vessel / Aneurysm Monitoring:**
- Regular imaging surveillance per vascular team
- Blood pressure control paramount; avoid heavy straining
- Report any new abdominal/back pain urgently

## Wellbeing Tips

- **Nutrition:** High protein (1.2–1.5g/kg/day) for tissue integrity & pressure sore prevention; vitamin C, zinc, hydration (6–8 cups/day unless fluid-restricted)
- **Skin checks:** Daily inspection of stump, sacrum, heels — early redness = reposition immediately
- **Mental wellbeing:** Limb loss + isolation risk; encourage audiobooks, radio, video calls; Limbless Association & Blesma offer peer support
- **Activity:** Seated upper-body exercises (resistance bands) maintain strength; physiotherapy review quarterly
- **Environment:** Ensure wheelchair fits through doors, ramp access, grab rails at key transitions
