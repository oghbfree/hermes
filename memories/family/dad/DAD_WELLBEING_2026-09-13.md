🔔 Dad's 3-Day Check — 13/09/26

**Health Intelligence:**
• Prostate (post-op): 2026 EAU/AUA updates keep follow-up simple — regular PSA + symptom check; for an older survivor with other conditions monitoring can be spaced out but not stopped. Keep flagging bone pain, weight loss or urinary changes.
• Dilated vessel: New 2026 research questions how often very elderly men need aneurysm scans — surveillance can be individualised. NICE still advise keeping blood pressure ≤140/90 and continuing the routine ultrasound schedule.
• Mobility/DVT: Immobile elders are at higher clot risk; DVTs are often silent. Watch the sound leg for sudden swelling, tenderness, warmth or redness.

**Wellbeing Tips:**
• Keep the sound leg moving daily — simple ankle pumps and small lifts in the chair; keep below-knee compression stockings (never rolled at the top) and note them over with socks if needed.
• Pressure care: aim for daily skin checks + pressure-relieving cushion; good protein (1.25–1.5g/kg), energy and fluids help keep skin healthy and speed any healing. If appetite drops, raise it — poor nutrition raises ulcer risk ~4x.
• Stay connected: regular calls, and ask GP about social prescribing — Blesma and the Limbless Association offer peer support befriending for amputees.

**Action Items:**
• Confirm aortic scan date is upcoming and BP is being reviewed at the vascular clinic.
• Check cushion/pressure-relieving seating is current (Wheelchair Services or tissue viability nurse).
• Raise appetite, hydration and low mood with the care team if any concern.
