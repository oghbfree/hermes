# Dad's 3-Day Wellbeing Check — 28 Aug 2026

🔔 Dad's 3-Day Check — 28 Aug 2026

**Health Intelligence:**
• Prostate (post-surgery): 2026 guidance is unchanged — regular PSA checks remain the core of survivorship care; a stable/near-zero PSA is reassuring. Pelvic floor exercises can help continence if it bothers him. 
• Below-knee amputation: keep the stump washed & dried daily and check for soreness; the remaining leg/foot now bears extra load, so it needs gentle daily checking. A stump support can help prevent stiffness and swelling.
• Dilated blood vessel (AAA): current NICE guidance (NG156) centres on regular ultrasound surveillance and well-controlled blood pressure — both lower the small rupture risk. Good hydration also lowers DVT risk linked to his immobility; a one-sided red, swollen or painful leg warrants same-day GP / NHS 111.
• Prevented: if h seated long, a supportive gel/foam wheelchair cushion and pressure-relief position changes help protect his skin.

**Wellbeing Tips:**
• Keep meals balanced with plenty of protein and fluids — supports muscle, wound/skin health and keeps bowels moving.
• Keep a gentle daily routine and stay socially connected — family calls mean a lot.
• Free peer support is available via Limbless Association Volunteer Visitors (0800 644 0185) if he'd like to talk to a fellow amputee.

**Action Items (if any):**
• Confirm with his care team that AAA surveillance and any blood-thinning care is current.
• Ask about a wheelchair pressure-relief cushion / stump support if not already in place.
