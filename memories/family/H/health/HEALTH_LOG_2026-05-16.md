### Pericarditis
- **Type:** Inflammation of the pericardium (fluid-filled sac around the heart)
- **Main symptom:** Sudden sharp stabbing pain behind breastbone
- **Causes:** Viral infection most common; also bacterial, trauma, post-surgical. Often idiopathic.
- **Prevalence:** ~5% of A&E chest pain admissions. More common in men.
- **Prognosis:** Usually full recovery within 7 days. NSAIDs effective.
- **Recurrence rate:** ~30% — usually milder episodes.
- **Complications to watch:**
  - **Cardiac tamponade** — excess fluid compresses heart, prevents proper beating → life-threatening
  - **Constrictive pericarditis** — scarring/hardening of pericardium restricts heart function → life-threatening
- **Status:** Active/recurring — patient reports having this condition
