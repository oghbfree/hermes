Designed FOR Autistic Learners

Our autism-inclusive program is purpose-built from the ground up to honor how autistic children learn best. Using the same world-class mBot robotics kits, we've adapted every element for visual learning, sensory sensitivity, and predictable structure.

Why Robotics for Autistic Children?

Robotics naturally aligns with autistic cognitive strengths: pattern recognition, visual thinking, systematic reasoning, and attention to detail. Programming offers clear cause-and-effect (press button → robot moves), concrete problem-solving, and predictable outcomes—perfect for autistic minds.

What Makes This Different

Small Groups: Maximum 4 students with 2 trained facilitators (1:2 ratio)
Visual Supports: Picture schedules, step-by-step photo instructions, choice boards
Structured Routine: Predictable session format with clear expectations and transitions
Parent Partnership: Parallel parent workshops if needed—learn strategies for home
No Diagnosis Required: If your child thinks differently or learns visually, they're welcome here
