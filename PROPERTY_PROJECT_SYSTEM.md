## 8. LH2 — LOVE HONOUR HOPE CO-OPERATIVE

### Our Mission
**Make Positive Change — Create Opportunities for All.**
Key values: ACTIVE | IMPACT

### Introduction
LH2 is a community & global growing network. A collective of active members from diverse backgrounds — people of all ages, social organisations, companies & individuals.

### Our Story
ENDC Contracts sponsored a community-based organisation called **One Love Community**. Members of the ENDC Team saw firsthand the positive impact and were inspired to form new partnerships with common goals, creating The Love Honour Hope Co-operative.

- Positive change & creating opportunities in our communities
- Network is global, all people are welcome
- "Realizing the great advantage of opportunity, the right attitude and great effort leads to success"
- Handwritten note: **"people & Community"**

### Members
A diverse range of people creating: **Bridges, Insight, Knowledge, Experience, Diversity, Fresh Foresight & Positive Change & Energy.**

- Working with people of all ages is essential
- Social members tackle local community & global issues
- Company members provide: Sponsorship, Fundraising, Aid work, Creating Opportunities, Value Services, Support & Mentoring
