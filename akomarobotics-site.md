### Programs (programs.php)

#### 1. The Robotics Explorer (10-Week After School Short Course)
- **Duration:** 1 hour weekly
- **Price:** ₵1,000 | £100
- **Description:** Perfect introduction to robotics and coding. Build solid foundation and discover passion for technology.
- **What Your Child Will Learn:**
  - Assemble and understand the mBot robot
  - Master block-based coding fundamentals (movement, light, sound)
  - Program ultrasonic and line-follower sensors (obstacle avoidance, line tracking)
  - Collaborate on small final project to showcase skills
- Certificate of Completion at end of course

#### 2. The Young Innovator (Year-Long Programme)
- **Price:** ₵2,000 | £200
- **Description:** Flagship programme for deep, immersive experience. Goes beyond basics to foster advanced problem-solving, creativity, and confidence long-term.
- **Structure:** Holiday Camps (school breaks) — flexible, full-day options for working parents
- **Exclusive Benefits:**
  - Advanced sensor integration and complex coding challenges
  - Inter-class competitions and project showcases
  - Certificate of Completion recognizing mastery
