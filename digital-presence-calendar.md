Sunday is not a rest day for content. It's **distribution day:**

### Tasks:
- [ ] Share best-performing posts from the week into WhatsApp groups

- [ ] Repurpose top Facebook content → WhatsApp Status (reach people who missed it)
- [ ] Post to all relevant WhatsApp Groups:
  - Parent groups (Akoma)
  - Construction/trades groups (2 Real)
  - Community groups (both)
- [ ] Review week's performance — what got the most engagement?

### PDF Ideas:
- **Akoma:** Weekly class schedule, upcoming workshops, student spotlight
- **2 Real:** Deals flyer, new arrivals list, featured products with prices
- **Combined:** "Week Ahead" newsletter with both brands

## Gemini Gems Workflow

### Step 1: OpenClaw Extracts (Sunday/Monday prep)
OpenClaw generates a week's worth of prompts based on:
- Current inventory levels (tools stock high → push 2 Real)
- Partnership discussions (active → push Akoma)
- Calendar events (holidays, school terms)
- Taiwah character anchor prompt

### Step 2: Paste into Gems
- **Gem: "2Real Content Director"** — Pre-loaded with brand voice, product catalog, target audience
- **Gem: "Akoma Content Director"** — Pre-loaded with mission, curriculum highlights, parent pain points

Each Gem outputs:
1. Post caption (with hashtags)
2. Image prompt for Nano Banana
3. Platform-specific formatting

### Step 3: Create in Nano Banana
- Taiwah anchor prompt for influencer content
- Product photography templates for tool showcases
- Naming: `[brand]_[YYYY-MM-DD]_[platform]_[type].png`

### Step 4: Verify → Send to John
- Review for brand accuracy
- Send via WhatsApp for posting
- John posts to scheduled platforms

## Inventory-Based Content Triggers

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Tool stock HIGH | Push 2 Real product posts, Taiwah shots (Thu/Sat) |
| Tool stock LOW | Focus how-to/tips (Thu), reduce product CTAs |
| School partnership ACTIVE | Push Akoma enrollment (Mon/Wed/Fri) |
| School partnership QUIET | General educational content, awareness |
| New shipment arrived | Behind-the-scenes unboxing (Thu or Sat) |
| Sale/clearance needed | Flash sale Saturday, urgency posts |
| Both brands need push | Taiwah crossover content (Sat) |

### 🔴 Golden Rule — 2 Real Enterprises
> **NEVER promote products with fewer than 2 units in stock.** Only suggest items with 2+ units for marketing, advertising, or promotion. Single-unit items risk immediate sellout and customer frustration.
