🎂 Mum turns 92 on Friday 28 August! Let's make it warm and gentle for her — no big fuss, just love.

For the grandchildren joining in:
- Ring / WhatsApp-call her — and one by one call the UK family (Dad etc.) so she can see and talk to everyone
- Ask her a question about her life and record the answer — her stories (Winneba, coming to England in 1964, working in the London hotels) are gold
- Queue her favourite music / TV on her phone and sing along
- Help her tend her flowers or take a slow walk round the compound holding her arm
- Write "one reason we love Granny" each and read them to her

Keep it short and spaced out — a few happy moments through the day beat one long tiring party. Her evening stays as normal (warm milk, quiet wind-down) so she sleeps well.

💛 Happy 92nd, Granny!
