## 9. Red Flags & Escalation

### Immediate Escalation Criteria

- **Vitals:** BP >160/100 or <90/60 | Pulse >100 or <50 | Temp >38°C | O2 sat <92%
- **Foot/limb:** New/worsening foot ulceration, black/discoloured toes, acute stump pain, signs of infection (redness, heat, swelling, discharge)
- **Vascular emergency:** Sudden cold/pale/paraesthetic leg (possible graft occlusion)
- **DVT/PE:** Leg pain/swelling, chest pain, breathlessness — **seek urgent medical advice**
- **Medication:** Missed DAPT doses (aspirin/clopidogrel) — risk of graft thrombosis
- **Fall/injury:** Any fall from wheelchair or during transfer
- **Acute confusion:** New cognitive decline or behavioural change
- **Inability to eat/drink:** Full day refusal
- **Skin breakdown:** New pressure marks, redness over stump or sacrum

### Escalation Path

1. **District nurse** — wound care concerns, routine issues
2. **GP (Dulwich Medical Centre)** — 020 8693 2727 — medication queries, non-urgent concerns
3. **KCH Vascular (Mr Slim's team)** — graft/stump concerns, foot clinic
4. **KCH A&E / 999** — acute vascular compromise, severe infection, DVT/PE symptoms
5. **H notification** — immediate Telegram to main topic for any red flag
