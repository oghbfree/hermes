**Drawing conventions (learn these — they're standard on Ghana drawings):**
- Walls = thin/thick black lines
- Doors/windows = blue lines; door swings = red arcs
- Stairs + key labels = red
- Plumbing fixtures = cyan/light-blue outlines (bath, toilet, sink)
- Landscaping = green dots / tree icons outside walls
- Furniture = thin grey line drawings (beds, sofas, pianos) — shows intended use

## 4. Cross-Lesson: Vertical Zoning (key education point)

| Level | Function | Rooms |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Ground | Public + service + 1 bed + staff + detached gym/pool | living, dining, kitchen, office, bed1, house-boy, garage, gym, pool |
| First | Family sleeping | 4 beds, 3 baths, family lounge, 2 balconies |
| Third | Roof garden / sky lounge | deck, public lounge, kitchenette, bath, green |

**Lesson:** Luxury Accra homes stack by *use*, not just by room count. Noise/damp (gym, pool, garage, kitchen) pushed to ground + detached; sleeping isolated above; entertainment roof reclaims the top. The central stair is the only vertical link → fire/egress planning starts there.

## 5. Accra-Relevant Notes
- **House-boy room + utility** = standard local staff inclusion; plan for it (or consciously omit).
- **2-car garage** + **porch** = gated compound entry logic.
- **Detached gym/pool pavilion** = smart for our climate (equipment humidity, pool chemicals kept off the main slab).
- Title-block typos (2025 vs 2015) = always verify drawing dates/revisions before build — a real-world trap.
- Scale 1:100 = 1cm on paper = 1m on site. Learn to read furniture at 1:100 to sanity-check room sizes.
