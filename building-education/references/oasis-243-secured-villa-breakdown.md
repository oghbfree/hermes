## 3. Perimeter Wall Engineering Detail (THE GOLD NUGGET)

Title: **"4.00 M HIGH PERIMETER WALL – TECHNICAL DRAWING"**
System: **vertical R.C. columns every 2.50m, connected by horizontal "hat-shape" tie beams at every 2.00m height.** Stone cladding over.

### Specs
| Element | Size |
|---------|------|
| Wall height | 4.00 m |
| Column | 25 × 30 cm, every 2.50m c/c |
| Tie beam (hat shape) | 25 × 40 cm, continuous, at every 2.00m |
| Wall thickness | 25 cm |
| Footing beam | 25 × 50 cm |
| Foundation depth | 1.20–1.80 m (soil-dependent) |
| Steel grade | fy = 420 MPa (min) |
| Stone cladding | natural stone, 4–5 cm thick |

### Reinforcement
- **Columns (25×30):** 4 @ 12mm vertical bars + 8 @ 9mm ties
- **Tie beams (hat):** 2 @ 12mm top, 2 @ 12mm bottom, 8 @ 20mm stirrups
- **Wall (with cladding):** 8 @ 20cm horizontal + 8 @ 20cm vertical (each face)

### General notes
- Columns 2.50m c/c; hat tie beams every 2.00m; beams continuous between columns
- All R.C. vibrated + cured
- Stone cladding properly anchored to wall
- **Weep holes at base of wall for drainage** ← important in rainy climate
- All dims in metres; do not scale drawings

### Education takeaways
- The "column + hat tie-beam" frame = a *reinforced concrete skeleton* clad in stone — far stronger & crack-resistant than plain masonry for a 4m wall.
- 2.00m tie-beam spacing = horizontal reinforcement ring every 2m stops the wall bulging/overturning.
- Footing depth 1.20–1.80m = goes below Accra's seasonal shrink-swell clay movement (if that soil type).
- Weep holes = let trapped water out, prevent damp/efflorescence.

## 5. Accra-Relevance Watch-outs

- Through-lot (2 roads) → check both front building lines & gate permits.
- 4m wall → confirm permitted wall height in the district; 4m is tall.
- Flat/2-story + heavy rain → waterproofing + roof fall essential (same as Ref-1 note).
- Underground cellar + plant room → waterproofing + sump pump in high water table.
- Stone cladding 4–5cm → real anchoring detail needed (wind + seismic); don't just stick-on.
- Barb-less security edge = humane alternative to razor wire (good PR + less liability).
