## 4. Roof & Structure

- **Roof:** Flat — perimeter shows high parapet walls, no gables/pitch. Likely reinforced concrete slab (could double as terrace/roof garden, not visible here).
- **Structural system:** Load-bearing masonry (stone/concrete block) with stucco/EIFS finish.
- **Levels:** Split-level — living/dining/kitchen elevated; gym/garage/pool deck lower. Central concrete staircase with stone cladding connects them, with retaining walls.

## 6. Build Feasibility Notes

**Complexity: Moderate–High**

- (+) Single story — no vertical structural complexity.
- (+) Flat concrete roof is straightforward to form/shutter.
- (−) Split-level requires earthworks + retaining walls (cost + drainage detail).
- (−) High perimeter walls add significant masonry volume/materials.
- (−) Flat-roof waterproofing must be done well (common failure point in tropical/wet climates — relevant for Accra).
- (−) Pool + spa + mosaic + underwater lighting is a specialist sub-trade; budget accordingly.
- Stone cladding / stucco finish is labour-intensive but locally buildable in Ghana.

**Accra-relevant watch-outs:**
- Flat roof + heavy rains → robust waterproofing + fall/drainage essential.
- High walls → check setback/building-line bylaws.
- Mosaic pool tiles + imported stone → material cost & lead time.

## 7. As Design Inspiration (takeaways)

- Walled courtyard = privacy + security (good fit for urban Accra plot).
- Central spine path organizing zones is a clean planning move.
- Split-level on a slope could reduce fill/raise costs.
- Pool + spa at low end, living at high end = sensible gravity/drainage logic.
