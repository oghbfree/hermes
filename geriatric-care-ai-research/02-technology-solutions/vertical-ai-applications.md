- Solutions: Bluetooth BP monitors, AI symptom checker, medication reminders
- Cost: -35 per unit wholesale
- Implementation: WhatsApp-based monitoring with Mobile Money payments

3. COMPANIONSHIP (Medium Priority)
- Why: Loneliness is major issue, dementia care emerging need
- Solutions: Voice companions in Twi/Ga, cognitive games, family connection tools
- Cost: -50 per unit (software-based)
