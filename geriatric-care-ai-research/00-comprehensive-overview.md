COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW - ELDER CARE AI IN GHANA

PROBLEM
- Ghana has growing elderly population with limited care infrastructure
- Caregiver shortage is acute
- Families separated by geography need remote monitoring solutions

SOLUTION
- WhatsApp-based vertical AI elder care platform
- Fall prevention, vital monitoring, companionship
- Mobile Money payment integration
- Local language support (Twi, Ga, Ewe)

BUSINESS MODEL
- Monthly subscription: GH500-1,000 per senior
- Hardware cost: ~ one-time per client
- Break-even: 2.5-5 months per client
- Lifetime value: 12-24 months

MARKET OPPORTUNITY
- First-mover advantage in West Africa
- No local vertical AI competitors
- Established mobile money infrastructure
- High WhatsApp penetration

INVESTMENT REQUIRED
- Phase 1 (Months 1-3): 10,000-15,000 USD
- Phase 2 (Months 4-6): 25,000-30,000 USD
- Phase 3 (Months 7-12): 50,000-75,000 USD
- Total to profitability: ~100,000 USD

KEY SUCCESS FACTORS
1. Start with premium clients in Accra
2. Leverage WhatsApp (no new app needed)
3. Partner with churches/community leaders
4. Mobile Money integration essential
5. Local language support for AI

TIMELINE TO PROFITABILITY
- Month 1-3: Pilot and refine (10-20 clients)
- Month 4-6: Scale to 50 clients
- Month 7-12: Scale to 100+ clients, achieve profitability
