MARKET OVERVIEW - ELDER CARE AI IN GHANA

Demographics
- Ghana population over 60: Growing rapidly
- Urbanization: Increasing demand for home care services
- Family structure: Multi-generational households common

Market Gap
- No major vertical AI elder care players in Ghana yet
- International solutions focused on developed markets
- First-mover advantage opportunity in West Africa

Key Drivers
1. Caregiver shortage (global trend: 2.1B seniors by 2050)
2. Aging in place preference (family-based care culture)
3. Mobile money infrastructure enabling remote payments
4. WhatsApp penetration (80%+ of smartphone users)

Target Segments
- Urban affluent families (Accra, Kumasi)
- Diaspora families supporting parents in Ghana
- Retirement communities and assisted living facilities

Competition
- None: No local vertical AI players
- Indirect: Traditional home care agencies (no tech)
- Future: International players may enter
