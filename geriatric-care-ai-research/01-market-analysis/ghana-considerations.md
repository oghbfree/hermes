GHANA-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS

POWER SUPPLY
- All devices need solar/battery backup
- Unreliable grid power in many areas
- Recommend: UPS backup for monitoring hub

CONNECTIVITY
- Internet penetration varies by region
- WhatsApp is dominant platform (80%+ penetration)
- SMS fallback essential for rural areas
- 3G/4G coverage good in urban areas, patchy rural

LANGUAGE SUPPORT
- Primary: English (official)
- Essential: Twi, Ga, Ewe for voice companions
- Documentation must be in local languages

TRUST & ADOPTION
- Partner with churches and community leaders
- Family-based decision making (not individual)
- Demonstrations and testimonials critical
- Start with premium clients, expand to mass market

REGULATORY
- Ghana FDA: Medical device registration required
- Data protection: Comply with Ghana Data Protection Act
- Insurance: Partner with local health insurers

PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE
- MTN Mobile Money: 50%+ penetration
- Vodafone Cash: Alternative
- Bank transfers: For premium clients
- Cash on delivery: For initial pilots
