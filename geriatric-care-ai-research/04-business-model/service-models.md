BUSINESS MODELS FOR GHANA ELDER CARE

OPTION A: HOME CARE SERVICE + TECHNOLOGY (Recommended)
- Provide actual caregivers equipped with monitoring tech
- Monthly subscription: GH500-1,000 per senior
- Margins improve with scale
- Best for: Building long-term business value

OPTION B: TECH-ONLY PLATFORM
- Sell hardware + software subscription
- Partner with existing home care agencies
- Lower startup cost, faster to market
- Best for: Quick validation and revenue

OPTION C: HYBRID MODEL
- Start with tech platform for existing agencies
- Build own care team as revenue grows
- Best of both worlds
- Best for: Phased growth approach

RECOMMENDED: Option A for long-term, Option B for initial validation

PRICING STRATEGY
- Premium segment: GH1,000/month (comprehensive care)
- Mid-tier: GH750/month (monitoring + occasional visits)
- Basic: GH500/month (tech-only monitoring)

MARGIN ANALYSIS
- Hardware cost per client: ~200 USD
- Monthly revenue: 42-83 USD equivalent
- Break-even: 2.5-5 months per client
- Lifetime value: 12-24 months average
