## Logo Design
- Heart shape formed by interlocking gears
- Handshake icon inside the heart (collaboration + teamwork)
- "AKOMA ROBOTICS" in bold uppercase sans-serif below
- Colors: Purple outline (#6A0DAD), gold gear fill (#FFD700)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, uppercase, sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- **Subheadings:** Bold, sans-serif, white or gold
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Style:** Modern, tech-oriented, approachable

## Visual Style
- Circuit board patterns as backgrounds
- Transparent robots showing internal components (educational)
- Friendly robot designs with smiley faces
- Gears as decorative elements
- Clean layouts with clear CTAs
- Target audience: parents of children 8-14, schools

## Programs
1. **Robotics Explorer** — 10-Week After School Course (₵1,000 / £100)
2. **Young Innovator** — Year-Long Programme (₵2,000 / £200)
3. **School Partnerships** — Turnkey robotics program for schools

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Parents of children aged 8-14 (especially mothers making education decisions)
- **Secondary:** School administrators and head teachers (partnership decisions)
- **Tertiary:** Children themselves (they influence parents)
- **Demographics:** Middle-class parents in Accra, education-conscious, mobile-first, WhatsApp users

## Brand Voice
- **Warm and encouraging** — parents want the best for their kids
- **Educational but accessible** — explain STEM concepts simply
- **Aspirational** — show what's possible, inspire ambition
- **Trustworthy** — we're professionals with a real curriculum, not a play centre
- **Inclusive** — boys AND girls, all skill levels, no experience needed
- **Ghanaian-proud** — we're building Ghana's future innovators

## Key Messaging
- Future-ready skills: Coding, problem-solving, teamwork
- Hands-on learning: Not just theory — kids BUILD real things
- Proven curriculum: Structured programme with clear learning outcomes
- Safe environment: Small classes (max 8 students), trained facilitators
- No experience needed: All skill levels welcome
- Girls included: "Who Said Robots Are Just for Boys?"
- WhatsApp enquiry: Easy for parents to ask questions

## Do NOT
- Use fear-based messaging ("Your child will be LEFT BEHIND!")
- Make promises about specific career outcomes
- Post photos of children without permission
- Use overly technical jargon that confuses parents
- Compete with schools — we complement, not replace
- Ignore girls in imagery — always include female students
