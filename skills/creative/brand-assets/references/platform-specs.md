# Platform Format Specifications

## Overview
Format specs, dimensions, and guidelines for every platform Akoma and 2Real publish to.

---

## Instagram

### Feed Post
- **Image:** 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait)
- **Caption:** Hook → value → CTA with emoji
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant (in caption or first comment)
- **Best times:** 9-11am, 7-9pm (Ghana time)

### Reels
- **Video:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds
- **Audio:** Trending audio preferred
- **Caption:** Short, punchy, includes CTA

### Stories
- **Image/Video:** 1080×1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 15 seconds max per slide
- **Stickers:** polls, questions, links when available

---

## Facebook

### Page Post
- **Image:** 1200×630 (landscape) or 1080×1080 (square)
- **Caption:** 2-4 sentences max, include price (2Real), include WhatsApp CTA
- **Emojis:** Sparingly (1-2 per post)
- **Hashtags:** 3-5
- **CTA:** Always end with "📱 WhatsApp [number] to order" (2Real) or "📱 Message us on WhatsApp to learn more" (Akoma)

### Facebook Marketplace
- **Images:** Up to 10, 1080×1080 minimum
- **Title:** Product + condition + brand
- **Description:** Specifications, condition, price, location, delivery options, bulk discounts
- **Price:** Clear GHC amount
- **Category:** Tools & Hardware

---

## TikTok

- **Video:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds
- **Hook:** Visual of tool/robot in action in first 2 seconds
- **Text overlay:** Price and key spec
- **Audio:** Trending construction/tool audio
- **CTA:** "DM for price" / "Link in bio"
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 in caption

---

## WhatsApp

### Status
- **Image/Video:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- **Text:** 1-2 lines max, punchy
- **Price:** Visible if applicable
- **Urgency:** "Only 3 left" / "Weekend special" when relevant
- **Format:** Image-first, text supports visual

### Broadcast
- **Frequency:** Weekly (Sunday distribution)
- **Content:** 3-5 featured products with images
- **Prices:** Clear for each item
- **Framing:** "last chance" or "new stock"
- **CTA:** "Reply to order" or contact link
- **Format:** Text + image per product

---

## LinkedIn

- **Image:** 1200×627 (landscape)
- **Caption:** 3-5 sentences, professional tone
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 at end
- **Frequency:** 1-2x/week per brand

---

## Jiji Ghana (2Real)
- **Images:** Multiple angles, clear product shots
- **Title:** Product name + brand + condition
- **Description:** Full specs, price, location
- **Category:** Tools & Hardware
- **Price:** Negotiable or fixed
