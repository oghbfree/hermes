- **Name:** Taiwah

- **Role:** AI influencer and brand ambassador for 2 Real Enterprises

- **Shape:** Round to oval face with soft, well-defined features

- **Sheen:** Natural dark sheen, well-maintained

- **Style:** Medium to large gold hoop earrings (always worn)

### Build

### Hands & Feet
- **Hands:** Anatomically correct, natural positioning, never floating
- **Feet:** Always fully visible and grounded, single shadow system
- **Gloves:** White work gloves when handling tools (context-dependent)

## 4. Technical Rendering Standards

### MUST Apply (Every Generation)
- Photorealistic rendering — NOT illustration, NOT cartoon
- Ultra-high detail skin texture with visible pores and natural vellus hair (peach fuzz)
- Anatomically accurate facial geometry and body proportions
- Single-source lighting with natural shadow falloff
- Grounded contact shadows where body meets surfaces
- Realistic subsurface scattering on skin
- Natural skin imperfections retained (NO smoothing)
- Cinematically composed frame
- Feet always fully visible and grounded
- Hands anatomically correct — correct finger count, natural grip

### NEVER Allow (Negative Prompt — ALWAYS Include)
```
smooth plastic skin, airbrushed, beauty filter, floating, severed limbs, extra fingers, blurry face, synthetic skin, doll-like, waxy skin, extra limbs, deformed hands, bad anatomy, cartoon, illustration, painting, drawing, anime, 3d render, cgi, oversaturated, neon earrings, blue earrings, silver earrings
```
