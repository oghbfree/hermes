# 2 Real Enterprises — Full Brand Specification

## Identity
- **Name:** 2 Real Enterprises
- **Tagline:** "Real Tools. Real Work. Real Ghana."
- **Niche:** Construction, hardware retail, tools, Ghana business
- **Market:** Ghana (Accra-based), West Africa
- **Website:** https://2-real.zobaze.shop
- **Online store:** www.2real.online
- **What we sell:** Quality new and used tools from the UK for Ghana's construction market
- **Brands stocked:** DeWalt, Bosch, Makita, quality consumables
- **Locations:** Retail shop in Kantamanto, warehouse in Accra
- **Digital presence:** Jiji Ghana, Facebook Marketplace, WhatsApp
- **Supply chain:** Source from UK, ship via Jobyco to Ghana

## Logo Design
- Stylized tree with leaves forming the number "2"
- Leaves transition from yellow (#FFC107) to green (#4CAF50)
- Curved lines beneath resembling waves/fields
- Text: "2REAL" in bold green (#4CAF50), "Enterprise" in smaller yellow/green
- Clean, modern, minimalistic style
- Symbolizes growth, nature, sustainability, reliability

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat or Roboto)
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Style:** Modern, professional, approachable

## Visual Style
- Clean white backgrounds
- Bright, warm color palette (green + yellow dominant)
- Friendly human imagery (smiling people, outdoor settings)
- Blurred natural backgrounds (trees, blossoms)
- Clear CTAs with contrasting colors
- Professional yet approachable

## Target Audience
- Ghanaian construction professionals (ages 25-55)
- Small contractors building homes and offices
- Tradesmen (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, masons)
- DIY homeowners doing renovations
- Hardware shop owners looking for wholesale

## Brand Voice
- **Direct and confident** — we know our tools, we know our market
- **Professional but approachable** — not corporate, not street slang
- **Value-focused** — quality matters, but price matters too
- **Action-oriented** — every post should move people toward a WhatsApp enquiry
- **Trust-building** — we're reliable, our tools are genuine, we deliver

## Content Rotation
| Day | Focus | Content Types |
|-----|-------|---------------|
| **Tuesday** | Product Showcase | Catalogue shots, specs, condition, price, multiple angles |
| **Thursday** | Tips & How-To / BTS | Tool guides, maintenance tips, unboxing, warehouse tours |
| **Saturday** | Deals / Taiwah | Flash sales, weekend specials, Taiwah influencer content |

## Content Balance Mix
- 40% Product showcase
- 30% Tips/educational
- 20% Deals/urgency
- 10% Taiwah/influencer

## Stock-Based Content Rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Stock HIGH | Product showcase posts, clear CTAs, multiple products |
| Stock LOW | Tips & how-to content, educational, reduce product CTAs |
| New shipment | Behind-the-scenes unboxing, "Just Landed" urgency |
| Clearance needed | Flash deals, "Last Chance" messaging, price-first |

🔴 **Golden Rule:** NEVER promote products with <2 units in stock

## Seasonal & Market Awareness
- **Rainy season:** Waterproofing, roofing materials, outdoor tools
- **Building season:** Cement, power tools, safety equipment
- **Holiday periods:** DIY projects, home improvement
- **Competitor position:** We compete on quality and UK sourcing, not just price

## Do NOT
- Use Nigerian slang (we're Ghanaian-focused)
- Make claims we can't back up (warranties, delivery times)
- Post without a clear CTA
- Use more than 5 hashtags
- Create generic "motivational" content — we sell tools, not dreams
- Promote products with <2 units in stock
