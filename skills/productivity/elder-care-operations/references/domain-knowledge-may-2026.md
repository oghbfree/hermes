## NMC VERIFICATION

- **Website:** nmc.gov.gh
- **What to verify:** PIN number, registration status, qualification
- **Who needs it:** Any candidate claiming to be a Registered Nurse
- **Red flag:** "PIN pending" or "waiting for PIN" — NOT yet registered

## CANDIDATE VETTING — BEST PRACTICES

### For RN Candidates:
1. **Ask for NMC PIN** — verify at nmc.gov.gh
2. **Ask for appraisal forms** from previous clinical placements/employers
3. **Ask about immediate clinical action** — not just "call the doctor." A good nurse intervenes first.
4. **Check for "applied themselves"** — the best NCAs are those who went beyond their role during rotations

### For NCA Candidates:
1. **Appraisal forms unlikely** — they won't have formal clinical placement records
2. **Focus on character, warmth, and willingness to learn**
3. **Ask about cooking from scratch** — this is a key differentiator
4. **Check phone etiquette during interview** — if they're on their phone, that's a red flag

### The "Dorcas Factor":
An NCA who has genuinely applied themselves during clinical rotations can be as capable as a junior RN. Look for:
- Specific examples of clinical actions they took (not just "reported to the nurse")
- Understanding of vitals, medications, and emergency response
- Initiative and problem-solving

## TROPICAL CLIMATE PROTOCOLS

### Hydration
- **Target:** 1.5 litres minimum daily
- **Pre-breakfast:** 250ml water before any tea/coffee
- **In 30°C+ heat:** "Forgetting to drink" is the #1 cause of hospital visits for elderly returnees
- **Nurse must actively offer fluids every 1-2 hours**
- **Hydration log:** Record exact fluid intake at each meal/snack

### Equipment Care
- **"Dry Box" Strategy:** Store all medical electronics (BP monitor, glucose strips, oximeter) in airtight container with silica gel packets
- **Humidity damages electronics** and causes glucose strips to give false readings
- **Backup power:** 20,000mAh power bank or small UPS for router/cameras during Dumsor
- **Equipment calibration:** Check BP monitor accuracy monthly

### Pharmacy Vetting
- **Use only:** Top Up Pharmacy or Ernest Chemist (reliable chains, no counterfeit risk)
- **Avoid:** Small unregulated drugstores — counterfeit medications are common

## TRAVEL PROTOCOLS

### Senya Trips
- **Frequency:** Every 2 weeks maximum
- **Depart:** 05:30 AM (Kasoa traffic — leave at 7 AM = 2 hours becomes 4)
- **Compression socks:** Applied BEFORE getting out of bed
- **Mandatory Kasoa stop:** 10-min stretch, ankle rotations, hydration
- **Return by 2:00 PM** — avoid evening rush
- **Post-travel:** Remove compression socks, 15-min Epsom salt foot soak
- **STRICT:** No outings the following day (total rest)
- **STRICT:** Never Senya AND Kokomlemle in the same week

### Kokomlemle Trips
- **Best days:** Tuesday or Wednesday
- **Comfort stays in car or clean zone** — NOT on construction site
- **Dust mask** if site is dusty
- **Nurse stays with Comfort at ALL times**
