1 whole lemon daily (mandatory - handwritten), Bananas 2x/week before 12pm only, Strawberries, Blueberries, Raspberries (encouraged)

### Fish (FRESH ONLY - never tinned, never smoked)

## Meal Structure

Plate: 6-inch saucer, 7 spoons total (Fish | Rice+Potato | Veg+Salad)
Eating: 1 PM and 6 PM only. No food after 7 PM. Fruit before 12 PM.
Detox cycle: Day 1 juice/salad only -> Day 2 rice/potato/veg/beans -> Day 3 fish/rice/potato/veg+salad (repeat ongoing)
Daily schedule: 8AM exercise, 9AM vitamins, 1PM eat, 6PM eat, 7PM exercise, 11PM sleep

## Herbal Supplement Protocol
Herbs in small plastic bag. Start Day 2. 2 tsp in 1/2 cup boiled water, 2 doses/day, 3 days then stop.
