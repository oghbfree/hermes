## Red Flag Criteria

Escalate immediately if any of the following:
- BP > 160/100 or < 90/60
- Pulse > 100 or < 50
- Temperature > 38°C (100.4°F)
- O2 sat < 92%
- Missed diabetes or BP medications
- Inability to get out of bed
- New or worsening pain
- Fall or near-fall
- Refusal to eat/drink for full day
- Acute confusion or behavioural change
- Signs of skin breakdown
