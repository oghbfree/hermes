- 12-bedroom 2-story building, private bathrooms
- Daycare program (full/half day with meals)
- Services: ADL assistance, medication management, exercise/wellness, hairdressing, barbering, manicures/pedicures, therapeutic massage

- Transportation for medical appointments

- Website: https://sencareghana.com/
