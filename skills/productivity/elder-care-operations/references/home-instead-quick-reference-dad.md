## Key Contractual Terms

- Invoices: monthly, raised 1st of month
- Direct Debit: taken ~8th of month
- Other payment: due within 10 days
- Late payment interest: 4% above BoE base rate (min 4%)
- Cooling-off: 14 days after signing
- Service changes: 2 weeks' written notice
- Non-poaching: 6 months post-termination, £1,000 fee
- Insurance: £10M public/products liability, £10M employers liability (Covea)
