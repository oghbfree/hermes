## 5. CLINICAL NOTES FOR THE NURSE

### Swelling (Primary Concern)
- Bilateral leg oedema — daily assessment required
- Elevate legs when sitting
- Epsom salt foot soaks every evening
- Compression socks for travel
- Report any sudden increase to Mr Blankson immediately

### Diabetes
- HbA1c 41 (controlled)
- Fasting glucose target: 4.0–7.0 mmol/L
- No sugar, no honey, no sweetened drinks
- Bananas max 2x/week before 12pm only
- Watch for hypo symptoms: shaking, sweating, confusion

### CKD Stage 3b
- eGFR 41
- Monitor fluid intake (1.5–2L/day unless fluid-restricted)
- No salt (sodium drives fluid retention)
- Report reduced urine output immediately
- Soak dried fish extra if used (sodium)

### Hypertension
- Fluctuating — monitor morning and evening
- Target: 110–140 / 60–90 mmHg
- Zero stress environment
- Report >160/100 immediately

### Mobility
- Housebound
- Assist with walking — clear pathways
- Fall risk: do NOT leave unattended when unsteady
- Morning walk 10–15 min before 10 AM
