### Key Responsibilities

1. **Personal Care:** Assist with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility
2. **Health Monitoring:** Take and record vital signs (BP, pulse, temperature, O₂ saturation, blood glucose) morning and evening
3. **Medication Administration:** Ensure all prescribed medications are taken correctly and on time. **Only the Nurse administers medication** — no one else.
4. **Nutrition:** Plan and prepare 3 meals + snacks daily following Dr Ferguson's protocol (vegan + fresh fish, no nuts/meat/dairy/eggs/wheat/salt, 1 whole lemon daily, garlic protocol, cocoa mid-morning, fruit always available). Prepare meals in advance for your off-day.
5. **Swelling Reduction:** Daily leg/foot massage, Epsom salt foot soaks, compression socks during travel, leg elevation
6. **Hydration Tracking:** Ensure 250ml water before any tea/coffee; log daily fluid intake
7. **Domestic Duties:** Maintain cleanliness of Mum's room, laundry, kitchen
8. **Companionship:** Engage Comfort in conversation, TV, reading, garden time
9. **Reporting:** Post daily health updates to Mr Blankson via WhatsApp (3x daily — morning, afternoon, evening) with photo of signed medication chart
10. **Errands:** Market shopping, pharmacy runs (preferably mornings; can be done by you or a designated person)
11. **Emergency Response:** Follow emergency procedures, contact Dr [Name] and Mr Blankson immediately when needed
12. **Juicing:** Prepare fresh juices from allowed fruits and vegetables
