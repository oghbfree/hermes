### Key Responsibilities

1. **Personal Care:** Assist with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility
2. **Health Monitoring:** Take and record vital signs (BP, pulse, temperature, O₂ saturation, blood glucose) 2x daily
3. **Medication Administration:** Ensure all prescribed medications are taken correctly and on time
4. **Nutrition:** Plan and prepare 3 meals + snacks daily (diabetic-friendly, low-sodium)
5. **Domestic Duties:** Maintain cleanliness of the home, laundry, kitchen
6. **Companionship:** Engage Comfort in conversation, TV, reading, garden time
7. **Reporting:** Post daily health updates to WhatsApp group (3x daily)
8. **Errands:** Market shopping, pharmacy runs (electric bike provided)
9. **Emergency Response:** Follow emergency procedures, contact Dr. Emmanuela and H immediately when needed
