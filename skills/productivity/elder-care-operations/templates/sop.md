Kontomire stew (no salt), garden egg stew, light fish soup (no palm oil), okro stew (light), grilled fresh fish (never fried), jollof rice (no salt, no meat), banku with okro, steamed vegetables, fruits

### AVOID:
Shito, kelewele, fried plantain, processed/canned foods, tinned/smoked fish, white rice, sugar, bread, pasta, Maggi cubes

## 4. SWELLING REDUCTION PROTOCOL

Primary goal: Reduce swelling in Mum's feet and legs.

- **Elevate legs** when sitting — use a footstool or pillow
- **Epsom salt foot soaks** — 15 minutes in warm water, every evening
- **Gentle leg/foot massage** — daily, upward strokes toward the heart
- **Compression socks** during travel and long car rides
- **Ankle rotations** during prolonged sitting or travel
- **Monitor and report** any increase in swelling to Mr Blankson daily
- **Reduce salt to absolute zero** — sodium drives fluid retention

2. Check for consciousness and breathing
3. Call Mr Blankson immediately
4. If unconscious or severe injury → call 112
5. Record the incident in the incident report form

**Very High Blood Sugar (>15 mmol/L):**
1. Check for symptoms: excessive thirst, confusion, fruity breath
2. Call Mr Blankson immediately
3. Do not give extra insulin unless specifically instructed
4. Encourage water intake
5. If Comfort becomes drowsy or unconscious → call 112

**Very Low Blood Sugar (<4 mmol/L):**
1. Check for symptoms: shaking, sweating, confusion
2. Give sugar water or glucose tablet if conscious
3. Call Mr Blankson immediately
4. Recheck glucose after 15 minutes
5. If unconscious → call 112, do not put anything in mouth

**Chest Pain / Difficulty Breathing:**
1. Call 112 immediately
2. Call Mr Blankson
3. Sit Comfort upright, loosen clothing
4. Stay with her until help arrives

**Seizure:**
1. Clear the area around Comfort
2. Do not restrain or put anything in her mouth
3. Call 112
4. Call Mr Blankson
5. Time the seizure
6. Record in incident report

**BP Spike (>160/100):**
1. Keep Comfort calm and seated
2. Recheck after 15 minutes
3. Call Mr Blankson immediately
4. Call Dr. Emmanuela if directed
5. If severe headache, vision changes, or chest pain → call 112
