## 1. PURPOSE OF MONITORING

The Employee acknowledges that Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) cameras are installed in common areas of the residence. The primary purposes for this monitoring are:

- **Patient Safety:** To provide 24/7 oversight of the Client (Comfort Blankson) for medical emergencies or fall detection
- **Security:** To protect the household, residents, and staff from external security threats
- **Quality of Care:** To ensure adherence to the agreed-upon medical and dietary protocols
- **Employee Protection:** To provide evidence of professional conduct by the employee in the event of any incident

---

## 2. SCOPE OF MONITORING

- **Authorised Areas:** Cameras are located in the living room, kitchen, hallways, and outdoor entry points
- **Private Areas:** No cameras are located in the Employee's private bedroom or any bathroom facilities. This is a strict boundary.
- **Coverage:** Cameras cover common areas only where care activities take place

---

## 3. DATA HANDLING & ACCESS

- **Access:** Only Mr Blankson (the Employer) and authorised family members will have access to live feeds or recorded footage
- **Storage:** Footage is stored securely and will be deleted after 30 days unless required for an incident investigation
- **Professionalism:** Footage will never be shared publicly or with third parties, except in the case of a legal or medical investigation
- **Monitoring:** Mr Blankson may review footage at any time, including in real-time via mobile phone
