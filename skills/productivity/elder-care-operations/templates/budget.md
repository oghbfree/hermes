## COST-SAVING NOTES

- **Zero living expenses for nurse** (accommodation + meals covered) = she saves 100% of salary
- **Buy fresh daily** at local market (cheaper than supermarkets)
- **Use local Ghanaian vegetables** (kontomire, alefu, okro, garden eggs) — naturally compliant with Dr Ferguson protocol and affordable
- **Prepare meals in advance** to reduce waste
- **Use power bank + UPS** to protect medical electronics from Dumsor damage
- **Use reputable pharmacies only** (Top Up, Ernest Chemist) — avoid counterfeit meds that waste money
