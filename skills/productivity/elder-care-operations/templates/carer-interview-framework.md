# Carer Interview Framework

## Schedule Template
Use 40-min interviews with 15-min gaps for note comparison.

| Time | Candidate |
|------|-----------|
| HH:MM | Candidate 1 |
| HH:MM+40 | Break (15 min) |
| HH:MM+55 | Candidate 2 |
| HH:MM+95 | Break (15 min) |
| HH:MM+110 | Candidate 3 |

## Red Flags 🚩
- On phone during interview
- Dismissive of "Western standards" or "do things my way"
- Uncomfortable with live-in arrangement
- Can't describe cooking from scratch
- No questions asked (passive)
- Overconfident without substance

## Green Flags ✅
- Asks thoughtful questions about mum's conditions
- Describes cooking with enthusiasm
- Comfortable with training/"your way"
- Warm, patient demeanor
- Practical problem-solver (market, errands, multitasking)
- Genuine care motivation (not just "need a job")

## Post-Interview Decision
1. Compare scores immediately after all 3
2. Check gut feeling vs. score alignment
3. If tied, prefer warmth/character over experience
4. Offer 3-month trial to top candidate
5. Have backup candidate in mind
