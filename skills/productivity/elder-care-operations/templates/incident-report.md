## WHEN TO USE THIS FORM

Complete this form for **any** incident, no matter how minor, including:

- Falls (even if no injury)
- Medication errors or missed doses
- Refusal of medication or food
- Behavioural incidents (aggression, confusion, wandering)
- Injuries (cuts, bruises, burns)
- Illness episodes (fever, vomiting, diarrhoea)
- Near-misses (something that could have caused harm)
- Property damage
- Safety hazards identified
- Any event you are unsure about — **when in doubt, report it**

## INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLETION

1. Complete this form **as soon as possible** after the incident — ideally within 1 hour
2. Be **factual and objective** — describe what you saw and did, not what you think happened
3. Do not blame yourself or others — the purpose is to learn and prevent recurrence
4. Give the completed form to H and keep a copy for your records
5. If the incident is serious, call H **first**, then complete the form
6. If in doubt about whether to report something — **report it**
