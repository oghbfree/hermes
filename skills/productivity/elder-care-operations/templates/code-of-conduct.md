## 1. PURPOSE

This Code of Conduct sets out the behavioural and professional standards expected of you while employed as Lead Carer/Nurse for Mrs. Comfort Blankson. It is designed to protect Comfort, you, and the household. By signing this document, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by these standards.

## 2. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

### 2.1 General Standards
- Conduct yourself with dignity, respect, and professionalism at all times
- Treat Comfort, family members, visitors, and fellow household staff with courtesy and kindness
- Maintain a neat, clean, and professional appearance during working hours
- Wear clean scrubs or appropriate uniform during active shifts
- Keep your living quarters clean and tidy

### 2.2 Punctuality & Attendance
- Arrive on time for every shift — lateness without notice is unacceptable
- If you are unwell or unable to work, notify Mr Blankson as soon as possible, and no later than 2 hours before your shift
- Unexplained absence for more than 2 consecutive days may result in termination
- Your day off must be pre-agreed — do not change it without prior approval

### 2.3 Communication
- Report to Mr Blankson via WhatsApp 3 times daily (morning, afternoon, evening)
- Be honest, clear, and prompt in all communications
- Do not withhold information about Comfort's health, behaviour, or any incidents
- If you are unsure about anything, ask — never guess when it comes to health matters
- **Refer to the employer as "Mr Blankson"** in all communications

---

## 3. CLIENT CARE STANDARDS

### 3.1 Dignity & Respect
- Always address Comfort respectfully — "Madam Comfort" or as she prefers
- Knock before entering her room
- Respect her privacy, especially during personal care (bathing, toileting, dressing)
- Never raise your voice, use harsh language, or show frustration
- Respect her choices and preferences, even when they differ from your clinical recommendation — escalate to Mr Blankson if there is a conflict

### 3.2 Clinical Care
- Follow all medication schedules exactly as prescribed
- Record all vital signs accurately and on time
- Follow Dr Ferguson's dietary protocol strictly (vegan + fresh fish, no nuts/meat/dairy/eggs/wheat/salt, 1 whole lemon daily, garlic protocol, cocoa mid-morning, fruit always available)
- Report any changes in Comfort's condition immediately — do not wait for the next scheduled update
- Never administer medication not prescribed or approved by Comfort's doctor or Mr Blankson
- **ONLY the Nurse administers medication** — Maa Joyce must NOT give any medication under any circumstances

### 3.3 Nutrition & Meals
- Prepare 3 meals + snacks daily as per the approved meal plan
- Use the 6-inch saucer plate, 7 spoons per meal as directed
- Ensure food is fresh, well-cooked, and appropriate for a diabetic, CKD patient
- Do not give Comfort any food or drink that is not on the approved plan
- If Maa Joyce or any household member offers Comfort food or herbal medicine not on the plan, politely decline and report to Mr Blankson immediately
- **Prepare meals in advance** for your off-day; excess stored in fridge or freezer
- **Avoid microwave** — warm food on stove or air fryer
- Use lemon, ginger, or garlic for flavour — NO Maggi cubes, NO salt

### 3.4 Hydration
- Ensure Mum drinks 250ml water BEFORE any tea/coffee
- Log exactly how many litres she drinks daily
- Target: 1.5–2 litres per day

### 3.5 Swelling Reduction
- Daily leg/foot massage (upward strokes toward heart)
- Elevate legs when sitting
- Epsom salt foot soaks in the evening
- Compression socks during travel
- Report any increase in swelling to Mr Blankson

- Do not discuss Comfort's condition with anyone outside the immediate care team (Mr Blankson, doctors, approved family)

## 6. HEALTH & SAFETY

### 6.1 Your Health
- If you are unwell (fever, infection, diarrhoea, etc.), inform Mr Blankson immediately — you may need to temporarily step back from direct care
- Maintain your own vaccinations and health checks
- Practice good hand hygiene — wash hands before and after all care activities

### 6.2 Comfort's Safety
- Never leave Comfort unattended if she is at risk of falling or injury
- Keep the home environment clean and free of hazards
- Report any safety concerns (faulty equipment, electrical issues, etc.) immediately
- In an emergency, call Mr Blankson first, then call 112 (Ghana emergency number) or the nearest hospital

### 6.3 Fire Safety
- Familiarise yourself with fire exits
- Do not block exits or corridors
- Report any fire hazards immediately

### 6.4 CCTV
- Cameras are in common areas only — NOT in your bedroom or bathroom
- Cameras must remain unobstructed 24/7
- **Any blackout or covered lens = immediate formal warning**
- Cameras protect both Mum and you (evidence of professional conduct)

### 6.5 Power Outages (Dumsor)
- Keep a torch/flashlight accessible at all times
- Use the 20,000mAh+ power bank for router/cameras
- Keep fridge closed to preserve medications
- Never leave Comfort in darkness

Breaches of this Code of Conduct will be dealt with as follows:

| Level | Examples | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Minor** | Occasional lateness, minor phone use during shift | Verbal warning |
| **Moderate** | Repeated lateness, failure to follow meal plan, poor communication, failure to send WhatsApp reports | Written warning |
| **Gross Misconduct** | Abuse/neglect, breach of confidentiality, theft, working under the influence, sharing private information on social media, covering/tampering with cameras, giving medication to Maa Joyce to administer | Immediate termination |

During the trial period, any serious breach may result in termination with 1 week notice or immediately for gross misconduct.
