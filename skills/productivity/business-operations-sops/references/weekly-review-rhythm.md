# SOP: Weekly Review Rhythm — All Ventures

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Overview
Standard weekly review cadence to keep all ventures on track. H reviews, decides, adjusts.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Review | Who | Format |
|-----|--------|-----|--------|
| **Monday** | Week plan kickoff | H + agent | Review week's priorities, content plan, tasks |
| **Wednesday** | Mid-week check | H + agent | Progress update, blockers, adjustments |
| **Friday** | Week close | Staff → H | John/Sammy send weekly summaries |
| **Saturday** | Performance review | Agent | Content performance, sales data |
| **Sunday** | Week ahead planning | Agent | Generate next week's content plan |

## H's Weekly Review Checklist

### 2 Real Enterprises
- [ ] Sales figures for the week (total, by channel, by location)
- [ ] Cash reconciliation — all money received?
- [ ] Stock levels — any items running low?
- [ ] Out-of-stock orders — status of each?
- [ ] Content posted — was the week's plan executed?
- [ ] Staff issues — any concerns?

### Akoma Robotics
- [ ] School pipeline — new contacts, demos, closes?
- [ ] Enrollment — new students, payments received?
- [ ] Classes delivered — attendance, issues?
- [ ] Equipment — any maintenance needed?

### Construction
- [ ] Site progress — on schedule?
- [ ] Contractor payments — any due?
- [ ] Materials — any orders needed?
- [ ] Safety — any incidents?

### Farm
- [ ] Crop status — any issues?
- [ ] Upcoming activities — weeding, harvest?
- [ ] Expenses — within budget?

### Property (London)
- [ ] Rent received on time?
- [ ] Any maintenance requests?
- [ ] Any tenant issues?

### Staff
- [ ] All weekly reports received?
- [ ] Any performance concerns?
- [ ] Any hiring needs?

## Monthly Deep Dive (Last Sunday of Month)
- Full P&L review (revenue, costs, margin per venture)
- Staff performance review
- Inventory audit (Zobase vs physical)
- Content performance review
- Next month's priorities and targets

## Pitfalls
- **Don't skip weekly reviews** — small problems compound without regular check-ins
- **Staff reports must be on time** — Friday is the deadline, not "when I get to it"
- **Be honest in reports** — sugarcoating helps no one
- **Act on the data** — if something isn't working, change it
