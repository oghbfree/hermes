# SOP: Sales & Order Processing — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 3.0 (Final) | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Approved by:** H

## Sales Channels & Pricing

| Channel | Location | Handled By | Pricing Source | Who Records Sale |
|---------|----------|------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Jiji | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS price | John / Madam |
| WhatsApp | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS price | John / Madam |
| Facebook | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS price | John / Madam |
| Walk-in (Oyarifa) | Oyarifa shop | John / Madam | On-Zobase: Zobase POS. Off-Zobase: photo to H, H sets price | John / Madam |
| Walk-in (Kantamanto) | Kantamanto | Sammy | On-Zobase only, confirms on Zobase POS | Sammy |

## Roles

- **H** — Enters inventory/stock into Zobase. Reviews staff inputs. Handles discounts. Sets off-Zobase prices.
- **John** — Oyarifa sales, all adverts, out-of-stock sourcing
- **Madam** — Same as John at Oyarifa
- **Sammy** — Kantamanto walk-in (on-Zobase only), enters own sales

## Oyarifa Off-Zobase (Only Exception)

1. Item not on Zobase → John/Madam takes photo → sends to H on WhatsApp
2. H calculates: cost + shipping + margin
3. H sends price back → John/Madam quotes → sale → enters Zobase

## Kantamanto Rules

- Sammy ONLY sells items on Zobase POS
- If not on Zobase → does NOT sell, directs to Jiji/WhatsApp or contacts H

## Out-of-Stock Quotes

1. Request goes to H → H forwards to John
2. John has **24 hours** to source 3 comparables: UK eBay, direct company, competitive third party
3. John posts all 3 in dedicated WhatsApp group (screenshots, links, GHS prices)
4. **H sets final price** → John relays to customer
5. Confirm → order → track → update Zobase on arrival

## Discount Authority

- Staff: **NO** discount authority. Say: "Let me check with the boss"
- H only approves discounts, via WhatsApp

## Payment Rules

- No credit. Payment before goods, always.
- All payments are MoMo or cash. No float.
