## Key Rules Across All Ventures

1. **H approves all spending** — no staff member commits funds without H's sign-off
2. **H approves all pricing and discounts** — staff use Zobase prices, no exceptions
3. **Report incidents immediately** — don't hide problems
4. **Weekly reports due Friday** — all staff send summaries
5. **No credit, no float** — all sales are MoMo or cash, sent to H daily
6. **Document everything** — if it's not written down, it didn't happen
