# SOP: Inventory Management — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Approved by:** H

## UK Stock Logging (H's Process)

1. H logs **title, description, and scans barcode** of everything in the UK into Zobase
2. H puts title and quantities in personal notes
3. When items arrive in Ghana, H goes through with **Madam** and adds the quantities into Zobase

## Oyarifa Stock

- **Madam** keeps record of what she keeps at Oyarifa
- Madam does NOT report stock levels to H (her own records)
- Stock at Oyarifa is managed independently by Madam and John

## Kantamanto Stock

- Sammy manages stock at Kantamanto
- Sammy enters sales into Zobase daily (which reduces stock levels)

## Stock Visibility

- **H** can review stock levels on Zobase at any time
- **John** creates adverts based on what's available on Zobase
- If stock is low or out, John should flag to H before advertising

## Stock Reconciliation

- H reviews Zobase stock levels regularly
- Discrepancies between Zobase and physical stock should be reported immediately
- Any missing stock is investigated by H
