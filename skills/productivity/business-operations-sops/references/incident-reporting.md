# SOP: Incident Reporting — All Ventures

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Scope
Any significant incident across any venture must be reported to H immediately. This covers safety, financial, legal, reputational, and operational incidents.

## What Counts as an Incident

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| **Safety** | Injury on site, accident, near-miss, equipment failure causing harm |
| **Financial** | Theft, fraud, significant cash discrepancy (>200 GHS), property damage |
| **Legal** | Threatened lawsuit, regulatory visit, contract dispute |
| **Reputational** | Customer complaint going viral, negative press, social media crisis |
| **Operational** | Major system failure, supplier breach, key staff resignation |
| **Health** | Staff medical emergency, elder care health crisis |

## Reporting Process

### Immediate (Within 1 Hour)
1. Ensure everyone is safe — safety first
2. Contact H via WhatsApp with:
   - What happened
   - When it happened
   - Who is involved
   - Immediate actions taken
   - What you need from H
3. **Do not** post about the incident on social media
4. **Do not** make statements to media, regulators, or third parties without H's approval

### Within 24 Hours
5. Send H a written incident report:
   - Full details of what happened
   - Timeline of events
   - People involved
   - Evidence (photos, screenshots, documents)
   - Root cause (if known)
   - Actions taken so far
   - Recommended next steps

### Follow-Up
6. H decides on further action
7. Implement H's decisions
8. Log incident in `incident-log.md`
9. Review: what can we do to prevent this happening again?

## Incident Log Format

| ID | Date | Venture | Type | Description | Severity | Status | Resolution |
|----|------|---------|------|-------------|----------|--------|------------|
| INC-001 | 2026-05-16 | 2Real | Financial | Cash shortage GHS 300 | High | Resolved | Sammy error, corrected next day |

## Severity Levels

| Level | Meaning | Response |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Critical** | Immediate danger, major financial loss, legal threat | H notified within 30 mins, all hands |
| **High** | Significant impact, needs H's decision same day | H notified within 1 hour |
| **Medium** | Moderate impact, can wait until next business day | Report within 24 hours |
| **Low** | Minor issue, staff can handle, inform H routinely | Report in weekly check-in |

## Pitfalls
- **Never hide an incident** — H finds out eventually, and it's worse if you hid it
- **Never speculate** in reports — stick to facts
- **Never admit fault** to third parties without H's approval
- **Document everything** — photos, screenshots, timestamps
- **Don't wait** — a small incident becomes a big one if you delay
