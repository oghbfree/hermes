# 2 Real Enterprises — SOP Compiled Reference
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-14

## A. ROLES

- **H** — Enters inventory/stock into Zobase. Reviews staff Zobase inputs. Handles discounts. Steps in to sell when present.
- **John** — Oyarifa sales (all channels), creates ALL adverts (Jiji, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), sources out-of-stock quotes.
- **Madam** — Same as John at Oyarifa. Keeps her own Oyarifa stock records.
- **Sammy** — Kantamanto walk-in sales only. On-Zobase pricing only. Enters his own sales into Zobase.

## B. SALES CHANNELS AND PRICING

| Channel | Location | Handled By | Pricing | Records Sale |
|---------|----------|------------|---------|-------------|
| Jiji | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS | John / Madam |
| WhatsApp | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS | John / Madam |
| Facebook | Online | John / Madam | Zobase POS | John / Madam |
| Walk-in (Oyarifa) | Oyarifa | John / Madam | On-Zobase = Zobase POS. Off-Zobase = photo to H, H sets price | John / Madam |
| Walk-in (Kantamanto) | Kantamanto | Sammy | On-Zobase only, confirm on Zobase POS | Sammy |

## C. KEY RULES

- All payments = MoMo or cash. No credit. No exceptions.
- All prices from Zobase POS. Only exception: Oyarifa off-Zobase (photo to H first).
- Kantamanto = on-Zobase only. If not on Zobase, Sammy does NOT sell.
- No discount authority for staff. "Let me check with the boss."
- H approves all discounts via WhatsApp.
- John creates all adverts. Pricing = Zobase POS.

## D. OUT-OF-STOCK QUOTE PROCESS

1. Customer asks for something not in stock
2. Request goes to H
3. H forwards to John
4. John has 24 hours - sources 3 comparables (UK eBay, direct company, third party)
5. John posts all 3 in the dedicated WhatsApp group
6. H sets final price
7. John relays to customer. Order. Track delivery. Update Zobase on arrival.

## E. CASH RECONCILIATION (Daily, Before 6 PM)

1. Tally sales by channel
2. Count cash on hand
3. Check MoMo transactions
4. Reconcile: Cash + MoMo = Total Sales
5. Send all money to H's MTN MoMo (no float)
6. Report to H with format: [Location] EOD [Date], Sales, Cash, MoMo, Sent, Discrepancy

Discrepancies: Under GHS 50 = note it. GHS 50-200 = report same day. Over GHS 200 = report immediately.
Weekly: Sammy and John send Friday summary.

## F. INVENTORY

- UK: H logs title, description, barcode into Zobase. Adds quantities with Madam when items arrive in Ghana.
- Oyarifa: Madam keeps her own stock records.
- Kantamanto: Sammy manages stock, enters sales into Zobase daily.
- H reviews Zobase stock levels regularly.

## G. CONTENT AND LISTINGS

- John creates ALL adverts - Jiji, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok.
- All product posts include: product name + price overlay.
- Instagram/Facebook: Use Canva or CapCut for professional overlays.
- WhatsApp Status: Photo/video + text overlay. Post daily.
- TikTok/Reels: 10-20 seconds, product in first 3 seconds, large readable price, trending sounds.
- Jiji listing: Title + description + price (from Zobase POS) + clear photos with serial numbers.

## H. GRA TAX FILING

1. GRA Portal / TRIPS. Log in with TIN.
2. Select tax type (VAT / PAYE / Income Tax)
3. Enter details, upload documents. Review. Submit. Generate payment slip.
4. Pay via bank / MoMo.
5. Save confirmation to Google Drive / FINANCE / VAT_Returns/
6. VAT deadline: 25th of each month.
