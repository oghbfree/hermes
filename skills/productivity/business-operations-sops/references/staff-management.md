# SOP: Staff Management — Hiring, Onboarding, Performance & Communication

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Scope
Covers all staff across all ventures: John, Sammy, Matthias, Ben, Kanzoni, Madam, and any future hires.

## Hiring Process

### Step 1: Define the Role
1. Identify need: what work needs doing, who's overloaded
2. Define: title, responsibilities, location, reporting line, salary range
3. H approves the hire before any outreach

### Step 2: Source Candidates
4. Channels: personal network, WhatsApp groups, referrals from current staff, job boards
5. For skilled roles (nurse, site supervisor): verify qualifications (NMC, trade certs)
6. For entry-level: character > qualifications (trainable)

### Step 3: Interview
7. Phone screen first (5-10 mins) — availability, salary expectations, location
8. In-person or video interview (30-45 mins) — use role-specific questions
9. For elder care roles: use the carer interview framework (elder-care-operations skill)
10. Check references for any candidate you're serious about
11. H makes final hiring decision

### Step 4: Offer & Onboarding
12. Send offer: role, salary, start date, reporting line, key expectations
13. First day: introduce to team, show workspace, explain tools (Zobase, WhatsApp groups)
14. First week: shadow experienced staff, practice key tasks
15. First month: check in weekly, correct gaps early

### Performance Review (Quarterly)
1. Review: targets met, quality of work, reliability, initiative
2. Feedback: what's going well, what needs to improve
3. Agree: next quarter's priorities
4. Document: brief notes saved to staff file
5. Salary review: H decides any adjustments

### Performance Issues
1. **First occurrence:** Verbal warning — clear about what's wrong and what's expected
2. **Second occurrence:** Written warning (WhatsApp message counts) — document it
3. **Third occurrence:** H decides — further warning, suspension, or termination
4. **Gross misconduct** (theft, violence, serious breach): Immediate termination, H decides

## Communication Standards

### Staff → H
- Use WhatsApp for day-to-day
- Be concise: what happened, what you need, what you recommend
- Report problems early — don't wait until they're crises
- Format: "[Topic] — [Brief details] — [What I need from you]"

### H → Staff
- Be direct and clear — staff don't need context, they need instructions
- One message, one purpose
- If it's a task: what, by when, what good looks like
- If it's feedback: specific, not general

### Staff ↔ Staff
- Professional, respectful
- No gossip about H, finances, or other staff
- Operational issues: resolve between yourselves, escalate to H only if stuck
- John and Sammy should coordinate on stock/listings (shared Zobase)

### What NOT to Discuss with Staff
- Other staff's salaries
- Business finances (revenue, profit, debt)
- Supplier research or negotiations
- Property plans
- H's personal matters
- Internal systems (cron, agent, workspace)

## Pitfalls
- **Don't hire in haste** — a bad hire costs more than a vacancy
- **Don't skip reference checks** — especially for roles with financial access
- **Be consistent** — same rules for everyone, no favourites
- **Document performance issues** — verbal warnings you can't prove didn't happen
- **Don't discuss one staff member with another** — breeds distrust
- **Pay on time** — late pay destroys trust fast
