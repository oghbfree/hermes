# SOP: Class Delivery — Akoma Robotics

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Overview
Standard operating procedure for delivering Akoma Robotics mBot courses at schools or Akoma venues.

## Course Structure

| Week | Topic | Activity |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Introduction to Robotics | What is a robot? mBot unboxing, first assembly |
| 2 | mBlock Basics | Drag-and-drop programming, LED control |
| 3 | Line Following | IR sensors, basic line-follower program |
| 4 | Obstacle Avoidance | Ultrasonic sensor, if-then logic |
| 5 | Remote Control | Bluetooth + app control |
| 6 | Sound & Music | Buzzer programming, simple melodies |
| 7 | Light & Display | RGB LED, custom displays |
| 8 | Combining Sensors | Multi-sensor project |
| 9 | Student Project | Design and build own robot behavior |
| 10 | Showcase | Demo day — parents invited, certificates awarded |

## Pre-Class Checklist (1 Week Before)

- [ ] Confirm student count with school
- [ ] Verify mBot kit availability (1 kit per 2 students minimum)
- [ ] Charge all mBot batteries
- [ ] Test all kits (sensors, motors, Bluetooth)
- [ ] Prepare printed student workbooks
- [ ] Confirm venue, date, time with school
- [ ] Arrange transport for kits and instructor
- [ ] Prepare attendance sheet
- [ ] Prepare certificates (pre-printed with student names)

## Class Session Flow (2 Hours)

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 0:00 | Welcome + attendance | 5 min |
| 0:05 | Recap last week + intro today's topic | 10 min |
| 0:15 | Instructor demo (projector/large screen if available) | 15 min |
| 0:30 | Hands-on: students build/program | 45 min |
| 1:15 | Break | 10 min |
| 1:25 | Continue hands-on / troubleshoot | 20 min |
| 1:45 | Showcase + wrap-up | 10 min |
| 1:55 | Preview next week + dismiss | 5 min |

## Instructor Guidelines
- Speak clearly, use simple language (kids 7-14)
- Walk around during hands-on — don't sit at the front
- Praise effort, not just results
- If a kit malfunctions, swap immediately — don't waste class time troubleshooting
- Take photos each week (for social media + parent updates)
- End each class with a "wow moment" — something exciting to keep them engaged

## Post-Class
1. Update attendance sheet
2. Note any students who struggled (follow up next week)
3. Report to H: attendance, engagement level, any issues
4. Post 1-2 photos to Akoma social media (with school permission)
5. Charge mBot batteries for next week

## Materials Per Class
- mBot kits (1 per 2 students)
- Laptop with mBlock 5 installed
- Printed workbook for each student
- Extension cables / power strip
- Spare batteries
- Attendance sheet + pen
- Camera/phone for photos

## Pitfalls
- **Always test kits before class** — dead batteries waste 20 minutes
- **Don't go too fast** — better they understand 1 concept than rush through 3
- **Keep spare kits** — at least 1 backup per 10 students
- **Get school permission before posting photos** of children
- **Track attendance** — if students drop off, find out why
