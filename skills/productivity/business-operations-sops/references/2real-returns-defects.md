# SOP: Returns & Defects — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Scope

Covers customer returns, defective items, and warranty claims across all sales channels (Jiji, WhatsApp, Facebook, walk-in).

## Return Policy

- **Defective items:** Accepted within 7 days of purchase with proof of purchase (MoMo receipt, Zobase record, or WhatsApp chat)
- **Change of mind:** Not accepted unless item is unused, in original packaging, and within 48 hours
- **Custom/special order items:** No returns

## Process

### Step 1: Customer Reports Issue
1. Customer contacts via WhatsApp, Jiji message, or in-person
2. Staff member logs: customer name, item, purchase date, issue description, photo/video of defect
3. Staff responds: "Thank you for letting us know. I'll check with the boss and get back to you shortly."

### Step 2: H Approves Resolution
4. Staff forwards details to H via WhatsApp
5. H decides: **replace / repair / refund / decline**
6. H communicates decision to staff

### Step 3: Execute Resolution
- **Replace:** John/Madam/Samy sources replacement from stock. If not in stock, treat as out-of-stock quote.
- **Repair:** If repairable, arrange repair. Cost borne by business if within 7 days.
- **Refund:** H issues refund via MoMo. Staff confirms receipt with customer.
- **Decline:** Staff communicates politely with reason. "Unfortunately this falls outside our return window / doesn't qualify under our policy."

### Step 4: Record
7. Staff updates Zobase: mark item as returned/defective
8. Defective item removed from sellable stock
9. Log in `returns-log.md` (date, customer, item, resolution, cost)

## Defective Stock Handling

- Defective items are **NOT** to be resold
- If supplier warranty applies, John handles claim with supplier
- Defective items stored separately until resolved
- Monthly: H reviews defective stock for supplier claims or disposal

## Pitfalls
- **Never approve a return without H's sign-off.** Staff have no authority.
- **Don't argue with customers.** Log, escalate to H, let H decide.
- **Always get photo evidence** before escalating.
- **Don't re-list defective items** on Jiji/FB/WhatsApp.
