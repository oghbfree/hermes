## Crop Decisions
- **New crops:** H decides after consulting Ben (Ben advises on soil, climate, market)
- **Selling:** H decides when and where to sell (local market, bulk buyer, etc.)
- **Reinvestment:** Profits go back into farm or H decides allocation

## Reporting
Ben reports to H via WhatsApp:
- Weekly: brief update (crops OK / any issues / rainfall)
- Urgent: pest, disease, theft, irrigation failure — immediately
- Before H's visit: summary of progress since last visit

## Emergency Protocols
- **Pest/disease outbreak:** Ben reports immediately → H decides treatment
- **Theft:** Report to local authorities + notify H
- **Irrigation failure:** Ben attempts fix → if not possible, notify H within 24 hours
- **Severe weather damage:** Document with photos → notify H

## Budget & Expenses
- Ben submits expense requests to H via WhatsApp
- H approves before spending
- Keep receipts for all purchases
- Monthly: Ben sends expense summary to H

## Pitfalls
- **Don't start new projects without H's approval** — even small ones
- **Report problems early** — a small pest issue becomes an infestation fast
- **Keep the farm maintained** — overgrown farms attract pests and thieves
- **Don't sell produce without H's say-so** — Ben manages, H decides sales
- **Travel protocol is strict** — no back-to-back outing days after Senya
