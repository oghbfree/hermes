# SOP: School Outreach Pipeline — Akoma Robotics

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Overview
John leads school outreach for Akoma Robotics. Goal: secure partnerships with schools for mBot robotics courses (ages 7-14, 10-week program, 1,000 GHS/student).

## Target Schools

### Priority Tiers
| Tier | School Type | Why | Approach |
|------|------------|-----|----------|
| 1 | Private schools (Accra, Takoradi) | Budget for extras, parent demand for STEM | Direct to headmaster |
| 2 | International schools | Already have tech programs, easy add-on | Partnership pitch |
| 3 | Public schools with ICT labs | High volume, government STEM push | Through district education office |
| 4 | Churches/community groups with youth programs | Weekend/holiday camps | Community outreach |

## Outreach Process

### Step 1: Research & Identify
1. Compile list of target schools: name, location, headmaster name, contact, school size
2. Check if school already has robotics/ICT program
3. Prioritize schools within 30km of Akoma's base
4. Update `akoma-school-pipeline.md` tracker

### Step 2: Initial Contact
5. **Preferred:** In-person visit to school office with brochure
6. **Alternative:** Phone call to headmaster's office
7. **Email:** If school has website with contact info
8. Script: "I'm calling from Akoma Robotics. We run hands-on coding and robotics courses for children aged 7-14 using mBot kits. I'd love to schedule a 15-minute demo with your headmaster to show how this could benefit your students."

### Step 3: Demo Session
9. Schedule demo at school (30-45 minutes)
10. Bring: 2-3 mBot kits, laptop with demo program, printed brochure
11. Demo structure:
    - 5 min: What is robotics + why it matters for kids
    - 15 min: Live mBot demo (line following, obstacle avoidance)
    - 10 min: Course structure, pricing, logistics
    - 10 min: Q&A with headmaster/teachers
12. Leave brochure and contact card

### Step 4: Follow-Up
13. Follow up within 48 hours via WhatsApp/call
14. If interested: send formal proposal (see template)
15. If "not now": note reason, schedule follow-up in 3 months
16. If "no": thank them, ask for referral to other schools

### Step 5: Close
17. Agree on: start date, number of students, venue (school or Akoma), schedule
18. Send invoice for first term
19. Confirm enrollment and collect student names
20. Prepare materials and instructor

## Proposal Template
- Akoma overview (mission, what we do)
- Course details (10 weeks, 2 hours/week, mBot kit included)
- Pricing: 1,000 GHS/student (negotiable for 20+ students)
- Benefits for school: STEM credential, parent satisfaction, competitive edge
- Testimonials (once available)
- Next steps

## John's Weekly Outreach Targets
- Minimum 5 school contacts per week
- Minimum 2 demo sessions per week
- Track all contacts in `akoma-school-pipeline.md`

## Pitfalls
- **Don't pitch to teachers first** — always go to the headmaster/decision-maker
- **Don't leave without a next step** — always schedule follow-up before leaving
- **Don't discount without H's approval** — 1,000 GHS is the standard rate
- **Don't promise what you can't deliver** — confirm kit availability before committing
- **Track everything** — if John doesn't log it, it didn't happen
