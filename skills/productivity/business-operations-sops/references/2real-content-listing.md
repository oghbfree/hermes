# SOP: Content & Listing Procedures — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Approved by:** H

## Who Creates What

- **John** creates ALL adverts — Jiji listings, WhatsApp posts, Facebook posts, Instagram posts, TikTok posts
- **H** provides product info, prices, and photos when needed
- **Pricing on all adverts comes from Zobase POS** (except Oyarifa off-Zobase items)

---

## Instagram Photo/Video Post (with name + price overlay)

1. Open Instagram → tap **+** → choose **Post**
2. Select photo(s) or video
3. Tap **Next**
4. Tap the image/video preview (or **Aa/Text**)
5. Type product name and price
6. Drag text to position — change font/color for visibility
7. Add caption, hashtags, location, tags
8. Tap **Share**

**Better overlays:** Design in **Canva** or **CapCut** before uploading:
- Product name at top
- Price at bottom corner
- Bold white text with shadow/background

---

## Instagram Reels (with name + price overlay)

1. Open Instagram → tap **+** → **Reel**
2. Record or upload video
3. Tap **Next** or **Preview**
4. Tap **Aa** → type product name + price
5. Move and resize text
6. Tap text bubble → use timeline slider to control when text appears/disappears
7. Add music, effects, voiceover
8. Add caption and hashtags → tap **Share**

**Tips:** Keep reels 10–20 seconds. Show product in first 3 seconds. Large readable prices. Add "DM to order" or WhatsApp contact.

---

## WhatsApp Status (with name + price overlay)

1. Open WhatsApp → **Updates/Status** tab
2. Tap camera or **Add Status (+)**
3. Choose photo/video or record one
4. Tap **T (Text)** icon
5. Type product name and price (e.g., `Ladies Handbag – GHS 250`)
6. Drag to position, change font/color
7. Add emojis, stickers, captions if desired
8. Tap **Send/Post**

---

## WhatsApp Broadcast (Photo)

1. Open WhatsApp → tap **three dots** → **New broadcast**
2. Select contacts
3. Tap check mark/create
4. Tap **camera/gallery** icon → select or take photo
5. Add caption if needed
6. Tap **send/publish arrow**

---

## TikTok FYP Post (with name + price overlay)

1. Open TikTok → tap **+**
2. Record or upload video
3. Tap **Next** or **Edit**
4. Tap **Text (Aa)** → type product name + price (e.g., `Sneakers – GHS 300`)
5. Move and resize text
6. Tap text again to set duration/timing
7. Add music, filters, voiceover
8. Write caption with hashtags: `#fyp #tiktokghana #shopghana`
9. Set cover photo → tap **Post**

**FYP tips:** Keep 10–20 seconds. Use trending sounds. Show product immediately. Bright lighting. Post consistently.

---

## TikTok Story (with name + price overlay)

1. Open TikTok → tap **Profile photo +** (or **+** → **Story**)
2. Record or upload photo/video
3. Tap **Text (Aa)** → add product name + price + "DM to order" if needed
4. Position and style text
5. Add stickers/music if desired
6. Tap **Your Story/Post**

---

## Facebook Marketplace Listing

1. Take clear photos in good lighting
2. Open Facebook → **Marketplace** → **Sell/Create Listing**
3. Choose **Item for Sale**
4. Upload photos (best photo first)
5. Add: Title, Price, Category, Description, Location
6. Review → tap **Publish**

---

## Jiji Listing

1. John creates listing with: **title, description, price** (from Zobase POS)
2. Photos: clear, good lighting, serial numbers visible
3. Price must match Zobase POS exactly
