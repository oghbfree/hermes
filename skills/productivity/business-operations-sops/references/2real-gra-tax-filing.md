## 1. Filing Process

1. Visit the **GRA Portal** or **GRA Taxpayer Portal (TRIPS)**
2. Log in with **TIN** (Tax Identification Number) and password
3. Select tax type: **VAT / PAYE / Income Tax**
4. Enter required financial details
5. Upload supporting documents if needed
6. Review all information for accuracy
7. **Submit** tax return online
8. Generate payment slip/reference number
9. Pay through **bank, mobile money**, or approved payment channel
10. Download/print **filing and payment confirmation receipt**
