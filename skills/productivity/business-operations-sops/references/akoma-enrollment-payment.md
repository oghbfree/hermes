## Enrollment Process

### Step 1: School Partnership
1. School agrees to run Akoma course
2. Confirm: student count, schedule, venue, start date
3. School communicates to parents (Akoma provides flyer)

### Step 2: Parent Registration
4. Parents register via:
   - School collection (school gathers names and passes to Akoma)
   - WhatsApp (parent messages John directly)
   - In-person at Akoma/2Real shop
5. Collect: student name, age, parent name, parent phone, any special needs

### Step 3: Payment
6. **Standard:** Full payment before course starts
7. **School bulk:** School collects and pays Akoma in bulk (H approves)
8. **Payment methods:** MTN MoMo to H's number, or cash to John (John sends to H same day)
9. John records payment in `akoma-enrollment-tracker.md`

### Step 4: Confirmation
10. Send confirmation to parent: course dates, times, venue, what to bring
11. Add student to class roster
12. Prepare certificate with student name

## Refund Policy
- **Before course starts:** Full refund minus 100 GHS admin fee
- **After Week 1:** 50% refund
- **After Week 2:** No refund
- **All refunds processed by H only** — John directs parents to H

## Discounts
- **Sibling discount:** 10% off second child (H approval required)
- **Early bird:** 10% if paid 2 weeks before start (H approval required)
- **School bulk:** Negotiated per school — H approves
- **John has NO discount authority** — all discounts go through H

## Pitfalls
- **Never start a class without payment confirmed** — "I'll pay next week" doesn't count
- **John must send all cash to H same day** — no holding cash
- **Track every student** — if they're not in the tracker, they're not enrolled
- **Don't promise discounts** — always "Let me check with the boss"
- **Get school permission** before using student photos or data
