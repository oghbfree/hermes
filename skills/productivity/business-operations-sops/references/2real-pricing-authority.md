**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Standard Pricing

- **All prices come from Zobase POS** — no exceptions except Oyarifa off-Zobase
- **Oyarifa off-Zobase:** Photo to H → H calculates (cost + shipping + margin) → H sets price
- **Jiji/FB/WhatsApp listings:** Must match Zobase POS exactly
- **Price changes:** Only H can change prices in Zobase. Staff request → H updates → staff use new price

## Discount Rules

### Staff Response to Discount Requests
When a customer asks for a discount, staff say:
> "Let me check with the boss and get back to you."

Then forward to H via WhatsApp with:
- Customer name/contact
- Item(s) and quantity
- Zobase price
- Discount requested

### H's Discount Framework

| Scenario | Guideline |
|----------|-----------|
| Bulk purchase (5+ items) | H decides case by case |
| Repeat customer | H decides case by case |
| Item has been listed 60+ days | H may approve 5-15% reduction |
| Damaged packaging (item fine) | H may approve 5-10% |
| Walk-in cash payment | H may approve small discount |
| Below cost | **Never** — hard floor |

### Discount Limits
- Staff: **Zero authority.** No discounts without H's explicit approval.
- H: Full authority. Document all discounts in Zobase notes.

## Promotional Pricing
- Flash sales, clearance, and seasonal promotions: **H only** sets prices and duration
- John creates the promotional content once H provides the price and terms
- Promotional prices must be updated in Zobase before listings go live

## Competitor Price Matching
- If a customer claims a lower price elsewhere, staff log: competitor name, price, link/screenshot
- H reviews and decides whether to match
- Never match without H verifying the competitor price is genuine

## Pitfalls
- **Staff must NEVER say "I'll give you a discount."** Always "Let me check with the boss."
- **Don't change Zobase prices** without H's instruction.
- **Promotional prices must match across all platforms** — Zobase, Jiji, WhatsApp, FB.
- **Keep discount records** — helps H track margin erosion over time.
