# SOP: Construction Site Management

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-16 | **Approved by:** H

## Overview
Covers management of construction sites at New Amanful, Borkro (Takoradi), Kokomlemle, and Senya. Matthias is the site supervisor.

## Site Roles

| Role | Person | Responsibilities |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **H** | Owner | Budget approval, major decisions, contractor selection |
| **Matthias** | Site Supervisor | Daily site ops, contractor management, progress reporting |
| **Contractors** | Various | Physical construction work per scope |

## Site Visit Protocol (Matthias)

### Before Visit
1. Confirm purpose of visit (inspection, measurement, contractor meeting)
2. Bring: site notebook, measuring tape, camera/phone, site plans/documents
3. Wear: closed-toe boots, hard hat if active construction, high-vis vest
4. Notify H of planned visit date/time

### During Visit
5. Walk entire site — note progress, issues, safety concerns
6. Photograph all areas (date-stamped) — before/after comparisons
7. Check contractor work against plans/specs
8. Note materials on site vs. materials needed
9. Talk to contractors — any blockers, delays, disputes?
10. Update site log

### After Visit
11. Send H a site report within 24 hours:
    - Progress since last visit
    - Issues/concerns
    - Photos
    - Contractor status
    - Materials needed
    - Next steps
12. File photos in site folder

## Site Report Format

```
Site: [Location]
```

## Contractor Management

### Hiring
- H selects and approves all contractors
- Matthias can recommend but cannot hire
- Get minimum 2 quotes for any work >5,000 GHS
- Verify: previous work, references, availability

### Payment
- **Never pay 100% upfront** — max 30% deposit
- Balance on completion or milestone payments
- H approves all payments
- Matthias verifies work completed before payment is released
- Keep receipts and payment records

### Quality Control
- Matthias inspects work at each visit
- If work doesn't meet spec: document with photos, notify H, H decides action
- Don't let contractors cut corners — "It's fine" isn't fine

## Safety
- No unauthorised personnel on active construction sites
- Report any accidents or near-misses to H immediately
- Contractors must provide their own PPE
- If site is unsafe, stop work and notify H

## Materials Procurement
- Matthias identifies materials needed → sends list to H
- H approves and sources (UK or Ghana)
- Track delivery: expected date, actual arrival, condition on arrival
- Store materials securely — theft is a real risk on Ghana sites

## Site Security
- Lock up tools and materials at end of each day
- If no one on site: arrange watchman or secure fencing
- Report any theft or vandalism to H immediately
- Photograph and document all incidents

## Pitfalls
- **Never approve contractor payments without verifying work** — photos + inspection
- **Don't let contractors rush** — quality over speed
- **Document everything** — if it's not written down, it didn't happen
- **Report problems early** — small issues become big issues fast
- **Don't make design changes on site** — H approves all changes
