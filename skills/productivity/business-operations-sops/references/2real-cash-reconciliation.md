# SOP: Cash Reconciliation — 2 Real Enterprises

**Version:** 3.0 (Final) | **Date:** 2026-05-14 | **Approved by:** H

## Daily Process (Every Location, Before 6 PM)

1. Tally sales by channel
2. Count physical cash on hand
3. Check MoMo transaction history
4. Reconcile: Cash + MoMo = Total Sales
5. Send ALL money to H's MTN MoMo — no float
6. Report to H:
   [Location] EOD [Date]
   Sales: GHS X,XXX | Cash: GHS X,XXX | MoMo: GHS X,XXX
   Sent to H: GHS X,XXX | Discrepancy: None / GHS XX (reason)

## No Float

All sales are MoMo or cash. Everything sent to H daily.

## Discrepancy Protocol

| Variance | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Under GHS 50 | Note it, adjust next day |
| GHS 50-200 | Report same day |
| Over GHS 200 | Report immediately |
| Repeated shortages | H investigates |

## Weekly Summary (Friday)

Sammy and John send weekly totals: sales by channel, total sent to H, discrepancies, pending out-of-stock orders.
