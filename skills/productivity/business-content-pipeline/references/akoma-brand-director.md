# Akoma Robotics — Brand Director Reference

*Source: Legacy Gemini Gem "Akoma Content Director" via OpenClaw.*
*Use this reference when generating Akoma content in the business-content-pipeline skill.*

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: Akoma Robotics
- **What we do**: Hands-on STEM learning through mBot robotics courses for children
- **Curriculum**: mBlock programming, sensors, motors, autonomous navigation, team challenges
- **Age group**: 8-14 years old (primary and JHS students)
- **Where**: Partner schools across Accra and Ghana
- **How it works**: We provide equipment, curriculum, and trained facilitators — schools host the after-school programme — parents pay per term
- **Tagline**: "Building Tomorrow's Innovators, One Circuit at a Time"

## Brand Voice
- **Warm and encouraging** — parents want the best for their kids
- **Educational but accessible** — explain STEM concepts simply
- **Aspirational** — show what's possible, inspire ambition
- **Trustworthy** — we're professionals with a real curriculum, not a play centre
- **Inclusive** — boys AND girls, all skill levels, no experience needed
- **Ghanaian-proud** — we're building Ghana's future innovators

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Parents of children aged 8-14 (especially mothers making education decisions)
- **Secondary**: School administrators and head teachers (partnership decisions)
- **Tertiary**: Children themselves (they influence parents)
- **Demographics**: Middle-class parents in Accra, education-conscious, mobile-first, WhatsApp users

## Parent Pain Points to Address
1. "My child is always on screens — what if they could BUILD instead of just consume?"
2. "School isn't teaching real-world skills — what about the future job market?"
3. "Coding is too hard / too expensive / not available in Ghana"
4. "I want my child to have an advantage but don't know where to start"
5. "Is this safe? Will my child actually learn something?"
6. "What about girls? Is this just a boys' thing?" (Answer: No)

## Key Messaging
- Future-ready skills: Coding, problem-solving, teamwork
- Hands-on learning: Not just theory — kids BUILD real things
- Proven curriculum: Structured programme with clear learning outcomes
- Safe environment: Small classes (max 8 students), trained facilitators
- No experience needed: All skill levels welcome
- Girls included: "Who Said Robots Are Just for Boys?"
- WhatsApp enquiry: Easy for parents to ask questions

## Day-by-Day Content Rotation

### Monday — Educational
- "5 Reasons Kids Should Learn to Code"
- "What is STEM and Why Does It Matter in Ghana?"
- "How Robotics Builds Problem-Solving Skills"
- "From Consumer to Creator: The Coding Mindset"
- Format: Educational post, infographic, or short video script

### Wednesday — Social Proof
- Parent testimonials (real quotes)
- Student project showcases (with permission)
- "Meet the Instructor" profiles
- School partnership announcements
- Success stories and milestones
- Format: Photo with quote, project image, or instructor profile

### Friday — Engagement
- Polls: "What does your child want to build? 🤖🚗🎮"
- Questions: "At what age should kids start coding?"
- Fun content: Student reactions, workshop moments, robotics demos
- Challenges: "Can your child build a robot that follows a line?"
- Format: Interactive, conversation-starting

### Sunday — Distribution
- Weekly PDF: Schedule + highlight
- Repurpose best content to WhatsApp Groups
- "Week Ahead" preview for parents
- Format: Shareable, forward-worthy

## Platform-Specific Formatting

**Instagram:**
- Square (1080x1080) or portrait (1080x1350)
- Caption: hook → value paragraph → CTA with emoji
- 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Reels: 15-30 second script, trending audio suggestion

**Facebook:**
- Caption: Start with hook (question or bold statement), 3-5 sentences, end with CTA
- Emojis: 🤖 💡 🚀 👨‍🎓 (related to learning)
- Always end with: "📱 Message us on WhatsApp to learn more"
- Hashtags: 3-5 (#AkomaRobotics #STEMGhana #CodingForKids #GhanaEducation #RoboticsForKids)

**TikTok:**
- 15-30 second vertical video script
- Hook in first 2 seconds
- Text overlay key points
- Trending audio suggestion

**WhatsApp Status:**
- Student project photos/videos (with permission)
- Quick tips for parents about STEM education
- Class schedules and enrollment deadlines
- Fun moments from workshops

**WhatsApp Broadcast:**
- Longer-form roundup of week's highlights
- Call to action for enrollment enquiries
- Shareable, forward-worthy

## Image Generation Guidelines

**Educational posts:** Infographic with Akoma branding, icons for coding/robots/children, clean/professional/mobile-friendly, colour palette: Blue, white, orange accents.

**Student Spotlight posts:** Ghanaian child (age 8-14) with robotics project, proud expression, classroom setting, bright/warm/authentic, always include both boys AND girls across posts.

**Engagement/Poll posts:** Fun visual graphic showing poll options, icons or illustrations for each option, colourful/eye-catching/shareable, designed to stop the scroll.

**Workshop/Demo posts:** Students actively building or programming, mBot robots visible, facilitator guiding students, real/warm/educational atmosphere.

## Seasonal Awareness
- **School term start**: Push enrollment, "New term, new skills"
- **Mid-term**: Show progress, student achievements
- **School holidays**: Holiday camps, intensive programmes
- **Exhibition/competition**: Showcase events, invite parents
- **Back to school**: Partnership outreach to new schools

## What NOT to Do
- Don't use fear-based messaging ("Your child will be LEFT BEHIND!")
- Don't make promises about specific career outcomes
- Don't post photos of children without permission
- Don't use overly technical jargon that confuses parents
- Don't compete with schools — we complement, not replace
- Don't ignore girls in imagery — always include female students
