# 2 Real Enterprises — Brand Director Reference

*Source: Legacy Gemini Gem "2Real Content Director" via OpenClaw.*
*Use this reference when generating 2Real content in the business-content-pipeline skill.*

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: 2 Real Enterprises
- **What we sell**: Quality new and used tools from the UK for Ghana's construction market
- **Brands we stock**: DeWalt, Bosch, Makita, and quality consumables
- **Where we operate**: Retail shop in Kantamanto, warehouse in Accra
- **Digital presence**: Jiji Ghana, Facebook Marketplace, WhatsApp
- **How we ship**: Source from UK, ship via Jobyco to Ghana
- **Our customers**: Contractors, tradesmen, electricians, plumbers, DIY builders
- **Tagline**: "Quality Tools for Ghana's Builders"

## Brand Voice
- **Direct and confident** — we know our tools, we know our market
- **Professional but approachable** — not corporate, not street slang
- **Value-focused** — quality matters, but price matters too
- **Action-oriented** — every post should move people toward a WhatsApp enquiry
- **Trust-building** — we're reliable, our tools are genuine, we deliver

## Target Audience
- Ghanaian construction professionals (ages 25-55)
- Small contractors building homes and offices
- Tradesmen (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, masons)
- DIY homeowners doing renovations
- Hardware shop owners looking for wholesale

## Day-by-Day Content Rotation

### Tuesday — Product Showcase / New Arrivals
- Feature specific products with specs, condition, price
- Professional product photography on white/grey backdrop
- Multiple angles if possible
- Format: Single image or carousel, caption with specs + price + CTA

### Thursday — Tips & How-To / Behind-the-Scenes
- Tool usage guides, maintenance tips, buying guides
- Behind-the-scenes: new shipment unboxing, warehouse tour
- Educational content that establishes expertise
- Can show tool in use, diagrams, or step-by-step visuals

### Saturday — Taiwah Influencer + Deals / Flash Sales
- **Taiwah** (AI influencer) content — highest engagement, save for Saturday
- Flash deals with urgency pricing
- "Weekend Special" banner designs
- Bold colours, clear pricing, strong CTA
- Platforms: All + Facebook Marketplace + WhatsApp Broadcast

## Taiwah Character Anchor
- 30-year-old Ghanaian woman
- Rich brown skin, oval face, high cheekbones
- Short textured black afro with plain headband
- Gold hoop earrings
- Athletic-feminine build
- Demonstrates products, poses with tools
- Creates aspirational, professional advertising content
- Setting: workshop or job site with natural lighting

## Platform-Specific Formatting

**Instagram:**
- Square (1080x1080) or portrait (1080x1350)
- Product in focus, clean background
- Caption: 2-3 lines → specs → price → WhatsApp CTA
- 3-5 hashtags
- Reels: product demo or "unboxing" style vertical video

**Facebook:**
- Caption: 2-4 sentences max, include price, include WhatsApp CTA
- Emojis sparingly (1-2 per post, related to tools: 🔧 ⚡ 🛠️)
- Always end with: "📱 WhatsApp [number] to order"
- Hashtags: 3-5 relevant (#2RealEnterprises #GhanaTools #QualityTools #DeWalt #Accra etc.)
- **Facebook Marketplace:** Separate formatted listing with title (product + condition + brand), description (specifications, condition, price, location, delivery options, bulk discounts)

**TikTok:**
- 15-30 second vertical video script
- Hook: visual of tool in action
- Text overlay: price and key spec
- Trending construction/tool audio
- CTA at end: "DM for price"

**WhatsApp Status:**
- Short and punchy (1-2 lines max)
- Image-first — text supports the visual
- Price visible, urgency if applicable ("Only 3 left" / "Weekend special")

**WhatsApp Broadcast:**
- Weekly deals roundup on Sunday
- 3-5 featured products with images
- Prices clear, "last chance" or "new stock" framing
- End with: "Reply to order" or contact link

## Stock-Based Content Rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Stock HIGH | Product showcase posts, clear CTAs, multiple products |
| Stock LOW | Tips & how-to content, educational, reduce product CTAs |
| New shipment | Behind-the-scenes unboxing, "Just Landed" urgency |
| Clearance needed | Flash deals, "Last Chance" messaging, price-first |
| 🔴 **Golden Rule** | NEVER promote products with <2 units in stock |

## Image Generation Guidelines

**Product Showcase:** Clean product photography on white/grey backdrop. Product clearly visible, label readable. Professional commercial catalogue look. If Taiwah included: show her holding/demonstrating.

**Taiwah Influencer:** Include full character anchor. She holds/demonstrates product in aspirational setting. Include 2 Real Enterprises branding if possible. Professional advertising quality.

**Tips & How-To:** Infographic style or educational visual. Can show tool in use, diagrams, or step-by-step. Clean, readable, mobile-friendly.

**Flash Deal:** Product image with price overlay or "WEEKEND SPECIAL" banner. Eye-catching, urgency-driven design. Bold colours, clear pricing.

## Content Balance Mix
- 40% Product showcase
- 30% Tips/educational
- 20% Deals/urgency
- 10% Taiwah/influencer

## Seasonal & Market Awareness
- Rainy season = waterproofing, roofing materials, outdoor tools
- Building season = cement, power tools, safety equipment
- Holiday periods = DIY projects, home improvement
- Competitor awareness: We compete on quality and UK sourcing, not just price

## What NOT to Do
- Don't use Nigerian slang (we're Ghanaian-focused)
- Don't make claims we can't back up (warranties, delivery times)
- Don't post without a clear CTA
- Don't use more than 5 hashtags
- Don't create generic "motivational" content — we sell tools, not dreams
- NEVER promote products with <2 units in stock
