# Brand Guidelines — Akoma Robotics & 2 Real Enterprises

## Akoma Robotics

### Identity
- **Name:** Akoma Robotics
- **Tagline:** "The Heart of Robotics in Accra, Ghana | London, UK"
- **Headline:** "Build. Code. Innovate."
- **Website:** https://akomarobotics.com
- **Email:** accra@akomarobotics.com / london@akomarobotics.com
- **London Office:** 1st Floor, 239 Kensington High Street, London W8 6SN

### Brand Colors (OFFICIAL — from marketing materials)
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary Purple** | #6A0DAD | Logo, headings, banners, CTAs |
| **Accent Gold** | #FFD700 | Gears, accents, highlights |
| **White** | #FFFFFF | Text on dark, robot details |
| **Circuit Blue** | #0000FF | Background patterns |

**CRITICAL:** The live website uses Ghana flag template colors (Red #CE1126, Yellow #FCD116, Green #006B3F, Gold #DAA520) — these are NOT the brand colors. Always use purple #6A0DAD + gold #FFD700 for Akoma content.

### Logo
- Heart shape formed by interlocking gears + handshake icon inside
- "AKOMA ROBOTICS" in bold uppercase sans-serif below
- Purple outline, gold gear fill

### Programs
1. **Robotics Explorer** — 10-Week After School Course (₵1,000 / £100)
2. **Young Innovator** — Year-Long Programme (₵2,000 / £200)
3. **School Partnerships** — Turnkey robotics program for schools

### Content Tone
- Educational but fun, empowering, STEM-focused
- "Build. Code. Compete." / "Global Community, Local Impact"

### Hashtags
#STEMeducation #Robotics #Ghana #EdTech #AkomaRobotics #FutureOfWork
